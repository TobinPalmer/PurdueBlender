# Google Maps reference review

Read-only browser review by the coordinating agent, 2026-10-22. Map pins establish identity/context, not surveyed wall locations or heights. Source publication dates below are the gallery labels and may differ from image capture dates.

- Potter: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Potter+Engineering+Center+Purdue resolved to Potter Engineering Center, 500 Central Drive, 40.4273195,-86.9121142. Alan Cesar January2024 photograph showed rounded brick stair tower, small recessed windows in pale precast panels, entry canopy. A stair photo showed wood handrail and dark balusters. Findings sent to builder/reviewer.
- Mechanical Engineering: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Purdue+Mechanical+Engineering+Building resolved to 40.4282695,-86.9128779. February2023 photo attributed to White Knight/Torun S showed pale arched portal, narrow dark-framed reflective windows, brick and pale stone courses. Exact portal facade side was not resolved by this photo alone. Primary current/historic comparison photograph also viewed in browser: https://engineering.purdue.edu/ME/News/2025/purdues-mechanical-engineering-building-represents-more-than-a-century-of-history/MEhistory2-web.jpg .
- BHEE: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Brown+Family+Hall+Electrical+Engineering+Purdue showed Brown Family Hall pin40.4288777,-86.9117698 and Elmore School pin40.428876,-86.9117702, alongside MSEE results. Elmore gallery included a fountain-facing red-tile building likely MSEE; this ambiguous gallery image was explicitly rejected as direct BHEE facade evidence. Builder instead found a current identified Purdue BHEE exterior photograph.
- Knoy: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Knoy+Hall+Purdue resolved to Knoy,401GrantStreet,40.4277879,-86.9112356. Its Street View action opened405Northwestern Avenue, August2025; that panorama was not accepted as Knoy facade verification. This is an unresolved imagery identity limitation.
- Class of1950: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Class+of+1950+Lecture+Hall+Purdue resolved to648OvalDrive,40.4263482,-86.9150559. Jinchu Liu gallery image labeled August2018 (footer image capture September2016) showed curved wooden auditorium desks, central stepped aisle, pale textured side walls, single front screen, suspended grid ceiling with rectangular light panels. Exterior gallery thumbnail showed round brick columns with pale capitals and tall glazing. Parent requested correction of pale column shafts in builder's first render; builder corrected brick assignment.

Maps imagery was viewed for reference and not used as a texture baked into the model. Public GIS footprints remain the recorded horizontal geometry reference. Interior photo observations establish appearance in visible portions, not complete current layouts.
