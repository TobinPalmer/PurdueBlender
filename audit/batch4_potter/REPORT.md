The reviewer found that Round 7’s 4S04 path search used assumed rectangles, the stair width was formula-based, tread-centroid headroom rays did not test a continuous body volume, and the multi-floor cutaway was too cluttered to verify. A Round 8 exploratory BVH proxy places axis-aligned 0.8 × 0.8 × 2.0 m boxes at sampled tread-face centroids and tests intersection with nearby evaluated model geometry. It reports overlaps with historic plan partition meshes, neighboring rail/landing meshes, and at the upper 4S04 route, the estimated 4S04 enclosure/landing/roof. The recorded counts are not a swept-route proof: boxes are not oriented along the stair flight, landing transition samples may intersect walk surfaces by design, and the registered 2016 wall traces are not independently resolved to current physical wall faces. The result therefore makes the earlier “clear route” and all-flight person-clear claims unverified; it does not justify moving the stair cores without a clearer plan-supported routing interpretation. The stair box test flags likely plan/landing conflicts requiring a plan-aligned review; no continuous 0.8 × 2.0 m route or code compliance claim is made. The basement connection remains unresolved.
