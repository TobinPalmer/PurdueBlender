## Changes to the standalone asset

- Removed the south-face clock assembly and four crenellated corner turrets, which were not supported by the inspected current south photographs. The remaining upper tower mass still needs source-led window and parapet work.
- Replaced the three oversized dark gabled west/east/north wing roof meshes and their ridge/eave/valley trim with three pale, flat membrane masses tracing the existing U-shaped upper-wall footprint. The courtyard void remains open. This is a plan-level interpretation of the undated aerial, not a measured roof plan; segmentation, precise membrane limits, perimeters and junctions remain uncertain. South-end gables remain.
- Adjusted brick, limestone, relief, glazing and slate display colors toward the inspected photographic ranges. These remain uncalibrated visual estimates.
- Preserved `Asset_purdue_memorial_union`, local origin, and the asset file path. No master-scene edit.
