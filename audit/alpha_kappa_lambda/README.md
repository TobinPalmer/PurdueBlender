# Alpha Kappa Lambda evidence notes

Stable asset ID: `massing_alpha_kappa_lambda_88dda6c` (Rossen assignment). The saved blend keeps the manifest path, scene `Building_massing_alpha_kappa_lambda_88dda6c`, collection `Asset_massing_alpha_kappa_lambda_88dda6c`, object `Alpha Kappa Lambda`, identity object transform, and campus X-east/Y-north/Z-up coordinates.

The official Purdue GIS feature FID 784 identifies Alpha Kappa Lambda (SOAKL), 417 Waldron St. An independent Indiana statewide LiDAR-derived footprint, objectid 409920 (2018), was found at the same building and registered against Indiana current orthophotography (2022–2025 service coverage; exact capture date not exposed). The 14-vertex LiDAR polygon is used for the massing roof envelope because it better follows the visible roof in the orthophoto. Its EPSG:2968 native coordinates yield about 598.27 m² area and 149.23 m perimeter after exact US survey-foot conversion. Purdue FID 784 yields about 476.20 m² and 122.65 m. Their polygon IoU is 0.7573.

Both sources’ published `Shape__Area`/`Shape__Length` fields imply nearly identical nonstandard factors (about 0.160514 for area and 0.4008 for length) when divided by native ft²/ft geometry. Correct conversion factors are about 0.0929034 and 0.3048006. This repeated mismatch points to an apparent source-field unit-conversion inconsistency, although the responsible processing step is undocumented. The building geometry is therefore measured from the source rings in native EPSG:2968, not copied from the suspect shape attributes. See `footprint_registration.json`.

The captioned Kappa Chapter House exterior photo in *InsideAKL*, March–April 2022, supports brick/masonry walls, white trim, repeated rectangular windows, multiple dark pitched gables, a tall brick chimney and a modest covered entrance. The model represents those qualitative features. The photo is unscaled and oblique, so roof segmentation, eave/ridge heights, chimney size and exact opening/entrance positions remain estimates. The Purdue `Extrution=12` field has undocumented units; height is not verified. Indiana’s LiDAR outline is a roof-derived footprint, not a surveyed foundation perimeter.

Source URLs, dates, CRS, methods, local snapshots and uncertainties are recorded in `evidence.json`. `build_result.json` records the model bounds and explicit height/roof/facade status. `portability_check.json` records Blender reopen and fresh-file collection-link validation. `alpha_kappa_lambda_preview.png` is the saved preview.
