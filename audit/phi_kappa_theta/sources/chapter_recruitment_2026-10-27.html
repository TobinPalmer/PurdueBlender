Recruitment

Indiana Zeta Recruitment

Joining Phi Kappa Theta means more than joining a fraternity—it’s about having a brotherhood to support your Catholic faith and life. From lifelong brotherhood and friendships to Leadership and professional development opportunities, our chapter will support you in your collegiate journey and beyond. We have an extensive commitment to service and philanthropy, a large and connected alumni network local to Purdue, and a transformative spiritual development program to expand on your faith.

Interested in becoming a brother? Reach out to our recruitment chair under the Leaders tab.
