# Phi Kappa Theta / Purdue facility identity research

**Research date:** 2026-10-27  
**Scope:** Determine whether Purdue's current Phi Kappa Theta (Indiana Zeta) colony can be tied to a current physical facility and a distinct Purdue building GIS feature/roof footprint. Research only; no model or shared index was changed.

## Determination

**No distinct current Phi Kappa Theta facility or footprint can be established from public evidence located in this review. Do not assign a candidate asset or model a building from the current record.**

The evidence verifies that Purdue currently recognizes an active Phi Kappa Theta organization/colony, and that the chapter restarted/rebuilt beginning Spring 2025. Its live Purdue and chapter listings point to recruitment/contact but publish no chapter-house street address. The only Purdue BuildingShapes record whose name/abbreviation matches Phi Kappa Theta is FID 762 at 900 David Ross Rd. That site is a historical Phi Kappa Theta location (Purdue's 2007 report) but is identified by current Zeta Beta Tau sources as the Alpha Alpha chapter house. It is already the ZBT-assigned FID 762 site and must not be duplicated or reassigned on the strength of the stale GIS name.

This is an unresolved location identity, not evidence that Phi Kappa Theta has no facility. A different off-campus house, leased residence, or event location remains possible, but no current address or parcel link was found. The parcel and aerial for 900 David Ross establish the site/shape only; neither establishes Phi Kappa Theta occupancy.

## Verified facts

The description calls it the newest fraternity at Purdue. Its record says `modifiedOn: 2026-04-09` and `statusChangeDateTime: 2025-10-01`. Raw capture: [`boilerlink_2026-10-27.html`](sources/boilerlink_2026-10-27.html); source URL: <https://boilerlink.purdue.edu/organization/phikappatheta>.
2. **Purdue FSCL lists Phi Kappa Theta under IFC and links to the chapter website.** The official page is a list of chapters/websites, not a housing/address roster. Raw capture: [`fscl_ifc_chapters_2026-10-27.html`](sources/fscl_ifc_chapters_2026-10-27.html); source URL: <https://www.purdue.edu/fscl/councils/ifc/chapters.php>. 3. **The chapter itself confirms a recent restart, but not a house.** The current Indiana Zeta site says the colony was founded in 1918 and that founders began rebuilding the Purdue community in Spring 2025; the current Purdue chapter site presents recruitment/contact information but no street address. Sources: <https://purdue.phikaps.org/about> (captured as [`phikapp_about_2026-10-27.html`](sources/phikapp_about_2026-10-27.html)) and <https://www.phik.app/> (captured as [`phikapp_2026-10-27.html`](sources/phikapp_2026-10-27.html)). ## GIS and map method / limitations

- Provider: Purdue University `BuildingShapesZip4`, ArcGIS FeatureServer layer 0 (`BuildingShapes`). Feature query returned Web Mercator service geometry transformed to WGS84 (EPSG:4326) and all fields. The name/abbreviation query above found only FID 762. This is an attribute-based crosswalk check, not a search of all private rental records or every possible address.
- The exact source GIS record was also captured by the preceding ZBT review on 2026-10-26 and copied here as [`purdue_gis_fid762_copied_2026-10-27.json`](sources/purdue_gis_fid762_copied_2026-10-27.json). That previous review used the project approximation `X=(longitude+86.9137)*111412.84*cos(40.4274°)`, `Y=(latitude-40.4274)*111040` to compare the ring with the project's campus coordinate frame. That is a local map approximation, not a survey CRS or wall survey. Its geometry-derived area and GIS `Shape__Area` disagree, so the ring should be treated as a map outline only.
- The parcel response is parcel geometry/assessment evidence. It cannot be interpreted as a building footprint or chapter ownership. The image supports visible roof context, but date, resolution, canopy/obstructions, and roof-to-wall offsets limit footprint inference. The image was not used to propose a Phi Kappa Theta footprint because chapter occupancy is unverified.
- No distinct source feature ID or coordinates are proposed for Phi Kappa Theta. FID 762's address and ring are reported only to document the excluded historic-label collision. No candidate asset is recommended.

## Leads and non-findings

- The chapter website/social links and BoilerLink contact are leads for contacting current chapter officers; they are not evidence of a house address.
- The official site calls the current community a recent rebirth/restart and BoilerLink calls it the newest fraternity; this supports a new/current chapter identity but leaves open whether it has a chapter house.
- Searches of official Purdue FSCL chapter materials, the current BoilerLink listing, and current chapter pages located no address, named residence, chapter-house page, or facility-specific event address. This is a scoped public-source non-finding, not proof of non-occupancy.
- Purdue's 2007 report and the GIS label explain the historic 900 David Ross association. ZBT's current sources and the separate ZBT crosswalk provide affirmative evidence against treating that same site as a new Phi Kappa Theta building; there is no evidence of co-occupancy by the restarted chapter.

## What would resolve it

Request written confirmation from the current chapter adviser/house corporation or Purdue FSCL that provides the chapter's current residential or operating address and whether it is a chapter facility. Then cross-check that address against county parcel records, a current GIS/imagery footprint, and preferably a dated chapter source showing occupancy. A chapter post that gives a street address for an on-site recruitment event, or an FSCL property/house roster entry, would be useful initial evidence. Until then, leave the facility/footprint identity unresolved and do not map FID 762 or invent another feature association.
