# Phi Kappa Theta / FID 762 follow-up (2026-10-27)

**Research scope:** Re-check whether the recently restarted Purdue chapter can be linked to a current physical facility, particularly the existing FID 762 / 900 David Ross site. This supplements `crosswalk_resolution.md`; no building geometry, asset, shared index, or master file was edited.

## New lead: commercial directory lists Phi Kappa Theta at 900 David Ross

A web-indexed WhereOrg listing names “Phi Kappa Theta” at **900 David Ross Rd, West Lafayette, IN 47906**, gives phone **765-463-9521**, and identifies **Sam Brooks** as its president. The listing was crawled approximately five months before this review. Search result: <https://www.whereorg.com/phi-kappa-theta-6010653> (the search index also exposes the same entry in its [Tippecanoe fraternal-organizations directory](https://www.whereorg.com/businesses/civic-social-and-fraternal-associations-in-tippecanoe-cty-in)).

This is a concrete address lead and means the prior wording “no current address ... was found” should be qualified: a current-ish third-party listing associates the organization with the address. It is **not sufficient to establish that Phi Kappa Theta currently occupies or owns a chapter house**, because WhereOrg does not disclose its source or last verification date, and its phone and claimed chapter contact do not match the live chapter’s current public Purdue BoilerLink contact (which lists `phikaps@purdue.edu`, 317-872-9934, and no street address). The chapter website also publishes no address. The WhereOrg page could not be retrieved directly during this review (request timed out), so no source HTML was saved; the index result is preserved here as a lead, not as a raw source capture.

## Independent conflicting current address evidence

The current Zeta Beta Tau Purdue homepage says Fall 2026 recruitment events will be held at **900 David Ross Road**, and its current contact page lists that address for the chapter: <https://zbtpurdue.com/> and <https://zbtpurdue.com/contact/>. The ZBT crosswalk review already documents those primary chapter sources, a 2018 Purdue Exponent listing, and a 2017 national chapter-house sign/photo. The GIS returns one building footprint at this address (FID 762), not two. Thus the new WhereOrg listing could indicate an outdated Phi Kappa Theta listing, historical database carryover, shared use, or a site transition; public sources inspected here do not distinguish these possibilities. Do not create a second building or duplicate FID 762 on this lead alone.

## Alumni corporation address is not a house crosswalk

The IRS-derived nonprofit profile for `Phi Kappa Theta Fraternity INC Indiana Zeta Alumni` (EIN 35-1095809) identifies its FY2024 Form 990, says it supports Purdue students, and gives its IRS Business Master File principal address as Chicago, Illinois: <https://philanthropy.org/990/report/351095809/phi-kappa-theta-fraternity-inc-indiana-zeta-alumni>. It lists Sam Brooks among FY2024 officers. This is consistent with the commercial listing being connected to the alumni organization, but the Chicago mailing/principal address is not evidence for or against a local chapter residence. The profile is a third-party presentation of IRS filing/BMF data; its underlying FY2024 return could not be downloaded from that site during this check (HTTP 403).

## Updated determination

A **current-ish directory record now ties Phi Kappa Theta to 900 David Ross**, but a **current chapter-house occupancy crosswalk remains unverified** because stronger, current chapter evidence assigns that single site to ZBT and the chapter's own live address fields are blank. The FID 762 building is already modeled once as ZBT, so the physical structure is not unmodeled. The remaining work is identity/occupancy reconciliation, not creation of a second building model. A direct confirmation from the chapter or Purdue FSCL, or an official current house listing, is needed before changing the accepted building identity or marking a separate Phi Kappa Theta facility complete.
