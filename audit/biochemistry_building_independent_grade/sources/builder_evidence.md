# Biochemistry Building (BCHM) source record

Retrieved 2026-10-25. This model uses the existing manifest identity `biochemistry_building`, export path `buildings/biochemistry_building.blend`, collection `Asset_biochemistry_building`, and local placement. The asset is generated from the current Purdue GIS footprint and the source statements listed below.

## Horizontal geometry and placement

The primary footprint is Purdue University BuildingShapesZip4 FeatureServer, layer 0, feature FID 238 (`BLDG_ABBR=BCHM`, `BUILDING_N=Biochemistry Building`, address 175 S University Street). The source response is geographic EPSG:4326 and is preserved at [sources/biochemistry_building_gis_fid238_2026-10-25.json](sources/biochemistry_building_gis_fid238_2026-10-25.json); layer metadata is preserved at [sources/purdue_gis_building_layer_metadata_2026-10-25.json](sources/purdue_gis_building_layer_metadata_2026-10-25.json). The sole ring has 34 unique vertices and no courtyard hole. The source ring is transformed with the project's established local approximation (east/north meters, Z up) and left unscaled. Its polygon centroid reproduces the manifest placement `[-233.02125685161437, -509.4598104037796]` to floating-point precision. Approximate local ring area is 2,145 m² and perimeter 296.7 m; these are products of the project's local approximation, not survey values.

The GIS feature reports five levels and numeric `F01`–`F04`, `R` and `Extrution` fields. The repeated 12.08 spacing and `R=57.82` are treated provisionally as feet (3.682 m per story interval; 17.625 m roof datum). The service schema does not declare units or datum, so these elevations are explicitly estimates, not surveyed heights. No measured facade elevation or construction drawing was found in the reviewed sources.

## Historic form and use

Purdue Archives' corporate entity record for the Department of Biochemistry describes the Agricultural Chemistry Building as built in 1938, with an addition in 1952, renamed Biochemistry in 1955, expanded in 1981, and remodeled in 1985–87: [Purdue Archives](https://archives.lib.purdue.edu/agents/corporate_entities/641). A prior project evidence snapshot is retained at [sources/prior_biochemistry_building_evidence.json](sources/prior_biochemistry_building_evidence.json).

Purdue's 2008–09 *Catalyst* says “Agricultural Chemistry” remains emblazoned above the south portico. That supports the modeled inscription and south entrance treatment; it does not provide measured dimensions: [Catalyst PDF](https://ag.purdue.edu/department/biochem/_docs/the-catalyst-2008-09-final.pdf). The retrieval snapshot used by the prior project is [sources/prior_biochemistry_building_gis_feature_238.json](sources/prior_biochemistry_building_gis_feature_238.json).

Purdue's current department contact page confirms the address and room 120: [Biochemistry contact page](https://ag.purdue.edu/department/biochem/about-us/contact-us.html), with a local capture at [sources/purdue_biochemistry_contact_2026-10-25.html](sources/purdue_biochemistry_contact_2026-10-25.html). Purdue also publishes a building emergency-plan page: [Biochemistry building emergency plans](https://ag.purdue.edu/department/biochem/resources/building-emergency-plans.html), locally captured at [sources/purdue_biochemistry_emergency_plan_page_2026-10-25.html](sources/purdue_biochemistry_emergency_plan_page_2026-10-25.html). These confirm current building identity/use and support circulation context, not facade dimensions.

Synthesis Incorporated's project page describes laboratory and office renovation work in the Biochemistry Building and Office of the State Chemist: [Synthesis project record](https://www.synthinc.com/projects/purdue-university-biochemistry-building-office-of-the-state-chemist-lab-renovation-), locally captured at [sources/synthesis_biochemistry_renovation_2026-10-25.html](sources/synthesis_biochemistry_renovation_2026-10-25.html). It is a contractor's project record and is used only as contextual support for laboratory use and renovation, not as authoritative geometry.

## Model scope and uncertainty

The exact GIS ring controls only the exterior footprint. Five above-ground levels are supported by the GIS level count; their intervals and the roof height remain provisional because field units are undocumented. The massing, brick academic/laboratory character, south portico and historic Agricultural Chemistry inscription are supported by the records above. Window count and proportions, exact floor-to-floor elevations, facade composition, wall thickness, entry dimensions, roof profile, roof equipment placement, hidden courtyards/addition joints, and interior layout are not established by measured source drawings. Roof equipment and the small laboratory vignette are schematic. The single GIS exterior ring is modeled without any courtyard holes; no unverified interior room walls are included.

The original project folder contains a prior revision render, but it is retained as model history only and is not treated as independent source evidence. The canonical builder produced [the overall model preview](biochemistry_building_preview.png); [a south-facing portico view](biochemistry_building_south_portico_preview.png) makes the documented entrance inscription visible.
