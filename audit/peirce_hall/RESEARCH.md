## Identity and footprint

The Purdue official campus GIS snapshot identifies feature `FID=216`, abbreviation `PRCE`, name **Peirce Hall**, address **701 3rd Street**, and `NUM_LEVELS=3`. The feature reports `Shape__Area=1555.84 m²` and `Shape__Length=170.12 m`. The footprint comes from the local snapshot [`../official_purdue_buildings_wgs84.json`](../official_purdue_buildings_wgs84.json), with layer metadata in [`../official_building_layer_metadata.json`](../official_building_layer_metadata.json). The GIS service is [Purdue campus FeatureServer](https://mapsweb.lib.purdue.edu/arcgis/rest/services/Purdue/campus/FeatureServer).

The projected measured footprint bounds in the project campus frame are X −134.8194…−95.5219 m and Y −98.0691…−72.5735 m. Model-local bounds of the simplified footprint are X −19.6488…+19.6488 m and Y −12.7478…+12.7478 m. The model origin and corresponding master marker origin are the center of projected footprint bounds: **[−115.170644, −85.321301] m**. The asset root remains at local `[0,0,0]`, scale `[1,1,1]`; no baked campus translation is applied.

Google Maps satellite imagery was inspected on the retrieval date at the Purdue campus pin [40.42675, −86.91515](https://www.google.com/maps/@40.42675,-86.91515,163m/data=!3m1!1e3). It supports the attached roof footprint, flat roof, and neighboring-building context. The [Purdue Events Peirce Hall page](https://events.purdue.edu/peirce_hall) identifies the building and address and supplies a façade photo posted May 30, 2022; the photo was used for brick character, regular sash groupings, six tall recessed/infilled middle bays with narrow lower inset slots, and the principal entry/canopy cues. Its direct image is [here](https://localist-images.azureedge.net/photos/40102458412925/card/dcd8f86af992cb56cc847a7bbf16fdaae69d1eff.jpg). The modeled principal façade follows the visible six upper sash groups and six tall bays; each bay has a dark narrow lower inset interpreted from the photo. North façade windows, bays, and doors are cut into the shell as shallow apertures, with glazing and infill behind the exterior face and reveals in the openings. A close north-façade render makes all six lower slots legible. The photo supports a prominent canopied public entrance. A nearby second door remains an inference, so it is now narrower and has only a small lintel, without a canopy or hall plaque. The source does not give a reliable scale for its exact position. Long side and rear window openings have shallow reveals for depth; their count, spacing, and profile remain generalized because those elevations were not verified in imagery.

## Historical context and current interior clues

[Purdue Archives’ Peirce Hall history](https://archives.lib.purdue.edu/agents/corporate_entities/673) says the present hall is the remaining north end of a building constructed/connected in 1935; its south end was removed in 1989–90 for the Class of 1950 Lecture Hall. Psychological Sciences connected on the north in 1983, and a small addition was made in 1980. [Purdue Physics’ history](https://www.physics.purdue.edu/about/history/early_years.html) clarifies that the original 1905 Physics building was later renamed and its site is now occupied by Class of 1950; the model represents the current Peirce Hall footprint, not the demolished 1905 structure or Peirce Conservatory.

The [Purdue HHS Psychology graduate handbook (2023)](https://hhs.purdue.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/PSY-Graduate-Handbook.pdf) references offices, laboratories, a lounge, and PRCE room 385D. A [PRCE 277 room listing](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~bulrich/workshop.html) from 2008 provides historical classroom-use evidence, not a current room assignment. A [March 21, 2022 report](https://www.basedinlafayette.com/p/a-new-purdue-mural-from-all-angles) describes a two-piece mural in a third-floor break lounge. The model includes an interpretive lounge with abstract, non-copied artwork, modest workstations, shared tables, storage, and a small teaching-room setup. Interior partitions and furniture placement are illustrative; they do not claim to reproduce a current measured plan.

## Measured versus inferred

**Measured/identified from local official GIS:** building identity, code, address, 3-level count, polygon, polygon bounds and perimeter/area attributes. **Observed from imagery:** flat roof massing, attached context, front brick/window rhythm, recessed middle bays, and principal entry treatment. **Inferred:** exact roof height and floor elevations; official GIS `Extrution=21` is preserved as an attribute but its units/meaning are unknown and it was not used as a height. The model uses 11.7 m to roof and 3.6–3.7 m floor spacing as estimates from the reported three levels and photo proportions. Side/rear window counts and spacing, secondary-entry location, materials beyond visible brick/stone/metal/glazing cues, interior circulation, room positions, and furnishings are generalized or interpretive. No current dimensioned floor plan or elevation was verified; this pass added no speculative room layout.

## Asset checks and honest rating

The project was saved and reopened by Blender 5.2.3 for QA rendering. `verification.json` reports PASS: scene `Building_peirce_hall`; asset collection `Asset_peirce_hall` with nested `Interior_Interpretive`; root at zero, unit scale; no external image dependencies; 10 procedural/material-only materials; closed structural shell with zero non-manifold edges. QA counts six north façade upper sash groups, six tall recessed bays, and six lower inset slots. It verifies the apertures are cut into the shell and confirms doorway gaps and headers in the partition segments on all three modeled floors. Exterior, north elevation, north-bay detail, entry detail, ground-floor cutaway, and a clean mural close-up are saved as `peirce_exterior.png`, `peirce_front_elevation.png`, `peirce_north_bay_detail.png`, `peirce_north_entries.png`, `peirce_interior_cutaway.png`, and `peirce_third_floor_lounge.png`. Brick bump was strengthened subtly for better relief at close range; the lounge QA view isolates the documented two-part mural against a neutral wall backdrop.

**Self-assessment after the independent 8.6/10 re-review and this focused pass: 8.6/10.** Footprint alignment and modular structure are strong; the principal façade follows the photographed counts, has six visible lower inset slots, and the secondary entrance now reads as subordinate and inferred. Long side and rear window panels have real shallow shell recesses, although their schedule remains generalized. Interior doors pass through partition gaps with headers, and the office/classroom and mural detail are clearly presented. Exact dimensions/elevations and a measured current interior plan remain unavailable, so unphotographed elevations and room assignments remain visualization rather than survey-grade reconstruction. I keep the rating at the independent review level because this pass improved uncertainty handling and depth without adding new measured evidence.
