## Measurement and model decisions

- The GIS polygon is reprojected to the project’s local campus approximation using `X=(longitude + 86.9137) × 111412.84 × cos(40.4274°)` and `Y=(latitude - 40.4274) × 111040`. This is a project-local approximation, not a surveyed engineering CRS. The footprint bounding-box center is the local origin and agrees with the manifest placement.
- Reopen verification compares all 20 unique GIS-ring vertices with the plinth mesh bidirectionally. Maximum symmetric nearest-vertex error is recorded in `reopen_verification.json` and is below `0.000001 m` (floating-point precision). The GIS plinth is the survey-backed plan outline; decorative porch/ramp/roof geometry is not treated as a wall-footprint measurement.
- Purdue’s facility images show a two-storey white clapboard house, a steep shingle gable roof, the entrance/front porch, and sash-window rhythm. The Fifth Street front is modeled as an eave elevation with four upper window bays and the front entrance toward one end; the north/south ends carry the gable form. That composition follows the saved front and corner photographs.
- The source-backed room program includes a lounge/library, dining/pantry, kitchen, stairs/nook, conference room, and IT computer lab. The in-model partitions, furniture, and exact room positions are illustrative schematics, not measured plans.
