## R2 change

R2 reconciles the overlapping Purdue GIS records and corrects the unsupported height of the small FID 108 structure.

- Registered Purdue GIS FID 857 and FID 108 to the State of Indiana's current orthophoto. The orthophoto is a 2023 Tippecanoe tile in a statewide 2022–2025 collection, 6-inch nominal resolution, EPSG:3857, CC0. The documented approximate campus-local conversion was inverted to longitude/latitude and projected to Web Mercator. The overlay shows FID 857 tracing the main multi-wing ME complex and FID 108 tracing a small low-roof structure at its southeast edge. This resolves the broad identity of the two records but does not prove whether the structures touch or connect internally. The source image and overlay are `indiana_current_orthophoto_me_r2.jpg` and `indiana_current_orthophoto_me_r2_gis_overlay.jpg`; catalog response and transform are in `mechanical_engineering_ortho_registration_r2.json`.
- Registered the FID 108 GIS centroid (world XY 108.423, 69.182 m) to Indiana's 2018 3DEP QL2 DSM. The 5-ft DSM cell there is 634.840 ftUS. The same-point bare-earth DEM ImageServer query is 621.070 ftUS. Their difference is 13.770 ft / 4.197 m. The DSM cell and its eight immediate neighbors are tightly grouped at 634.72–634.91 ftUS, and the 2023 orthophoto shows a low, nearly flat roof at that point. I lowered only `ME_Official_108_Structural_Envelope` to 4.20 m and moved its flat roof cap to z=4.20–4.45 m. This is a rounded approximate roof datum, not a surveyed wall/parapet height.
- Removed the unsupported FID 108 floors at z=4.5, 9.0, and 13.5 m from the exported `Asset_mechanical_engineering` collection. They remain recoverable in the hidden `ME_R2_Archive_Unverified_FID108_Upper_Floors` collection, outside the asset export.
- FID 857 geometry, its 18.5 m top, windows, entrance, local XY boundary, and the asset root were not changed. The 2018 DSM/2016–2020 DTM comparison gives FID 857 a broad 6×6 interior-grid surface-minus-terrain distribution of about 24–71 ft (p10–p90), with strong spatial variation. Since the DSM contains roof, parapet, equipment, and possible canopy returns, and there is no registered roof plan tying these surfaces to individual wings, these readings cannot safely set one common height or map every roof section. The existing height remains unverified pending a registered wing elevation/roof plan.
- Reviewed Purdue's 2025 ME emergency-plan evacuation maps (dated 2025-08-21, PDF slides 11–15) together with the 2015 ME-RAIL-HAMP planning study (PDF pp. 40–46 and 64–65). The current maps document evacuation paths and show ME/Gatewood floor plans and vertical cores. The 2015 study says the original ME/Gatewood areas connect horizontally only at the ground and second floors, and documents the main ME elevator serving ground through third and mechanical floors. The maps are not metric/as-built plans, and route diagrams are not a complete circulation or room layout; no interior geometry was moved or newly traced from them. Current post-renovation room arrangement and precise corridor/stair/elevator coordinates remain unresolved.

## Source records and measurement limits

1. **Purdue BuildingShapes FeatureServer layer 0.** [Source service](https://services1.arcgis.com/mLNdQKiKsj5Z5YMN/arcgis/rest/services/BuildingShapesZip4/FeatureServer/0); cached polygons and project-local XY in `audit/official_building_references.json`; feature IDs 857 and 108. The source coordinates are converted with the project approximation around 40.4274°N, 86.9137°W. This is not survey control.
2. **Indiana Current Orthophotography.** [ImageServer metadata](https://di-ingov.img.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/DynamicWebMercator/Indiana_Current_Imagery/ImageServer?f=pjson) states a 2022–2025 statewide image collection, nominal 6-inch pixels, CC0. Catalog identify at the ME points selected `in2023_30001885_06` (Tippecanoe, 2023). Request and coordinate details are in `mechanical_engineering_ortho_registration_r2.json`. This is an overhead roof-footprint comparison, not a wall survey.
3. **Indiana 2018 QL2 3DEP DSM.** [DSM tile](https://giselevationingov.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/dsmoptimized/statewide/2018/SPW/5ft/in2018_30001885_12.tif); 2018-04-20 collection, 5-ft cells, US survey feet, NAD83(HARN) / Indiana West (ftUS), CC0. Downloaded source bytes and SHA-256 are recorded in `mechanical_engineering_lidar_r2.json`. [Indiana 2016–2020 bare-earth DEM metadata](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=a95c857cb47649ec87b04810214b0c1c) documents a 2.5-ft product for most counties and statewide 10 cm vertical/positional accuracy standards. The DSM minus DTM result is a coarse surface-above-ground estimate; local roof surfaces and exact wall tops remain subject to raster, registration, and interpolation uncertainty.
4. **Purdue ME emergency plan, current safety presentation.** `safety2025.pdf` in this audit folder, slides 11–15, each dated 2025-08-21. Rendered source pages are `me_safety2025_page_11.png` through `me_safety2025_page_15.png`. Use is limited to current evacuation paths, spaces shown, and vertical-core/connection context; it is not a metric architectural drawing.
5. **Purdue ME-RAIL-HAMP Study, June 30, 2015.** [Original PDF](https://engineering.purdue.edu/~frosch/Renderings/Masterplans/ME-RAIL-HAMP%20Study.pdf), retained as `study2015.pdf`. Pages 40–46 show historical existing floor plans and the study explicitly excludes the Gatewood Addition. Page 64 describes building phases and floor connections and includes qualitative north/east elevations; none is tied to measured facade/roof elevations. Its historical interior does not establish current post-renovation room layout.
