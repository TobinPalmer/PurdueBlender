Purdue University Harrison Street Parking Structure

Client:
Purdue University
Location:
West Lafayette, IN

Project Details

In an attempt to realign nine academic units into a new college linked to Purdue University’s strategic plan, the university launched its new College of Health and Human Sciences at Purdue University.

WGI completed functional design and structural engineering services for the Harrison Street Parking Structure, an 855-space stand-alone cast-in-place parking structure that will support the adjacent Health & Human Sciences building and a new Drug Discovery building across the street. The six level structure includes one level below grade.

American Concrete Institute (ACI) Indiana Chapter Outstanding Achievement Award-Winner.
