How can I look up information about a business (registered agent, status, address, etc.)

  • February 24, 2023 05:50
  • Updated
Follow

You can easily search for businesses records on INBiz and see their status here: https://bsd.sos.in.gov/publicbusinesssearch

Enter your business name and choose Search. Locate your business in the search results by considering the name, address and registered agent information that is displayed.

The status for your business will appear in the Status column. The status will appear as Active if the entity is current on its business entity reports and has a valid registered agent.
