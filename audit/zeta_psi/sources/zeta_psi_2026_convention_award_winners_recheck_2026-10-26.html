2026 Convention Award Winners

August 12th, 2026 in Convention, News

Zeta Psi Fraternity was proud to recognize the achievements and contributions of brothers, chapters, and friends of the Fraternity during our 179th Annual Convention in Chapel Hill, North Carolina.

These awards celebrate outstanding leadership, service, scholarship, brotherhood, and dedication to Zeta Psi and the broader fraternal community. Congratulations to all of this year’s award recipients!
