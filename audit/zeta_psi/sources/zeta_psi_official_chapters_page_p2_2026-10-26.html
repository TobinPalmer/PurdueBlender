Chapters

Chapters are listed at the Universities where the charters were granted. Being listed here does not imply that the chapter is recognized by that University. Where recognition is available, Zeta Psi typically seeks formal recognition from schools where that status is available. In some cases, Zeta Psi chapters are located at Universities without a formal recognition process — and are therefore “unrecognized.” Please check with the host institution for inquiries on Zeta Psi’s recognition status at a specific institution.
