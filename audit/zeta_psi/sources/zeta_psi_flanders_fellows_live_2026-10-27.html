Introducing the Flanders Fellows Program

October 20th, 2026 in Alumni, General, Impact

Investing in the Future of Zeta Psi

For more than 175 years, Zeta Psi has been built on a simple idea: that the relationships, experiences, and opportunities of the Fraternity shape lives long after college. The Flanders Fellows Program represents a new opportunity for Brothers to invest in that future and ensure educational initiatives remain a defining part of the Zeta Psi experience.

Established through the Lt. Col. John A. McCrae Leadership Fund, an endowed fund designed to provide perpetual support, only the interest from contributions will be dispersed annually. The program is named in recognition of Lt. Col. John A. McCrae, whose legacy connects Zeta Psi’s history with its commitment to developing future leaders.

Building a Legacy Through Leadership

A $1,000 gift to the Lt. Col. John A. McCrae Leadership Fund qualifies a donor for membership as a Flanders Fellow at the Bronze level. Donors may make cumulative contributions to reach additional recognition levels:

  • Bronze: $1,000+
  • Silver: $5,000+
  • Gold: $10,000+
  • Diamond: $15,000+

Gifts may be made in honor of another person, to commemorate a Zeta Psi milestone (such as graduation or board service), or in memory of a special individual. Flanders Fellows will be recognized with a unique pin device commemorating their commitment.

Note: Flanders Fellow contributions are intended to supplement, not replace, Annual Fund support, which provides flexible resources for immediate needs.

 

Growing a Culture of Major Giving

The Flanders Fellows Program is part of a broader effort to strengthen philanthropic support for Zeta Psi. Currently, major annual gifts of $1,847 or more are recognized through the 1847 Society.

Over the next three years, the Development Team will focus on identifying and stewarding the Foundation’s top 200 alumni prospects. This effort builds on relationships cultivated through regional alumni receptions, international events (Kirby, Convention, Board Meetings), and personal outreach, reinforcing our core philosophy: build relationships before asking for gifts.

An Endowment for Generations

The Zeta Psi Educational Foundation provides over $160,000 in scholarships and $380,000 in educational grants annually, supporting programs like the Kirby Conference and Leadership Convention.

Unlike annual gifts spent immediately, endowed contributions remain invested, allowing your gift to support Brothers year after year. Join the Flanders Fellows today and help ensure that young Zetes continue to become scholars, leaders, and citizens for generations to come.
