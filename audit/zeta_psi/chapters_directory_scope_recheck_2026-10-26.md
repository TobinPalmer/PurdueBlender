# Zeta Psi live chapter-directory scope check

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-26  
**Question:** Does the absence of Tau Gamma from the default view of Zeta Psi's official chapter page imply that it is no longer an active chapter?  
**Conclusion:** No. The page is paginated. Its first result page ends with chapters established by 1892, while page 2 explicitly lists “Tau Gamma — Purdue University,” West Lafayette, Indiana, established 1968, with links to Purdue Tau Gamma's Facebook and Instagram accounts. The apparent omission resulted from reviewing only page 1. The live directory supports current national listing of Tau Gamma at Purdue and is consistent with Purdue FSCL's current IFC roster; it says nothing about the chapter's current house address or occupancy at 950 David Ross Road.

## Scope and method

The official [Zeta Psi Chapters page](https://zetapsi.org/about/chapters/) states that chapters are listed at the universities where their charters were granted. Its default HTML view includes a four-page FacetWP pager (the page-1 snapshot is `sources/zeta_psi_official_chapters_page_recheck_2026-10-26.html`). The first page shown in the live page text contains entries from 1847 through 1892 and does not contain Purdue. Directly requesting `https://zetapsi.org/about/chapters/?fwp_paged=2` returns the next result page; its retained HTML snapshot is `sources/zeta_psi_official_chapters_page_p2_2026-10-26.html`.

Page 2 identifies Tau Gamma at Purdue and links both `facebook.com/zetapsipurdue` and `instagram.com/zetapsipurdue`. The page also lists some inactive chapters explicitly, so a missing entry could otherwise appear potentially meaningful; here the pagination explains it. The official directory's own record means no inference from absence is warranted.

## Effect on the occupancy assessment

This strengthens evidence that Zeta Psi's national organization continues to list Tau Gamma at Purdue. It does **not** establish a current facility address: the page gives only West Lafayette, Indiana, and social-media links. It therefore does not resolve the conflict between Tau Gamma's undated 950 David Ross profile address and Chi Psi's dated July 2024 identification of 950 David Ross as its new Lodge.
