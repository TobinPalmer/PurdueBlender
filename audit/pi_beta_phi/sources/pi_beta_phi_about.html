About Us

Each year, thousands of women become members of Pi Beta Phi, joining one of our more than 130 chapters across North America. Pi Phi's collegiate chapters are divided into nine regions––see where each chapter is located here.

The Mission of Pi Beta Phi Fraternity for Women is to promote friendship, develop women of integrity, cultivate leadership potential and enrich lives through community service.
