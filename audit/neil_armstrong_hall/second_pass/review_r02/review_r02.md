## Top remaining findings, by visual impact

1. **West wing SE (courtyard) face.** The model has two-storey framed windows. The photos (`user_provided_rear_entrance_1/2`) show one row of small punched windows with sunshade fins and mostly blank brick below.
2. **NW point soffit.** From below, the triangular opening (with sky through it), the strut and the bright red inner panel barely read. The panel-joint grid is also missing. Compare `2016_01` and `2016_03` with `r2_point_below` and `r2_photo5_point_W`.
3. **Colour.** The brick is too orange and too light; the photos show a darker red-brown with purple tones. The glass is an opaque dark teal; the photos show lighter, reflective blue-green-grey glass.
4. **Main bar window rhythm and trim.** The south windows are about twice as widely spaced as in `rear_entrance_1` and have no limestone sills or heads. On the north facade, the ground-floor windows should be taller (`2016_01`, drone photo).
5. **West wing SW face** (`DD_24`). Left of the pier there should be blank brick with the name letters, and two near-square upper windows with fins, not a tall window and slot pairs. The opening under the canopy should read as two door pairs.

Smaller findings:
- The lower part of the rear curtain wall has no mullions.
- The window at the east end of the west wing's north face is a narrow slot; the drone photo shows a wide window bay.
- The white cap over the wing's glass top floor has no overhang or sloped soffit.

## Uncertainty

- There is no ground photo of the east end, so its windows, the east entrance and the SE wing heights are unverified.
- The NW point's tilt and reach are estimates.
- Colours were judged from photos that are not colour-calibrated.
