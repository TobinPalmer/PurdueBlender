The source library reopens, material checks pass, and lane one measures about 199.989 m around the six-lane track. Trusses, GIS-edge bands, and the historic tower cue improve the scene. The main issue is massing: the continuous arched roof does not distinguish the documented one-story 172×303 ft fieldhouse from the separate four-story 110×210 ft gym section. Roof heights/curvature remain estimates. The historic postcard supports a tower historically, but not its current survival/location/size. Band and pier placement is not geometrically registered, and the interior view weakly demonstrates track/balcony layout.

Next revision should separate the source-supported fieldhouse and gym volumes, include a plan/section overlay against the official footprint and archival dimensions, label the tower as historic-only unless current evidence supports it, verify facade features spatially, and provide a brighter connected interior view.
