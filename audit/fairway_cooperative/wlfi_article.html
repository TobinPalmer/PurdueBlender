Purdue BYX expansion puts focus on faith

By Cody Melin
Published: Sep. 28, 2026 at 10:38 AM EDT
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

WEST LAFAYETTE, Ind. (WLFI) - History is being made on Purdue University’s campus as the Fairway Cooperative Association celebrates the expansion of Purdue Beta Upsilon Chi at the Fairway House.

The expansion increases the home’s capacity from 70 to 130 students, making it the largest Greek or cooperative-style student residence in America by bed capacity, according to the Fairway Cooperative Association.

The Fairway House brings together the Fairway Cooperative Association and Beta Upsilon Chi, a Christian social fraternity.

The expanded house is designed to provide space for fellowship, brotherhood and community. New features include study rooms, lounges, sleeping rooms and a multipurpose chapter room that can be used for everything from meetings to worship services.

Ethan Johnson, president of BYX, tells News 18 the new facilities are an improvement, but the chapter’s purpose is what distinguishes it from other fraternities.

“We function just like any other fraternity. We throw the parties, we interact, we throw events, all those things. But the one thing that makes us different is we love the Lord and we serve the Lord and we exist to grow Christian men,” Johnson tells News 18. “So every fraternity grows brotherhood, but we do it uniquely in a Christian setting and to grow the men as believers.”

The expanded house also relies on its residents to help keep it running. Students share responsibilities for preparing meals, completing house duties and maintaining the property.

At the heart of the house is a dedicated prayer room, keeping the focus on faith.
