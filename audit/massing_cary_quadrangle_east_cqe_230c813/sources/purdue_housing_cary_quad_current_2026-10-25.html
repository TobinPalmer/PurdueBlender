Welcome to Cary Quadrangle

Cary Quadrangle is one of Purdue University's historic all-male residence halls, offering a unique blend of tradition and modern comfort. While Cary is the oldest hall on campus, its four outer buildings—West, Northwest, Northeast, and East—were fully renovated in 2007. These updates introduced air-conditioned double-suite-style rooms, where two rooms share a private connecting bathroom. 

The South building preserves its original layout, providing a variety of non-air-conditioned room types, including singles, doubles, triples, and quads. These rooms feature community-style restrooms located throughout each floor, fostering a classic residence hall experience.

Cary Club is the Residence Hall Club of Cary Quad. All Cary Quad residents are members of this student organization. Cary Club plans and implements events for residents, purchases materials for residents to rent from the front desk and works to create a sense of community throughout the entire hall. Our hall clubs across campus serve as a great gateway to your first leadership opportunity on campus! Interested? Connect with your Resident Assistant. 

 

Cary Club also operates WCCR, a student-run radio station, and Cary Racing, a club that participates in the Purdue Grand Prix each year.
