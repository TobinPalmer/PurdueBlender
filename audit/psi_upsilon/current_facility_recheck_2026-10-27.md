# Psi Upsilon / Delta Omicron facility status recheck — 2026-10-27

**Assignment:** Rossen. **Question:** Does newer official evidence identify a dedicated current facility, address, or footprint for Delta Omicron at Purdue?

## Findings

Current official Psi Upsilon pages show the Purdue chapter is active and recently installed as a full chapter, but do not give a residence address. The [national chapter roll](https://psiu.org/chapter-roll/) lists Delta Omicron at Purdue. The fraternity's [Purdue installation announcement](https://psiu.org/psi-upsilon-to-install-its-50th-chapter-at-purdue/) and current [chapter news](https://psiu.org/category/news/) describe chapter activity and installation, with no property or address claim. The [Purdue chapter site](https://psiupsilonatpurdue.com/) likewise publishes no street address or dedicated-facility statement.

The official [2025 petition PDF](https://psiu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Psi-Upsilon-Delta-Omicron-Provisional-Chapter-Petition.pdf), retrieved 2026-10-27, has a Facilities section (printed page 7) stating that members arranged their own housing, mostly off-campus, and that the chapter was saving for a housing fund while exploring permanent housing. This describes the petition's 2024 reporting period, so it cannot establish the chapter's housing status after installation. It does confirm that the historical 400 Northwestern / 1198 Third leads should not be treated as a verified chapter facility.

The latest [Purdue IFC roster](https://www.purdue.edu/fscl/councils/ifc/chapters.php), [national chapter roll](https://psiu.org/chapter-roll/), [national chapter-service contact page](https://psiu.org/about/staff/), and [current chapter site](https://psiupsilonatpurdue.com/) were rechecked on 2026-10-27. They establish current chapter activity or contact links, but none provides a current dedicated-facility address. The county and campus GIS checks remain as documented in [the October 26 county/GIS follow-up](current_facility_recheck_2026-10-26_addendum4.md); without a current address or traceable property entity, they cannot yield an attributable parcel or building polygon.

## Disposition

No current address, parcel, or defensible footprint is established. **No model is justified by this recheck.** Keep the facility lead unresolved; reopen modeling only if current chapter or property evidence identifies a dedicated facility and supports a building-footprint crosswalk.
