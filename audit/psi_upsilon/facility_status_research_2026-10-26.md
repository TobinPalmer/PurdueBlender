# Psi Upsilon / Delta Omicron facility status

**Research date:** 2026-10-26  
**Current assignment:** Rossen  
**GIS feature / asset:** None verified

## Finding

Do not model a chapter house from the available address evidence. The chapter's Fall 2024 petition says members procure their own housing (mostly off-campus), that the chapter is saving for a housing fund, and that it is working toward procuring permanent housing. The petition describes a future goal, not an existing chapter house or an address that can be mapped to a building. Psi Upsilon's national organization announced the Purdue chapter's installation in October 2025, and its 2025/2026 chapter news confirms activity, but those later sources reviewed here do not identify a house or show that the housing goal was completed.

This is **unverified current facility status**, not proof that the chapter still lacks housing in October 2026. Keep the assignment in facility research and confirm with a current Purdue chapter or FSCL contact before treating it as a physical-building assignment or constructing geometry. Do not infer a building from member mailing addresses, event locations, or an unrelated nearby chapter.
