# Psi Upsilon / Delta Omicron current-facility recheck

**Research date:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** Post-installation facility status, address, and GIS crosswalk. No people were contacted.

## Finding

No current Delta Omicron chapter house address or GIS feature can be assigned from the public primary sources reviewed. This is **unverified current facility status**, not proof the chapter has no house.

## Dated evidence

- **Fall 2024 petition, filed January 2025:** The chapter says members procure their own housing, mostly off campus, and that it was saving toward permanent undergraduate housing. Its bylaws also make a Housing Committee conditional on whether the fraternity owns or operates a house. These are pre-installation statements and do not establish the post-2025 status. [Official petition, pp. 7 and 35](https://psiu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Psi-Upsilon-Delta-Omicron-Provisional-Chapter-Petition.pdf).
- **October 18, 2025:** Psi Upsilon's Winter 2025 report documents Delta Omicron's installation as a full chapter. It gives no facility/address. [Official report, p. 2](https://psiu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Garnet_Winter25_12.10.2025_2315.pdf).
- **2025–2026 activity:** The national chapter roll lists Delta Omicron at Purdue (2025), and its 2025 annual chapter report describes a 32-member chapter and 2026 activity. Neither source identifies a facility. [Chapter roll](https://psiu.org/chapter-roll/), [2026 chapter news](https://psiu.org/category/news/).
- **Current chapter site:** The national roll links the chapter's site, [psiupsilonatpurdue.com](https://psiupsilonatpurdue.com/). Its homepage and contact section provide no street address. The linked rush schedule shows events at UNIV 129 and the IM Fields; those are event locations, not evidence of a chapter house. No map pin or coordinates are published there.
- **400 Northwestern Avenue lead ruled out:** A third-party directory associates Psi Upsilon with 400 Northwestern Avenue. Tippecanoe County's April 15, 2021 zoning report identifies that site as the Purdue Delta Tau Delta property and its fraternity home. Delta Tau Delta's own Gamma Lambda annual fund page also calls 400 Northwestern its chapter house. This address must not be assigned to Psi Upsilon. [County report](https://www.tippecanoe.in.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/11186?fileID=21927), [Delta Tau Delta Gamma Lambda annual fund](https://gammalambda.causevox.com/).

## Address and GIS status

No supported Psi Upsilon street address, parcel, Purdue GIS FID, or building footprint was found. The 2024 petition's description of members' individual off-campus housing and the rush schedule's campus venues cannot be used as a facility crosswalk. A dated post-installation statement from the chapter or Purdue FSCL must identify a dedicated facility or confirm that none exists; any stated address then needs a current parcel/building-footprint match.

**Research limit:** This review found no public primary-source address; it cannot rule out a private or recently acquired lease that the chapter has not published.
