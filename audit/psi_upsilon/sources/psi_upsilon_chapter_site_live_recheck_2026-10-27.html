About Psi Upsilon at Purdue

Our Mission

Our Mission

Our Mission

At Psi Upsilon, we strive to uphold the five core values upon which our fraternity is based:

Lifelong Friendship, Moral Leadership, Intellectual Engagement, Responsible Social Conduct, and Service to Society.

Why Join?

Our Mission

Our Mission

We are one of Purdue's first gender-inclusive social fraternities, and we offer an incredibly unique and rich fraternity experience. Our members gain leadership experience and forge lifelong bonds of friendship between each and every member.
