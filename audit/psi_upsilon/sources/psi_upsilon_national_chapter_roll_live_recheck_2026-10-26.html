Link Policy | The chapters of Psi Upsilon Fraternity are encouraged to develop web sites that enhance the public image of the Fraternity. To be linked from the Psi Upsilon Fraternity web site, the contents of a chapter’s home page must be in good taste, maintained regularly, and appropriate. Web sites that contain inappropriate language, offensive images, or generally reflect poorly on the chapters and members of Psi Upsilon will not be linked.

Chapters may include social events in their web sites but should not in any way encourage the illegal use or abuse of alcohol. Web sites that promote the use of illegal drugs; or that depict races, cultures, religions, or any person in a manner that is demeaning, will not be linked.
