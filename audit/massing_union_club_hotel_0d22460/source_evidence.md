- The crosswalk is resolved: Purdue GIS FID 663 is `Purdue Memorial Union Club` (`PMUC`), address `201 N Grant Street`; it has seven levels and is distinct from the separately mapped `Purdue Memorial Union` (`PMU`). Purdue’s current Events page identifies the 201 N Grant Street PMUC property as the Union Club Hotel and says the hotel was built in phases from 1929–1953 as an addition to PMU. The official campus map independently lists `PMU Purdue Memorial Union` and `PMUC Purdue Memorial Union Club` separately. This detail models the hotel wing, not the main Memorial Union.

### Footprint conversion and comparison

The official GIS ring has 53 distinct vertices. It was converted using the project convention: `X=(longitude+86.9137)*111412.84*cos(40.4274°)`, `Y=(latitude-40.4274)*111040`, with coordinates in meters. This is an approximate local conversion, not a surveyed projection. Converted plan bounds are X 235.802–276.507 m and Y −284.826–−165.414 m (about 40.706 × 119.413 m). The ground-plane polygon area after this conversion is about 2,466.30 m². The previous simple massing had bounds X 235.163–276.737 m and Y −275.934–−165.361 m; its polygon was close but missed the official source's southern extent. The new shell now follows FID 663 directly. A fresh Blender reopen found all 53 converted GIS points in the shell base within a maximum 0.000019 m numerical tolerance.

## Model edits and validation

- Replaced the original one-piece 16 m massing extrusion with the exact converted FID 663 footprint, seven story bands, paired punched-window bays on longer elevations, limestone-color sills/frames and belt courses, segmented slate gable/hip roof masses with multiple ridge heights and larger cross-gable accents, a projecting pale-stone entrance tower with a tall arched window, and a broad covered gabled porte-cochère. Circle Design Group and Marriott exterior photographs directly support these forms/materials qualitatively; exact dimensions and placement remain estimates.
- Kept the stable ID, asset path, scene name, exported collection name, and direct campus-coordinate massing frame. Geometry remains in campus coordinates for direct linking; no marker, manifest, master file, or shared index was edited.
- The GIS `R=63.1` and `Extrution=72` are interpreted as likely feet because their units are absent from the local source export; that would suggest about 21.95 m overall. The model uses 19.23 m roof eaves and an approximately 21.75 m maximum gable ridge based on qualitative roof photos. These are estimates, not surveyed heights. Seven stories are reported by GIS, but floor heights, ridge heights, roof pitch and roof plan are estimated.
- Saved and reopened in Blender 4.5.15 LTS. The fresh validation report is `reopen_validation.json`: expected collection found; 28 objects (27 meshes); 53 source vertices match within 0.000019 m; evaluated world bounds are X 235.514–278.717 m, Y −284.971–−163.166 m, Z 0–21.750 m; all meshes have materials; no unpacked external image dependencies.
- Preview views are in `renders/east_oblique.png`, `renders/south_oblique.png`, `renders/northwest_oblique.png`, and `renders/top.png`. They were rendered after reopening the saved asset.

## Uncertainty retained for grading

The polygon and story count are strongly tied to the official feature. The local tangent-plane transform is approximate; source `Shape__Area` uses projected coordinates; the GIS vertical units are undocumented. Official Circle Design Group/White Lodging and Marriott photos directly support the tower, entry canopy, gable/hip roof variety, brick/stone palette and paired windows. The Purdue Board PDF provides levels 1–5 floor plans but no exterior elevations or roof plan. Perspective and unknown camera positions prevent dimensional measurements; roof pitches/ridges, tower/entry coordinates, bay rhythm and individual architectural proportions remain estimates. No unsupported rooftop penthouse is modeled. Master linkage/integration has not been checked or changed by this build agent.
