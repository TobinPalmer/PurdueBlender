SOS Business Entity Search

This mobile site allows the user to perform a “Full Name” or “Partial Name” search of any business entity within the State of Indiana on your smart phone.

Business Search

The initial search results displayed will indicate:

  • Entity Name
  • Business
  • Entity Type
  • City/State

Selecting an ‘Entity Name’ will provide the complete information for this Entity.
