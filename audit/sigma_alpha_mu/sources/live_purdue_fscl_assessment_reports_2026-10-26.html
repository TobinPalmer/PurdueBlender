FSCL Assessment and Semester Reports

Each semester the Fraternity, Sorority and Cooperative Life Office collects and reports key performance indicators, such as academic performance, service & philanthropy contributions, graduation rates, total membership and organization compliance for all FSCL organizations. Transparency is important to the Purdue Fraternity, Sorority and Cooperative community and governing councils concerning the status of the organizations. Accordingly, reporting includes various data that can be used by the various stakeholders for comparison purposes. We take great pride in the accomplishments of our various student organizations and have high expectations for our community. Therefore, we encourage all students, parents and families to be good consumers as they consider joining an organization.

Highlights & Trends from the Spring 2026 semester include:


Academic Performance

The All Fraternity, Sorority, and Cooperative Semester Average Grade total is a 3.33, which surpasses the All-Undergraduate Semester Average Grade total (3.21).  This is the 25th consecutive semester that FSCL average grade performance has met or surpassed the All-Undergraduate average grade performance.

  • IFC Semester GPA: 3.23
  • MGC Semester GPA: 3.12
  • NPHC Semester GPA: 2.84
  • PCC Semester GPA: 3.41
  • PHA Semester GPA: 3.43
  • New Member Semester GPA: 3.16
  • The FSCL 4-, 5-, and 6-year graduation rates continue to surpass the Purdue University average. University graduation/retention rates for FTFT New Beginners.
    • Fall 2021*** Four-Year Student Graduation Rate:
      • FSCL: 78% (998/1,280)
      • Purdue University: 68% (6,932/10,142)
    • Fall 2020*** Five-Year Student Graduation Rate:
      • FSCL: 88% (999/1,131)
      • Purdue University: 81% (7,074/8,778)
    • Fall 2019*** Six-Year Student Graduation Rate:
      • FSCL: 91% (968/1,061)
      • Purdue University: 85% (6,778/8,011)

Philanthropy & Community Service

FSCL organizations contributed $355,836 ($49.07 per member), 3,765 items, and 43,917 community service hours (6 hours per member) during the fall term. 

Membership:

Total FSCL membership reached 7,266, which is the largest FSCL community in Purdue history, and 90 total organizations. ~17% of Purdue undergraduate students are affiliated with FSCL organizations.
  • Average Chapter/House Size: (7,252/90) = 81                       
    • IFC: (3,778/39) = 97 Members                
    • MGC: (205/11) = 19 Members                 
    • NPHC: (105/9) = 12 Members                  
    • PCC: (331/11) = 30 Members                 
    • PHA: (2,833/20) = 142 Members 
                            
  • FSCL Members find belonging within the Purdue Community at higher rates than do nonaffiliated students.
