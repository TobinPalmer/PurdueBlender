# Identity challenge: 930 N Grant St — 2026-10-27

**Question:** Does evidence establish 930 N Grant St as Sigma Eta / Sigma Alpha Mu Purdue's current chapter house or leased residence, rather than a rush-event venue?

## Decision

**No. The house identity is not established.** The strongest address-specific chapter evidence is the chapter's Aug. 11, 2026 public rush post, which names **Aug. 24** and gives **930 N Grant St**. Its text invites people to join the chapter for rush; it does not call the address a chapter house, residence, or home. It establishes a chapter event/location connection, not occupancy.

The address is a plausible candidate because it is near Purdue and public real-estate/property records describe a residential property that has been rented. But those records do not name Sigma Eta as tenant or occupier. No current SAM, Sigma Eta, Purdue, or house-corporation statement found in this pass links the address to its residential use. No lease/occupancy evidence or competing Greek-letter organization identity surfaced. The available evidence therefore cannot distinguish “rush event at a rented/private residence” from “rush event at the chapter's current house.”

## Competing identity / event-only check

Exact-address searches of chapter, fraternity, rush, and house terms surfaced the chapter's rush post and general real-estate records, but no second fraternity or Greek organization claiming the property. This is only a limited public-web search, not proof that no competing user exists. The rush post's explicit event framing makes “venue” the demonstrated use; no public source found here makes “current chapter residence” the demonstrated use.

## Confidence-raising evidence

Any one of these dated, direct records would materially raise confidence if it names both **Sigma Eta / Sigma Alpha Mu** and **930 N Grant St** in a residential role:

1. A current lease or occupancy/license record naming the chapter, SAM Property Management, or its house corporation as tenant/occupant (sensitive terms may be redacted while retaining tenant, address, and term).
2. A current statement from SAM Property Management, the chapter, its advisor/house corporation, or Purdue FSCL explicitly saying 930 N Grant is the chapter house/residence for the relevant academic year.
3. A Purdue or municipal residential-facility/Greek-housing roster that links the chapter and address, or an official chapter document that lists the address as its house rather than as an event venue.

Until then, retain 930 N Grant as an **unverified event-address lead**, not an established chapter-house identity. Do not use the address alone to assign a building footprint or make a house model.
