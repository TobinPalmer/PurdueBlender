## Decision

## 930 N Grant as event location versus residence

The captured chapter Instagram post dated August 11, 2026 explicitly prints “930 N Grant St” beside an invitation to Fall Rush on August 24, 2026. It is an address-bearing chapter post, but its wording identifies where to join the rush event. The current public profile and roster tie the account to Sigma Eta/SAM Purdue; neither adds a home/residence label. In this fresh search, no separate dated chapter or SAM item was found that uses this address for residential occupancy. Accordingly, the single direct address-to-chapter link remains event/recruitment use, not residence identity.

## Required source to upgrade identity

The crosswalk would be established by a dated current lease or redacted lease excerpt naming Sigma Eta/Sigma Alpha Mu (or its house corporation/SAM Property Management) and 930 N Grant; an official Purdue FSCL or SAM housing record listing that street address as the chapter house; or a city rental registration/inspection record naming the chapter or management entity as occupant/tenant. A dated official chapter statement explicitly calling 930 N Grant the chapter house/residence would also resolve the identity question. For a modeling handoff, a measured building footprint should additionally be matched to that address/parcels and documented separately.
