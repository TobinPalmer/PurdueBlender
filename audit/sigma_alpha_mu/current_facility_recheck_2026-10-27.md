# Sigma Alpha Mu / Sigma Eta facility status recheck — 2026-10-27

**Assignment:** Rossen. **Question:** Do current primary sources identify Sigma Eta's Purdue facility, address, parcel, and building footprint?

## Findings

The current [Sigma Alpha Mu active chapter roll](https://sam.org/fraternity/chapter-roll/) lists Sigma Eta at Purdue. The national [properties and property-management page](https://sam.org/fraternity/housing/) lists a Purdue management account, but does not state a street address, legal property entity, or occupant-to-building crosswalk. Purdue's [IFC roster](https://www.purdue.edu/fscl/councils/ifc/chapters.php) links Sigma Alpha Mu to a social account without publishing a facility address. The [SAM Foundation program page](https://www.sam-fdn.org/about-programs) associates Sigma Eta/Purdue with campus activities, not a property.

The February 2026 [SAM digital edition](https://sam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Digital-Edition-Feb-26.pdf) was also checked for Sigma Eta/Purdue property details. It did not expose an address or parcel suitable for crosswalk. A self-reported public profile mentions a chapter-house lease but gives no address and is not adequate evidence for assigning a specific building.

The official Purdue BuildingShapes and Tippecanoe County parcel searches documented in [the October 26 facility recheck](current_facility_recheck_2026-10-26_addendum2.md) found no exact Sigma Eta/Sigma Alpha Mu address or entity match. Without a street address or traceable property/legal entity, rerunning spatial searches cannot identify which footprint, if any, belongs to the chapter.

## Disposition

Current chapter activity and a Purdue-specific management relationship are supported; the exact property is not. **No current address, parcel, or defensible footprint is established, so no model is justified.** Keep the facility lead unresolved until a traceable property identity or address is available.
