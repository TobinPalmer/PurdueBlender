# Sigma Alpha Mu / Sigma Eta current-facility recheck

**Research date:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** Current chapter facility status, address, and GIS crosswalk. No people were contacted.

## Finding

Sigma Eta is active, and public evidence points to a property-management relationship and a claimed chapter-house lease. No official public source reviewed gives the leased property's address or identifies a current building. No GIS feature can be assigned.

## Dated evidence

- **Current chapter status:** SAM's active roll lists Sigma Eta at Purdue. Purdue's current IFC roster also lists Sigma Alpha Mu and links its chapter account. The SAM roster cautions that school listing alone does not establish university recognition. [SAM chapter roll](https://sam.org/fraternity/chapter-roll/), [Purdue IFC roster](https://www.purdue.edu/fscl/councils/ifc/chapters.php).
- **Property management:** SAM's [National Properties & Property Management page](https://sam.org/fraternity/housing/) lists **Purdue — Manage Property** under current ownership and management accounts. The page's metadata says it was modified April 21, 2026. It provides no street address or building identity. This supports a SAM management account at Purdue; it does not independently establish the property's present occupant or location.
- **Lease claim, early 2025:** Sigma Eta's founder and former president states on his [public profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/neal-ssingh) that he secured a chapter-house lease by early 2025. This is a personal public claim rather than an official SAM, house-corporation, or Purdue property record; it supplies no address and does not confirm the lease remains in effect.
- **172 N. Littleton excluded:** Alpha Chi Theta's historical account says its house was sold to SAM in 1956, but Purdue BoilerLink currently lists **172 Littleton St** as Gemini Cooperative. The historic sale does not establish current SAM occupancy. [Historical account](https://alphachithetas.org/our-story/), [current Purdue Gemini listing](https://boilerlink.purdue.edu/organization/gemini).
- **1165 W Stadium excluded:** Purdue identifies **1165 West Stadium Avenue** as Tarkington Residence Hall's mailing address. It is not evidence of a Sigma Eta facility. [Purdue Residences: Tarkington](https://www.housing.purdue.edu/my-housing/options/residence-halls/tarkington.html), [Purdue building emergency plan](https://www.purdue.edu/operations/emergency-preparedness/tark/).

## Address and GIS status

The public official chapter, national property-management, Purdue IFC, and Purdue BoilerLink records reviewed do not identify Sigma Eta's current house address. The founder/president lease claim is an addressless lead. No street address, parcel, Purdue GIS FID, or building footprint can be assigned from it. A current address from Sigma Eta's property contact, the house corporation, SAM Property Management, or Purdue FSCL—and a current parcel/footprint match—remains necessary.

**Research limit:** The evidence makes a chapter facility plausible but does not prove its current occupancy or location. The absence of a published address in these sources does not establish that Sigma Eta lacks a house.
