Located On Campus

Crosswalk Commons is conveviently positioned on Purdue's campus! Located in the Tower Acres loop, Crosswalk Commons is a short walk or bus ride to anywhere on campus! View Map>

A New Suite Style Construction

Crosswalk Commons is a unique concept in the West Lafayette housing market. With layouts featuring one, two and four bedroom suite floor plans, as a Purdue student or scholar you will find comfortably sized suites that meet the needs of your budget.

Safe & Convenient

The suites are accessed from the interior hallways secured by control entry doors. Conveniently located coin-operated laundry facilities can be found on each floor. The apartment building includes off street parking on its large, paved parking lot next to the building.

Reserved Parking

Hurry and secure your lease today so you don't miss getting an off-street parking place in the largest, paved parking lot next to the building.

Pay Online

Save time and don't worry about late fees by missing the 1st of the month due date as well as saving paper by paying your rent online by direct debit or credit card.

Internet & Utilities

A fixed monthly utility fee insures you that you do not need to undergo the difficulty of utility deposits or service connection. Also, this ensures that there are no surprises with variable utility costs.
