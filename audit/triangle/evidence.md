## Geometry interpretation and uncertainty

The massing uses the full Purdue FID 777 GIS ring, rather than the old fallback cuboid. Its source fields report `NUM_LEVELS=1`, `CountOfFLO=1`, `Extrution=12`, and `Elevation=614`; the units/datum and semantic meaning of the vertical fields are undocumented. The official chapter photograph visibly presents multiple levels, so the one-level attribute conflicts with the facade evidence. The estimated wall height is 10.2 m with a 0.45 m parapet (10.65 m total); this is a visual massing estimate, not a measured elevation. The apparent entry on the west/University Street side is an address/map/photo inference. The exterior photo is not orthorectified; the locations and count of individual windows, entry width, terrace depth, roof material, exact story heights, and facade composition outside the photographed sides remain estimated or unverified.

No campus origin, placement marker, manifest values, or master Blender file were changed. The asset remains a simple massing in campus-world coordinates. `build_massing_triangle_fraternity_8477ad9.py` regenerates its dedicated library; `build_result.json`, Blender reopen validation, preview renders, and independent grade are recorded here after the model is rebuilt and reviewed.
