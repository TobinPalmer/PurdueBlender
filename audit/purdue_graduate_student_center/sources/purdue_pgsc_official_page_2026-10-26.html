Purdue Graduate Student Center (PGSC)

Current Graduate Student Resources

504 Northwestern Avenue
West Lafayette, IN 47906
(765) 496-0400

The Center is home to Purdue Graduate Student Government (PGSG) and Black Graduate Student Association (BGSA) .

facility guidelines
room reservation request form

Hours of Operation

Monday through Friday
Academic School Year – 8 am to 9 pm
Summer Hours – 8 am to 5 pm (starts in May, please call PGSCenter for updates)

(Hours of Operation are subject to change due to holidays & university breaks. For updates please call: 765-496-0400)

Jill Spencer, Senior Administrative Assistant

If you need any information about campus or Greater Lafayette, Jill Spencer at the lobby desk at PGSC, is a great resource! She has lived in the area for over 45 years. Feel free to stop in and see her or contact her at 765-496-0400.

As the Building Deputy, she also schedules space reservations within the Center. If you wish to use the Center for any specific time or any size group, please make a room reservation.

Building Uses

We would like for grad students to please make the PGSC your home on campus! It is located in the heart of campus and is a welcoming place with many features. We welcome any suggestions you may have to make the Center your place, a better one!

Room 105 A & B

The large multi-purpose conference room is for events, group or individual study space. The entire room can accommodate a group of up to 60 people, or it can be used with the wall separator to hold up to 30 on side A and 18 on side B. The room is equipped with two overhead projectors with computer stations. You are welcome to re-arrange the tables and chairs, as long as the room is returned to its original set up and all areas are wiped down with the provided cleaner and sanitizer.

The Lounge

This area is equipped with study tables, high-quality printer, computer station, large screen TV to watch your favorite shows, catch up on the news or play Xbox (which can be checked out from the front desk). We also have a piano keyboard with headsets. There are couches and comfy chairs for you have a relaxing meal or sit and talk with friends!

The Kitchen

The kitchen is fully equipped with drink and snack vending machines, microwave, large refrigerator/freezer to store your lunch/dinner. We require every person to please put their name and the date on your items being stored. Also please read the rules posted. We also have a coffee pot and/or coffee urns available for large meetings. We require for each person to please clean the areas you use.

Basement Study Area

A secluded, spacious area with WIFI, study tables and an area to hold group meetings.

Room 108

This room is perfect for groups and is equipped with a large board room table, white board. This room will hold up to 12 people, plus has a computer station with large TV monitor.

Room 112

If you or a group of up to 4 people need a place that is quiet and professional. We have dedicated this room for private meetings and/or interviews. We allow each person to schedule 60-90-minute sessions. (one per day)
