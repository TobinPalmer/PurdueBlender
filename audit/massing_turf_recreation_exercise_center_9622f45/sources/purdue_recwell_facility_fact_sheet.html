France A. Córdova Recreational Sports Center & Turf Recreation Exercise Center Fact Sheet

Opened:  October 2012
This project was an expansion/renovation of the existing facility that was opened in 1957, expanded in 1981 and 2000.

Project Size:  

TREC: 29,037 sq. ft.
CREC: 434,550 sq. ft.
Total:  470,760 sq. ft.

Project Cost:     $98 Million

Architect:          Moody Nolan Ltd., Inc.

View RecWell hours and additional information.

Facility Facts

Facility Awards

  • Athletic Business - 2014
    • Facility of Merit Award 
  • NIRSA Outstanding Sports Facility - 2014
  • LEED Gold - 2014
  • AS&U Educational Interiors Showcase - 2014
    • Recognized for educational interior design excellence in American School & University
  • AIA Ohio - 2014
    • Excellence in Architectural Design
  • Facility Design Award of Excellence - 2015
    • Association of College Unions International
  • AIA Indiana Merit Award - 2015
    • Expansion & Renovation, New Construction, Project Cost Greater Than $1 Million
  • The European Centre for Architecture Art Design and Urban Studies - 2015
    • International Architecture Award

Gymnasiums/Courts

Feature Gym: One basketball/volleyball court with high end acoustics and AV. Includes a special event entry. (6,656 sq. ft.)

Gold & Black Gym: 6 courts for basketball or volleyball.

  • Gold & Black Gym 1-3 includes separate special event entry (18,123 sq. ft.)
  • Gold & Black Gym 4-6 (18,225 sq. ft.)

Multi-Activity Court: 3 courts for badminton, volleyball and pickleball.  Can be configured up to 9 individual courts. (15,055 sq. ft.)

Upper Gym: 3 courts for basketball, volleyball or badminton / pickleball. (17,707 sq. ft.)

 

Total: 75,766 sq. ft.

Multipurpose Rooms

F45 Studio: The F stands for functional training, a mix of circuit and HIIT style workouts geared towards everyday movement. 45 is the total amount of time for sweat-dripping, heart-pumping fun. (1,929 sq. ft.)

MP 2: Best for dance and reservations (1,939 sq. ft.)

MP 3: Best for stretching (1,494 sq. ft.)

MP 4: Best for martial arts and table tennis (1,130 sq. ft.)

MP 5: Best for group exercise (5,103 sq. ft.)

MP 6: Best for group exercise, yoga and pilates (1,702 sq. ft.)

MP 7: Best for group exercise, yoga and pilates (1,737 sq. ft.)

Total: 15,034 sq. ft.

Fitness/Cardio/Strength Neighborhoods

Colby Fitness: Strength (pin-loaded, free weights and cables) and cardio (16,395 sq. ft.)

Personal Training Studio: Private personal training area (1600 sq. ft.)

East Fitness: Free weights, plate loaded, olympic platforms and squat racks (7,800 sq. ft.)

Scifres Functional Fitness: User defined fitness area (battle rope, TRX, free weights, cables and slam wall (8,250 sq. ft.)

Olympic Lifting Room: 8 platforms (798 sq. ft.)

Equipment Maintenance: In-house maintenance shop (651 sq. ft.)

Fitness Loft: Cardio and strength (cables) (2,565 sq. ft.)

Fitness Pavilion: Cardio and strength (cables, pin-loaded and free weights) (4,737 sq. ft.)

Upper Fitness: Cardio and strength (cables and free weights) (2,621 sq. ft.)

Cycling Studio: 30 spin bikes and instructor platform (1,424 sq. ft.)

Faculty/Staff Fitness: Cardio and strength (free Weights, pin-loaded and cables) (1,866 sq. ft.)

Lower Gym: Strength (cables, free weights and olympic platforms) and turf (14,000 sq. ft.)

Total: 62,707 sq. ft.

Aquatic Facilities

Morgan J. Burke Aquatic Center: This facility is accessed through the CoRec entry control and is managed by Recreation and Wellness.

  • 8 lane 50 meter Olympic Pool (20 – 25 yard lap lanes), Diving Pool with 4 springboards and 5 platforms, spa, spectator seating and mezzanine (39,831 sq. ft.)
  • Offices, lobby, meet management, lounge and classroom (4,000 sq. ft.)
  • Locker rooms (2,939 sq. ft.)
Recreation Pool: Myrtha Pool system, with padded floor, 25 person spa, classroom, lifeguard room, outdoor sundeck, 3 lap lanes, vortex, bubble bench, teaching ledge and wet classroom. (9,143 sq. ft.)

Climbing

Climbing Wall: 55 ft. high rope wall (3,300 sq. ft. of climbing surface)

Bouldering Wall: 55 ft. long x 12 ft. high (900 sq. ft. of climbing surface)

Indoor Tracks

Atrium Track: 3 lanes, 9.5 laps per mile (5,500 sq. ft.)

Upper Track: 3 lanes, 10.25 laps per mile (3,000 sq. ft.)
