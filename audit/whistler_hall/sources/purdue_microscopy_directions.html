Directions

The Purdue Electron Microscopy Center (PEMC) has microscopes, staff, and equipment in three buildings across Purdue's campus.

Addresses:

Whistler Hall (S042-S062 Suite)
170 S. University Street
West Lafayette, IN 47907
Phone: 756-494-6666
We are located in the sub-basement...look for the sign on the main door.

Birck Nanotechnology Center
1205 Mitch Daniels Blvd.
West Lafayette, IN 47907
Mitch Daniels Blvd. was renamed in honor of past-president Mitch Daniels. Depending on the navigation app you use, you may also find the Birck Nanotechnology Center on "1205 W State St." if the road name has not been updated on the app. 

Neil Armstrong Hall of Engineering (B200 Suite)
701 West Stadium Ave. 
West Lafayette, IN 47907

Parking:

Purdue has an online campus map which can be found here. In the top Menu, select the Parking Map tab to find the nearest parking.  Note that some street parking and parking garages require a parking permit. You can get a permit online at the parking office web page. Download pdf map.
