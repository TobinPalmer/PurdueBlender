# Neil Armstrong Hall — R12.8 source review

## New source search

- The [Purdue Emergency Preparedness building plan](https://www.purdue.edu/operations/emergency-preparedness/arms/) identifies a four-level facility (lower level, grade, and two upper floors). It is useful for floor count and program confirmation, not exterior dimensions.
- [Design and Build with Metal's project case history](https://designandbuildwithmetal.com/featured-projects/neil-armstrong-hall-of-engineering) identifies RATIO as architect and describes the distinctive wing-like cantilever roof, red ACM, and relationship to the brick/limestone facade. It confirms the architectural intent, but supplies no dimensioned plan/elevation.
- [Kawneer’s project page](https://www.kawneer.ca/projects/education/neil-armstrong-hall-of-engineering-purdue-university/) credits Purdue Architecture Department photography and identifies the 1600UT curtain wall, Versoleil sunshade/outrigger system, and Trifab framing. It provides facade-system context and visual project photographs, not measured geometry.
- [FRP Engineering’s project page](https://frpinc.com/projects/neil-armstrong-hall-of-engineering/) describes the three-story brick/glass building, cantilevered steel roof truss, and atrium bridges. It is corroborating project context, not a dimensioned drawing source.
- Purdue Archives' [Armstrong papers finding aid](https://collections.lib.purdue.edu/fa/pdf/msa5_armstrong_n.pdf) mentions “development plans” dated September 2004 in a record for Neil Armstrong's personal papers. The catalog record does not expose architectural working drawings or measurable building elevations, so it does not resolve the wall footprint or roof height.

## Geometry limits

The local 2023 Indiana orthophoto still supports the visible roof/eaves plan only. It does not prove the structural wall shell or courtyard edge. The available geotagged 2016 photographs support the roof’s wing-like folded character and facade material segmentation, but their headings/roll are not calibrated and they cannot register exact wall stationing. No reliable public dimensioned plan/elevation was located in this search. The 21.5 m peak remains a photo-based inference bounded by a coarse LiDAR ceiling, not a measured roof elevation.

## R12.8 changes

- Removed six exact overlapping faces from the restored low entrance awning and fifteen exact duplicate roof-shell faces at ring seams. One copy of each surface remains; no objects were added or removed.
- Reduced the inferred high shell thickness from 0.30 m to 0.18 m while preserving the traced roof/eaves XY and existing inferred height profile.
- Assigned a dedicated muted red-brown ACM material to the high canopy's underside. The restored lobby awning keeps its original finish, and the separate dark notch remains unchanged.
- Rendered fresh front/courtyard, geotag-inspired oblique, four elevation, and registered top views in `renders_independent_r12_8/` after reopening the saved asset.

These changes correct visible duplicate geometry and the blue-gray canopy soffit; they do not establish exact structural wall positions or roof dimensions.
