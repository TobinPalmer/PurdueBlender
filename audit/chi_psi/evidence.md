# Chi Psi — Epsilon Tau Lodge site and asset evidence

**Research/build date:** 2026-10-27  
**Disposition:** A real Chi Psi lodge is documented at 950 David Ross Road by Chi Psi's own address-specific newsletter. The Purdue GIS address and parcel identify the site, but GIS FID 748 carries a conflicting `Zeta Psi` label and its polygon is a poor structural-footprint fit. The asset therefore references FID 748 only for site/address context and uses Indiana's 2018 LiDAR building outline for its shell. Current exclusive occupancy and a current street-level image comparison remain unverified. The geometry is ready for independent review with those limits visible.

## Identity and address evidence

1. **Chi Psi national active chapter list.** Chi Psi's current [Where We Are page](https://chipsi.org/where-we-are/) lists `Epsilon Tau | Purdue University | Founded 2023 | Active since 2023`. Raw page captured in `sources/chi_psi_national_chapters_current_2026-10-26.html` (retrieved 2026-10-26). This verifies current chapter identity, not a building location.
2. **Current local chapter page.** The [Purdue Chi Psi site](https://purduechipsi.com/) currently has a “Life at the Lodge” section and identifies Alpha Epsilon Tau at Purdue. Its gallery says no photos have been supplied; the page gives no street address. Raw page and JS bundle captured 2026-10-27 in `sources/purdue_chi_psi_local_site_home_2026-10-27.html` and `sources/purdue_chi_psi_local_site_bundle_2026-10-27.js`.
3. **Address-specific first-party lodge source.** Chi Psi's [Summer 2024 alumni newsletter PDF](https://chipsi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Chi-Psi-Alumni-Newsletter-Summer-2024-1.pdf), page 3, labels the pictured red-brick, two-storey building **“The new Chi Psi Lodge at Purdue University,”** lists “Epsilon Tau Lodge: 950 David Ross Rd., West Lafayette, IN 47906,” and advertises a Lodge Opening Ceremony on September 14, 2024. The PDF is preserved as `sources/chipsi_alumni_newsletter_summer_2024.pdf`; its page-3 image is `sources/chipsi_newsletter_p3_preview.png`. The issue is titled Summer 2024 and its event copy is framed as a future opening, although the PDF is hosted in a `/2025/07/` path; the exact publication/upload date is therefore not treated as established. This is strong evidence of the chapter's claimed lodge/site in 2024, not a later dated proof of exclusive occupancy.

## GIS/site crosswalk and footprint method

- **Purdue BuildingShapes FID 748.** Official [Purdue ArcGIS FeatureServer query](https://services1.arcgis.com/mLNdQKiKsj5Z5YMN/arcgis/rest/services/BuildingShapesZip4/FeatureServer/0/query?where=FID%3D748&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=pjson), retrieved 2026-10-27, output CRS EPSG:4326. Preserved in `sources/purdue_gis_FID748_2026-10-27.json`; layer metadata in `sources/purdue_gis_buildings_metadata_2026-10-26.json`. It reports `FID=748`, `BUILDING_N=Zeta Psi`, `BLDG_ABBR=SOZY`, `OWNER=GREEK`, and `ADDRESS=950 David Ross Rd`. This is an address/site match, but the source's chapter label conflicts with Chi Psi's newer house-specific source. The service's `Shape__Area=1177.921875` (metadata advertises metres) differs materially from the 681.35 m² area obtained by applying this project's local approximation to the returned ring. Do not use the GIS area field as a measured footprint area.
- **Indiana 2018 LiDAR outline.** Indiana Geographic Information Office [hosted Building Footprints query](https://gisdata.in.gov/server/rest/services/Hosted/Building_Footprints/FeatureServer/0/query?geometry=-86.924604%2C40.434486&geometryType=esriGeometryPoint&inSR=4326&spatialRel=esriSpatialRelIntersects&outFields=%2A&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=pjson), retrieved 2026-10-26, output CRS EPSG:4326, returns footprint `OBJECTID=409718`, acquisition/year 2018. Raw source snapshot is `sources/indiana_lidar_footprints_near_fid748_2026-10-26.json`. The transformed quadrilateral has approximate dimensions **24.869 × 19.014 m**, area **472.843 m²**, and center **[-923.833154, 794.711836] m** under the project's approximate local conversion. The original source's `SHAPE__Area=817.451` differs from that transformed-ring area; spatial geometry, not the source area attribute, is used.
- **Parcel.** Tippecanoe County parcel feature `OBJECTID=5926645` names `950 DAVID ROSS RD`, parcel `79-06-13-476-005.000-029`; captured in `sources/tippecanoe_parcel_at_fid748_2026-10-27.json`. The parcel supports the address/site, not chapter occupancy.
- **Explicit comparison decision.** At this site, the FID 748 ring has approximate transformed area 681.355 m² and is centered about **7.85 m** from the LiDAR polygon center. The existing massing candidate at this address was compared against both source geometries in the prior site review (`audit/zeta_psi/footprint_comparison.json`): candidate/FID 748 IoU **0.422**, versus candidate/2018 LiDAR IoU **0.829**; the candidate covers 91.4% of the LiDAR outline and centers are 1.61 m apart. The detailed asset therefore uses the independently derived LiDAR outline for structural walls and the Purdue FID only as a site/address reference. This does not prove that the GIS label is current or that the whole GIS polygon is the lodge wall footprint.
- **Coordinate method.** Source rings are EPSG:4326. Local XY follows `X=(longitude+86.9137)*111412.84*cos(radians(40.4274))`; `Y=(latitude-40.4274)*111040`. Origin is the center of opposite corners of the LiDAR rectangle; the local X/Y basis follows that polygon's orientation. This is the repository's approximate local transform, not a surveyed projected CRS. Proposed placement origin for integration: `[-923.8331544758521, 794.7118364890861]` metres; keep the asset at local origin with no additional rotation.

## Geometry choices

- The official Chi Psi photo supports a warm red-brick, two-storey facade, two front gables with paired tall arched window features, a left-of-center raised entry, repeated sash openings, and a dark roof. These motifs are represented with reusable named objects and a procedural masonry material plus explicit mortar courses.
- Exact current facade orientation, window locations/dimensions, door coordinate, roof pitch, eave/peak heights, floor heights, trim profiles, and colors are not supplied by a measured architectural plan. They are visual estimates from the chapter's 2024 frontal photo; height is **not** asserted as verified by GIS. Purdue's GIS `NUM_LEVELS=1`/`Extrution=12` is inconsistent with the photo and remains a source conflict.
- Interior plan evidence was not located. The asset contains 11 objects in a nested `Schematic_Interior_chi_psi` collection (floor plates, partitions and shared-room furniture cues); every item carries a `representation` property identifying it as schematic. These are not a factual room plan.
- A current Google Maps/Street View image could not be retrieved through the available browser/search interface for this run. The current local chapter page has no photographs. The address and roofline crosswalk is consequently grounded in the first-party 2024 Lodge photo plus the exact GIS/parcel address and 2018 footprint, not a newly dated Street View comparison. Do not claim current imagery/roof-condition verification.
- Address conflict remains material: a Purdue-linked Zeta Psi profile capture also self-reports 950 David Ross, while GIS FID 748 labels that address Zeta Psi. The official Chi Psi opening source is the most specific house/location evidence found for Chi Psi; this asset models the physical lodge documented by Chi Psi but makes **no exclusive-occupancy claim**. Parent coordinator should resolve possible shared use or stale chapter/GIS records before any organization crosswalk/assignment change in shared indexes.

## Asset / portability

- Detailed file: `modular/buildings/chi_psi.blend`
- Builder: `modular/buildings/build_chi_psi.py`
- Scene/collection: `Building_chi_psi` / `Asset_chi_psi`
- Preview-only camera, lights, and ground are isolated in `Preview_Only_Render_Rig`, not exported in `Asset_chi_psi`.
- Exterior and clearly marked schematic interior are in the export collection; the exact expected object and mesh counts are stored in `fresh_open_verification.json`.
- No external image files or absolute texture paths are required; all materials are procedural/node-based.
- Front render: `chi_psi_front_render.png`; the fresh-open render is separately saved as `chi_psi_fresh_open.png`.

## Sources not used to assert building identity

Online property/listing aggregators, third-party fraternity directories, and event venue reposts were not treated as primary identity evidence. No individual was contacted. No README, manifest, shared index, master blend, or other building was edited; no commit/push was made.
