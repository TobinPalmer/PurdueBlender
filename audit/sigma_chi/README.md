## Geometry and limitations

The structural wall shell follows the complete FID 828 GIS ring in campus coordinates. It was decomposed visually into two gable wings that meet at the source polygon's re-entrant corner, keeping the L-shaped notch clear. The roof uses two main gable wings with three additional intersecting cross-gable bays to break the long slope. The principal elevation includes a gabled entry porch with timber knee braces, an extruded stone arch at the entry, an arched gable opening, irregular paired window groups, a wider mullioned window group, stone surrounds, dormers, and chimneys as source-informed visual cues. Their exact dimensions and locations are interpreted from perspective photos. Heavy half-timbering was omitted because the available photos do not establish it. There is no independent current survey, elevation drawing, point cloud, or measured height source in this evidence set.

The asset uses 8.8 m eaves and a 13.1 m primary roof ridge as estimates; the modeled chimney tops reach 16.96 m. None are measurements. GIS `Extrution=12` is undocumented and is not used as a measured height. The official GIS attributes conflict on storey count (`NUM_LEVELS=1`, `CountOfFLO=1`), and the model's three facade courses must not be called a verified floor count. The 2007 chapter report of 19,000 sq. ft. of added living space is not used to infer current exterior height or footprint.
