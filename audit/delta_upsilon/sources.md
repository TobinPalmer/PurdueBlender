## Model decisions and limits

- Verified source match: Purdue GIS FID 751 explicitly names Delta Upsilon and records address `1300 Stadium`; the chapter contact page lists the matching street address. The GIS outline's location and shape are close to OSM way 301841256. The FID-to-house identity is therefore strong.
- The wall plan follows the exact official FID 751 ring after the project geographic approximation, centered at project coordinates `(-1057.421769, 496.563548)` m. The exported library is now local-coordinate geometry: subtract that XY origin from each projected GIS vertex. The coordinator must place `Placement_massing_house_301841256_1cc7641` at that origin when integrating.
- The 2023 photo supports facade materials and broad details. Number and spacing of windows on sides not visible in the photograph are inferred from the official outline and generalized regular bays.
- Total height 11.58 m is an estimate derived from the GIS field `Extrution=38`, interpreted as feet. The GIS record does not document units for this field, and height, story heights, window dimensions, and roof pitch are **estimated**, not measured.
- The roof was modeled as a low complex hip roof, guided by the current aerial view. Exact roof sections and ridge heights are unverified.
- No reliable floor plan was obtained. There is no interior model; this avoids inventing room layouts.
- A small entry stair and freestanding monument sign are modeled. The 2023 photo supports the sign design; exact sign placement relative to the footprint is estimated from the photo/aerial, not surveyed.
- The official attribute area/perimeter appear consistent with Web Mercator units after latitude scale correction; the local comparison uses the project approximation and raw WGS84 polygon. This is not a licensed land survey or a structural-wall survey.
