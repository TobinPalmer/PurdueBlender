Wang Hall dedication

From left, West Lafayette Mayor John Dennis; Ragu Balakrishnan, Head of the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering; Leah Jamieson, Dean of the College of Engineering; Lucy and Patrick Wang; and Purdue President Mitch Daniels
Seng-Liang Wang
Seng-Liang Wang Hall
Seng-Liang Wang Hall, about 40% of which will house ECE faculty and research facilities, was dedicated on September 19, 2014. The $38.9 million facility is leased by PRF to the university and private businesses. The four-story, 147,000-square-foot building is located at 516 Northwestern Ave., across from the Purdue Mall and next to the Northwestern Avenue parking garage.
