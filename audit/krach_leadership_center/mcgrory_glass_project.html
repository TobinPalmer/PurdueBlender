Purdue University

Translucent etched laminated glass railings by McGrory Glass for the Krach Leadership Center at Purdue University.

Overview

The Krach Leadership Center at Purdue University in West Lafayette, IN, utilizes architectural glass to support an open, collaborative student environment. McGrory Glass fabricated the translucent etched laminated glass railings found throughout the main lobby and adjacent areas. The etched surface provides light diffusion and visual privacy for occupants on multi-level walkways while ensuring the structural integrity required for high-traffic educational facilities. This project highlights the skilled fabrication and extensive production capabilities required to integrate specialized architectural glass into a modern university environment. All solutions were fabricated in-house at our 250,000-square-foot facility.
