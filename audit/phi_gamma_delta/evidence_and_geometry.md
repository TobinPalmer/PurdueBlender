## Geometry edits

- Kept the stable scene `Building_massing_house_301837281_58ead22`, collection `Asset_massing_house_301837281_58ead22`, and root object `House_301837281`.
- Kept the existing 10-vertex GIS-derived XY outline unchanged. The outline in the asset has a plan bounding box of approximately 25.324 m east-west by 25.939 m north-south. These dimensions describe the source polygon/model outline, not a verified exterior wall survey.
- Replaced the 16 m flat-topped extrusion with a lower first-floor base following the full outline (4.25 m), upper west and south arms of the original house (to 10.35 m eaves), and three separate shallow hip roofs with an estimated 12.0 m tallest ridge. The upper volumes and roofs follow the GIS outline's open east-side inset instead of spanning it. The northeast arm is treated as the lower addition. This split and the roof/story heights are visual estimates from public photos and the official render; the GIS height attributes were not adopted as as-built truth.
- Added UV-mapped buff-brick texture with real-scale coursing, mortar relief, and roughness variation; added a procedural slate grain/roughness/bump material. Both are embedded node materials with no external images. Added asymmetric window groupings, stone surrounds/sills, segmented arched entry surrounds, corner trim, story bands, gutters/downspouts, and prominent round-headed glazing on the lower wing.
- Added low Lyles Addition massing within the GIS outline, preserving the open east-side inset. The GIS row does not identify which arm is original versus the addition, so the northeast-lower interpretation remains explicitly estimated from the official render and aerial.
- Closed the roof shells and converted the door/addition arch surrounds to solid voussoir blocks. Every component mesh is now watertight at the mesh-edge level; the fresh-open topology probe reports 0 boundary, nonmanifold, or wire edges across 2,769 edges.
- Did not add interior geometry. The official plan documents first-floor rooms, but it is a presentation drawing without a georeferenced anchor to the GIS outline. Its rotated sheet orientation, shaded existing-house footprint, and separate addition/other campus masses do not uniquely determine which GIS arm receives each room. A guessed registration would risk moving walls through the wrong wings. The drawing also omits structural upper floors; the basement plan is separately shown and not a basis for above-grade interior. Therefore the room plan remains evidence only, not modeled geometry.

## Uncertainty and remaining verification

The building identity is well supported by the named GIS feature and the official 640 Russell dedication documentation. Position/outline was preserved from that GIS-derived library geometry; no independent surveyed wall-footprint comparison was completed. Story count, buff brick, roof silhouette and asymmetric facade character are supported by direct construction photography, but exact dimensions, opening positions, roof pitch, addition partition, and hidden elevations remain estimated. The aerial's capture date is unknown; plan and concept renderings are official but do not prove final as-built dimensions. Fresh-open and closed-mesh topology results are recorded in `fresh_open_validation.json` and `topology_probe.json`; both are tied to the exact SHA in those records.
