## Geometry and uncertainty

The footprint follows Purdue feature 775 using the documented local approximation: `X=(lon+86.9137)*111412.84*cos(40.4274°)`, `Y=(lat-40.4274)*111040`, Z up, meters. This is not a surveyed projected CRS. The saved-file validation compares all 14 wall-footprint vertices with a maximum error of about 0.000016 m. GIS source ground elevation 625 has no documented vertical datum and is not applied.

The shell now represents two visible stories at 6.2 m estimated wall height, with an illustrative 0.4 m roof cap and a centered portico gable reaching about 7.62 m. Height, floor spacing, roof pitch/profile, portico dimensions, exact window count/spacing, and materials are estimates from an undated panorama, not measured elevations. The GIS one-level field conflicts with the visual two-story facade; its meaning is not resolved. The LiDAR outline independently checks broad footprint position and scale but disagrees on edge detail. No measured elevation or roof plan was found. Current facade condition cannot be confirmed from the panorama because its image capture date is not exposed.
