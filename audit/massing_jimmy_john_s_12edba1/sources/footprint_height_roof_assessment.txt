Jimmy John's footprint, height, and roof cross-check (retrieved 2026-10-26)

Independent footprint candidate
Indiana's statewide "Indiana Building Footprints 2016-2020" ArcGIS layer returned a polygon at the OSM building center. Feature objectid 443017 reports county Tippecanoe and lidar year 2018. The raw JSON response is saved as indiana_lidar_footprint_2016_2020.json. The layer metadata describes a statewide LiDAR-derived building-footprint product; the metadata says the layer derives from the 2016-2020 USGS 3DEP collection.

Using the returned WGS84 ring and the repository's documented campus-local approximation gives approximately 258.65 m2 / 2,784 sq ft and bounds 16.85 m by 21.03 m. The OSM ring gives approximately 209.25 m2 / 2,252 sq ft and bounds 14.71 m by 19.32 m. The independent polygon occupies the same building location and has a similar rectangular orientation, but is about 24% larger than OSM. LoopNet's secondary property record reports 1,908 sq ft; its measurement basis and date are unspecified, and floor area need not equal a footprint polygon. These three area figures do not establish the exact current wall boundary.

The model therefore retains the existing OSM ring and coordinates, while recording the 2018 Indiana polygon as independent corroborating evidence of a building footprint at the site. Purdue's campus BuildingShapes audit has no matching feature. The Indiana polygon is not treated as a cadastral survey or as proof that its outline is the exact current wall line.

Height and roof
The saved RoadsideArchitecture historical photo (photo URL in reference_index.json) visibly supports a single-story building, broad deep eaves, and a shallow roof silhouette. Its date is not stated. No measured building elevation, permit drawing, or surveyed height was found. The modeled 4.0-5.0 m roof-edge range, one-plane roof choice, 1.0 m rise over approximately 14.7 m (about 3.9 degrees), slope direction, and 0.5-0.63 m eaves are explicitly schematic visual estimates. Only the one-story count and broad shallow/deep-eave appearance are treated as photo-supported. The precise roof plane, pitch, direction, edge heights, and current condition remain unverified.
