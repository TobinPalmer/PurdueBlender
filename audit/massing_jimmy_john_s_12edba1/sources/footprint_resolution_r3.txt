Jimmy John's footprint and image evidence, r3 research (retrieved 2026-10-26)

Primary footprint choice
The building's exterior massing outline now uses Indiana Building Footprints 2016-2020, feature objectid 443017, Tippecanoe County, lidar year 2018. The exact returned WGS84 ring is preserved and projected with the same local campus transform used by this repository. It has a ground-projected area of 258.65 m2 / 2,784 sq ft and dimensions about 16.85 by 21.03 m. The source ArcGIS SHAPE__Area is 447.075 m2 in its Web Mercator map units; this is recorded separately and is not treated as ground area.

The polygons were overlaid on the Indiana Geographic Information Office's 2018 orthoimagery service. The saved overlay is renders/footprint_overlay_indiana_2018.png; the underlying service export is renders/indiana_2018_ortho.png. The LiDAR polygon and OSM way 116427223 describe the same rectangular roof at the same address. OSM's 209.25 m2 / 2,252 sq ft polygon is almost entirely nested inside the Indiana polygon (80.9% of the state polygon area; essentially all OSM area lies inside). In the 2018 image the OSM trace is nearer the visible roof edge on parts of the outline; the larger state trace extends roughly 0.5-1.2 m beyond that edge in places. The Indiana 2018 image has source resolution around 15 cm/pixel; county metadata reports its recent county orthophotography is within +/-2.5 ft horizontally 90% of the time. This offset is too small to tell confidently whether the difference is source alignment, a roof overhang, or an outline-generation convention. The state LiDAR ring is selected as the one primary exterior massing footprint because it is the independent, dated state product and its overall roof outline is consistent with the imagery. The OSM polygon is retained as identity/location corroboration only, not averaged with the state ring.

Property area reconciliation
The LoopNet/public-record summary for parcel 79-07-19-429-019.000-026 reports 1,908 sq ft. A second public-record aggregator labels that same 1,908 sq ft as gross building area, first-floor area, and one story; it also reports land area 18,317 sq ft (0.421 acres), built 1960, effective year 1982. The 1,908 figure is therefore best interpreted as a reported floor-area value, not parcel area. This is a source-based inference only: the official assessor's public Beacon portal is the county's linked source, but its page did not become accessible in this research session (the browser stopped at a Cloudflare "Just a moment" challenge and direct server retrieval returned 403). No attempt was made to bypass that barrier, and no official property card confirming 1,908 sq ft was available. The 1,908 floor-area figure, 2,252 sq ft OSM polygon, and 2,784 sq ft state exterior polygon need not measure the same surface. No cadastral/building survey or original architectural plan was found that resolves exact wall area.

County record and identity check
Tippecanoe County BZA minutes in the August 23, 2017 agenda packet record case BZA-1963 for Jimmy John's Enterprises LLC at 311 W State Street. The minutes describe an existing building, an existing freestanding pole sign, site renovations, new glass, a new roof, interior improvements, and a new patio. They report a 36-inch sign as allowed and a 54-inch proposed sign; the variance was denied (0 yes, 6 no). The text does not give surveyed wall dimensions or say the building footprint was expanded. The agenda minutes say photos and aerials were shown, but the public packet contains no submitted architectural plans or the displayed photographs. The exact current roof/wall line therefore remains unresolved.

Historic-photo measurements and model treatment
The RoadsideArchitecture photo file is 1512 by 554 px. Approximate visible measurements from that frame: the long glass facade run is 1050 +/- 80 px; the round projecting sign is 45 +/- 5 px across; the long facade shows about 15-17 narrow mullion bays, and the foreshortened return shows about 4-6 larger glass sections. If the pictured sign is the 36-inch sign referenced in the 2017 BZA case, the photo ratio suggests a long-facade length near 21 +/- 3 m. That is a rough perspective-limited cross-check against the state polygon's 21.03 m long dimension, not a survey. Approximate roof eave depth is 0.5-0.8 of the sign diameter (about 0.46-0.73 m if that sign identification is right). The image supports one story, continuous storefront glazing, a shallow roof plane, deep eaves, and a projecting circular sign. It does not supply calibrated height or roof slope: the modeled 4.0-5.0 m roof-edge height and 1.0 m roof rise are estimates only; high/low direction and exact pitch remain unverified. The source image date is unstated.
