Purdue University

Bindley Bioscience Engineering Center

Fostering collaboration

As one of three initial buildings that established Purdue's Discovery Park, the Bindley Bioscience Center serves as a functional anchor of this thriving science community – a place where complex problems are solved with innovative science, engineering, and technology advances that push past traditional boundaries. This biomedical engineering facility is a catalyst for researchers in Purdue's life sciences and engineering disciplines, allowing them to collaborate on new discoveries addressing the most complex, global biological and medical challenges.

With a significant building addition housing the Multidisciplinary Cancer Research Facility, the center supports these collaborative endeavors with modular laboratory space that accommodates the quickly shifting nature of multidisciplinary, team-based research. Well known for the unique variety of scientific investigation it hosts, the center's specialized labs and equipment are attractive to state, regional, national, and international industry partners, revealing opportunities that can truly impact our world with new and transformative solutions.
