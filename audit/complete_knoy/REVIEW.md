## Round 8 — Indiana 3DEP roof-surface check (2026-10-24)

Added a source-backed but deliberately approximate vertical comparison using Indiana’s 2018 Tippecanoe 3DEP surface tile `in2018_30001885_12` (5 ft cells) and the state’s 2016–2020 bare-earth DEM service (2.5 ft product). The project’s approximate GIS footprint conversion yields 1,126 valid DSM cell centers; their median surface elevation is 680.94 ft. Twenty-one bare-earth DEM point queries within the footprint have a median of 621.06 ft, giving an indicative median surface difference of about 59.9 ft. The whole-footprint DSM p10–p90 range translates to roughly 30.4–63.9 ft above the sampled bare-earth median because it combines stepped surfaces, openings, edges and rooftop objects. These values are not a surveyed roof, parapet, or penthouse height. The footprint conversion is approximate, the DTM is interpolated under the building, and the statewide QL2 accuracy standard is not a Knoy-specific assessment.

The ~59.9 ft median is close to the inherited L5 slab datum (~58.8 ft) and the 2018 Gateway study’s four 14′-8″ intervals from L1 through L4 reach about 44 ft. This makes it plausible that the L5 slab is a roof-level slab; the LiDAR cannot establish whether it is a roof deck or occupied fifth story, and the conflicting GIS 48 ft extrusion field is retained as unresolved. No roof, floor, penthouse, or facade geometry changed. The chart `knoy_roof_height_vertical_evidence_r8.svg` places the data and model levels on a common vertical scale; source measurements and all 21 terrain samples are in `roof_height_assessment_r8.json`; acquisition/tile metadata and a SHA-256 for the downloaded DSM are in `indiana_3dep_knoy_tile_metadata_r8.json`. `knoy_roof_height_vertical_evidence_r8_preview.png` is the legibility check of the SVG chart.
