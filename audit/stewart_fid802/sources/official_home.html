    WELCOME TO STEWART

    Stewart is Purdue University Christian women’s cooperative house. About 33 women share college life together, where we form bonds of friendship that last many years beyond college. We also share a special bond in Christ, and we strive to glorify Him in all that we do. It is this bond that creates sisterhood that lasts beyond a lifetime. We welcome you to discover more about our house!
