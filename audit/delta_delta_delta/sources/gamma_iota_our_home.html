Our Home

By setting a high standard for chapter housing, Tri Delta fulfills its mission to add value to the collegiate experience by ensuring safe, comfortable and competitive living environments for our members. Tri Delta homes are a part of our identity and a home away from home to many. Living in your Tri Delta chapter house is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to enjoy the comforts of home in a place where you can be yourself and belong to something bigger.

In a Tri Delta home, you'll create lifelong memories with friends who feel like family. Our homes feature safe, welcoming and inclusive spaces that foster sisterhood and belonging.
