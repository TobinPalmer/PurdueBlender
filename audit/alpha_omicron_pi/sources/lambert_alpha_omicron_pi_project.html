alpha omicron pi sorority

OWNER: Alpha Omicron Pi

ARCHITECTS: Krittenbrink Architecture, LLC.

DIMENSIONS: 19,500 sq. ft

COST: $3,702,232

New 19,500 sf three level Women's Fraternity House with a 2,800 sf basement / refuge area. The remainder of the house consisting of full operating kitchen, guest suites, library, lounge and reflection room all of which supports 73 residents. A full parking lot to accommodate the residents at the rear of the house with a full size sand volleyball court flanking the parking area.
