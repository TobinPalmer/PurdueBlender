Living in the House

Home Sweet Homicron

Phi Upsilon CHAPTER HOUSEOur Homicron

Our chapter house, lovingly referred to as our Homicron, is more than just a place to live—it's a home full of laughter, learning, and lifelong sisterhood. Whether we're gathering for meetings, sharing meals, or hosting events, this space brings us together in all the best ways!

Our facility houses 60 sisters who live in and provides plenty of shared spaces for all members to enjoy—from cozy study nooks to sunny patios and everything in between.
WELCOME HOMEA Look Inside

Just minutes from the Purdue Memorial Union, our house blends comfort, connection, and convenience. Inside, you’ll find inviting living areas, peaceful study spaces, and a fully equipped kitchen and dining room where 12 chef-prepared meals are served each week.

We’re proud to offer amenities that support every aspect of chapter life, including on-site laundry, parking, and study rooms. Our live-in House Director provides guidance and support to make sure everyone feels safe, cared for, and at home.
