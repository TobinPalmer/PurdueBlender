# Niswonger roof evidence and R3 treatment

## Sources checked

- MSKTD's project page and six architect photographs: https://msktd.com/project/purdue-university-niswonger-aviation-technology/ (local copies: `msktd_project_1.jpg` through `msktd_project_6.jpg`). These show the clerestory/connector, steel framing, white interior roof deck, glazing and adjacent brick/metal finishes. They do not show the full classroom/brick-wing roof plan.
- Purdue Board of Trustees Physical Facilities Committee, Airport HVAC/Window/Overhead Door project: https://www.purdue.edu/bot/meetings/past-meetings/2019/06.%20aug/pfc/508_Airport-HVAC-Window-Overhead-Door.pdf. This establishes an AHU replacement was in the 2019 airport project scope, but does not locate the unit or specify roof curbs, screens or the roof extents.
- Purdue ePlanroom: https://www.purdueplanroom.com/. The portal says it carries campus bid documents; no publicly retrievable Niswonger as-built roof plan or permit drawing was found in indexed results. Access to archived bid packages may require registration.
- Esri World Imagery tile service: https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer. Service metadata attributes imagery to Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community, and describes mixed-resolution imagery. Individual tile acquisition dates are not exposed through the tile endpoint. A z19 and z20 aerial mosaic around approximately 40.41638, -86.92944 is in `arcgis_world_imagery/` (`composite_z19.jpg`, `composite_z20.jpg`). It directly shows the classroom/brick wing roof footprint, articulated roof zones, Hangar 1's separate roof, and a service-equipment concentration on the eastern roof portion. The imagery is qualitative; no date, surveyed accuracy or exact registration to local model XY is asserted.

## R3 changes and limits

The existing R2 roof plate coordinates and elevations were not moved. Both plates remain approximate closures informed by modeled wall-top bounds. R3 adds a restrained charcoal-gray membrane-like finish with fine procedural surface response, narrow low-profile edge metal, and shallow linear lap-joint cues where the aerial shows repeated surface breaks. The old Hangar 1 membrane was made a darker gray to track its aerial appearance. The original sloped connector roof and hangar roof are now render-visible; these were previously hidden in render despite being modeled, which caused the earlier exterior previews to appear roofless. The edge/joint details are visual interpretations, not manufacturer-specific or measured details. No equipment layout was copied into the model because the tile imagery is not georegistered to the model's local XY frame closely enough to justify exact placements.

No claim is made that the roof plates reproduce an as-built plan. The public orthophoto was used for qualitative roof zoning and transitions, not exact dimensions. Exact roof perimeter, slope, material system and date remain unverified.
