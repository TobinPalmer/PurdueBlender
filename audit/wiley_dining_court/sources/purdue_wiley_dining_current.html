Wiley Dining Court

Wiley Dining Court offers diverse and flavorful options. Enjoy authentic Mexican dishes at the La Fonda station; or venture back to the Classics station where traditional comfort food is served ranging from burgers & fries to homemade macaroni & cheese. On your way there, you can enjoy a handcrafted slice of pizza from Mozzarella Fresca. No meal is complete without a trip to our Delectables station where you will always find something handmade and sure to hit the spot!

DIETARY RESTRICTIONS

Wiley Dining Court offers a Gluten-Friendly station at lunch and dinner. This station serves a complete meal including a protein, gluten-free starch, and vegetable. You can find starches like potatoes, rice, quinoa, and gluten-free pasta. Wiley also offers a Vegetarian station daily. For more information about managing food allergies and dietary preferences, visit our Food Allergies or Special Dietary Needs page.
