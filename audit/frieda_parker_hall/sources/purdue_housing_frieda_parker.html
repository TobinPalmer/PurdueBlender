Frieda Parker Hall is a six-story residence designed for upper-class students, offering suite and semi-suite style units with shared bathrooms, dual sinks, and furnished living spaces. Opened in Fall 2020, the hall provides a modern living experience just steps from central campus, with easy access to the Black Cultural Center, Purdue CoRec, and nearby bus stops. On the ground floor, residents enjoy convenient dining options including Jersey Mike’s and Chick-fil-A, along with close proximity to Starbucks, Third Street Market, and Wiley Dining Court. 

 

An American Legacy with Boilermaker Roots

During the early stages of the American civil rights movement and well before the landmark Brown v. Board of Education decision, sisters Winifred and Frieda Parker were leading the effort to integrate student housing on Purdue’s campus and across Indiana. On October 3, 2021, the Parker sisters were honored for their courageous leadership with the dedication of two residence halls in their name on the Purdue University campus.

Parker Halls Parliament is the Residence Hall Club of Parker Halls. All Parker Hall residents are members of this student organization. Parker Halls Parliament plans and implements events for residents, purchases materials for residents to rent from the front desk and works to create a sense of community throughout the entire hall. Our hall clubs across campus serve as a great gateway to your first leadership opportunity on campus!
