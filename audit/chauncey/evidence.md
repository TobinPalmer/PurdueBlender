## Geometry and placement method

- Project campus origin: latitude 40.4274, longitude −86.9137. Conversion follows `modular/AGENTS.md`: X = (longitude + 86.9137) × 111412.84 × cos(40.4274°); Y = (latitude − 40.4274) × 111040. This is the project's approximate local metric conversion, not a surveyed CRS.
- Polygon vertices were converted to local campus meters. The polygon centroid is **X 476.038305 m, Y −651.340747 m** from project origin. Detailed asset geometry is translated by that centroid, so the model's local origin is (0,0,0); the parent placement marker should supply the campus XY above.
- Official source wall footprint bounds in local coordinates: X [−12.191, 10.501] m; Y [−7.116, 7.773] m. The perimeter polygon is preserved as the base and upper wall ring. The main roof is an estimated envelope over that wall outline; roof overhang is not included in footprint matching.
- Current Street View and the older house photo support a two-storey light clapboard house with front-facing gable, steep dark roof, attic vent, shuttered windows, porch and masonry base. Eaves are modeled at 7.1 m and ridge at 13.3 m. These are visual estimates from unscaled imagery, not measured heights.
- Repeated side windows, exact door/window spacing, trim profiles, roof dimensions and pitch, and porch extents are interpreted from the imagery and remain estimated. The 2014 official photo appears to show a chimney, but a chimney is not clearly identifiable in the March 2026 Street View; the model omits it pending better current evidence. Exterior materials are procedural (clapboard relief, shingle mottling, stone variation, painted trim and glass); no external texture paths are required.
- Interior partitions, floor plates, common room, kitchen/dining cues, and a small number of schematic beds are illustrative only. No registered floor plan was located. The interior is not a measured 22-person room layout.
- The model does not use a nearby unverified massing candidate as a proxy. It does not model the surrounding park, landscaping, parcel, or site improvements as part of the building.

## Validation

The asset was built in Blender 5.2.3 LTS and rendered to `chauncey_preview.png`. A fresh-open Blender check is recorded in `fresh_open_validation.json`. Materials are procedural/local; the source HTML, GIS snapshot and reference photo are preserved under `sources/`. This is a source-backed first detailed model, not a surveyed or independently graded result. Master integration and marker placement remain coordinator-owned.
