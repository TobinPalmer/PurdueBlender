More About the Hi Vine Apartments

Welcome to Hi-Vine Apartments by Weida, the perfect home for Purdue University students. Located just one block away from campus at 302 Vine Street, West Lafayette, Hi-Vine offers unparalleled convenience and modern living.


What We Offer:

  • Furnished Apartments: Choose from our 1 and 2-bedroom units, fully furnished for your comfort.
  • Spacious Layouts: Enjoy bright, open floor plans with large room sizes and 9-foot ceilings.
  • Essential Appliances: Each apartment includes a stove, refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher, and an in-unit washer and dryer.
  • Prime Location: Just a short walk from Purdue University, making it easy to get to classes and campus events.
  • Convenient Shopping: Live above popular spots like  AJ’s Burgers, Hotbox Pizza, Everbowl, and Village Bottle Shoppe.
