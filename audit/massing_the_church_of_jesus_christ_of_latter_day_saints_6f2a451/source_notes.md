# LDS Institute evidence capture

All evidence refers to the Institute of Religion at 14 Waldron Street, West Lafayette, Indiana, matched to Purdue GIS FID 779 and to the assigned massing polygon. The separate Church meetinghouse at 3224 Jasper Street is a different facility and was excluded from the building-specific visual evidence.

## Source observations

- Official Purdue campus GIS snapshot: FID 779 is “Lds Institute of Religion,” address “14 WALDRON ST.”, owner “STUDENT ORGNZ”, one recorded level, and `Extrution: 12`. The numeric extrusion has no unit documentation in the snapshot and is not treated as a metric height. CRS is WGS84/EPSG:4326. The polygon was converted with the project’s approximate local transform (origin 40.4274, -86.9137; x scale 111412.84*cos(40.4274°), y scale 111040). It measures about 18 m east-west by 33 m north-south. Existing box massing base vertices match the transformed GIS polygon to about 0.98 m bidirectional nearest-vertex distance. GIS feature shape area/perimeter fields are carried as supplied; they do not agree exactly with the local approximation and are not used as dimensions.
- First-party Church location index lists both West Lafayette locations, keeping 14 Waldron separate from 3224 Jasper. The first-party Lafayette YSA Ward page gives the 14 Waldron Street address. These support identity/use and address, not geometry.
- Google Maps Street View photograph at the mapped address: panorama ID `iyVJi-tEDx_csjeK3GVwsA`, camera at approximately 40.4248225, -86.9179101 (23 Waldron St), heading 114.6046°, capture date July 2023. It faces the mapped 14 Waldron facility across the street. The downloaded 1200×800 preview shows a long, one-story red-brown brick building with a low dark roof, a glazed/recessed entrance, an access ramp and steps, landscaped frontage, and a small higher reddish roof behind the front roofline. Canopy/tree occlusion and perspective limit measurement. The image is preserved as `google_street_view_23_waldron_2023.jpg`.
- Purdue Archives UA62 Series 3 finding aid (Box 84, Folder 48.4) catalogs exterior Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints Institute of Religion photographs among religious-services images. Series 1 has related “Religious Life Cover” and “Churches” folders from 1987. The finding aids are not the images. Direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403/Cloudflare challenge from this environment; the PDFs were not bypassed. The online indexed descriptions are recorded below as source leads, not as visual evidence.

## Uncertainty

The archive images could not be obtained for visual comparison. No scaled elevation or survey height is available. Roof height, wall height, dimensions above the mapped base, fenestration counts/spacing, entrance offsets, and exact material boundaries remain estimates from the July 2023 photograph. The 2023 view appears associated with the 14 Waldron place but is taken from 23 Waldron; the target building is visible beyond the curb and its mapped pin is shown at the same parcel. Do not transfer details from the 3224 Jasper meetinghouse or generic Church imagery.

## Model interpretation

The saved asset preserves the GIS quadrilateral in campus-world coordinates and models a low main gable plus a small raised rear roof section to represent the two visible roof tones/heights. The body is 4.15 m, main ridge 5.45 m, and secondary ridge 5.90 m in the current estimate; none is a surveyed measurement. The main visible long facade has a left-of-center recessed glazed entry zone divided by brick piers, an access ramp/steps, and two small, irregularly placed window openings. The exact bay/pier layout, count, spacing, offsets, roof plan/pitch, and hidden elevations remain provisional because only one partly occluded oblique panorama was available. The roof colors express the observed dark main roof and reddish higher roof, not verified material specifications.
