# Neil Armstrong Hall — R6 review candidate

## Changes

R6 keeps the three coarse R5 LiDAR-derived wing/roof envelopes and their GIS/manifest registration. It adds a photo-supported upper glazing strip with illustrative mullions to the candidate north-side edge of the coarse envelope. The 2016 geotag is northwest of the building, but there is no camera bearing, lens calibration, control-point registration, or dimensioned elevation; therefore the strip's exact facade assignment, dimensions, and spacing remain schematic.

The FRP project page documents Mayflower Red ACM in the roof/truss expression. R6 applies a restrained red ACM material to upward faces of the highest LiDAR roof zone and harmonizes the existing building roof ACM material. The LiDAR mask supports only a broad roof-like region and height envelope. It cannot reproduce the cantilevered truss, exact roof edge, or roof slope. The existing simplified roof slabs/penthouses remain approximate and are not newly asserted as registered.

## Interior evidence and circulation

FRP describes exposed concrete bridges with glass rails at two atrium levels and three concrete stairs. R6 makes a legible isolated two-level bridge section render using the existing bridge and railing meshes. The render temporarily makes the glass opaque-tinted for legibility; this does not alter the saved source material.

This view is explicitly a feature demonstration, not a full atrium cutaway. The available source photographs and Purdue 360-tour listing do not provide a direction-registered plan. The existing bridge endpoints, the single schematic stair, full stair runs, and continuous circulation route remain unverified. No stair or floor geometry was extended to imply unsupported connections.
