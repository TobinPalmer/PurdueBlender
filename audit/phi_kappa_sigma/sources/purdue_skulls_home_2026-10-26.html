Chapter Overview

Built for brotherhood and leadership

About Our Legacy

Established as one of the oldest fraternities on campus, we uphold a tradition of excellence, camaraderie, academic success, and personal growth. With a legacy that spans generations, we take great pride in our distinguished brotherhood.

Our Community

At Skulls, we are not just a fraternity; we are a brotherhood. We embrace diversity and value the unique perspectives, talents, and aspirations of our members. Through meaningful connections and shared experiences, our members forge lifelong friendships.

Growth & Leadership

Our fraternity offers opportunities for personal and professional growth. From leadership development programs to networking events, we empower our members to become the leaders of tomorrow.

Core Values

What Defines Phi Kappa Sigma

Scholarship

Our commitment to academic excellence is foundational to our fraternity. We support our members in their pursuit of knowledge, both in and out of the classroom.

Leadership

We develop leaders through meaningful experiences, mentorship, and opportunities to take on roles that build essential skills for life beyond college.

Service & Philanthropy

We are dedicated to making a positive impact on our community. Our philanthropic endeavors embody the spirit of service that defines what it means to be a Skull.
