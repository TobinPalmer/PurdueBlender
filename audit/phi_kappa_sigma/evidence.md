# Phi Kappa Sigma (Alpha Xi) — FID 829

**Decision: confirmed match.** Official Purdue GIS feature 829 is explicitly named Phi Kappa Sigma and carries the address 234 Littleton St. The chapter's own current website identifies the Alpha Xi chapter at 234 Littleton Street. A georeferenced outline of the official GIS polygon overlays the white-roofed multi-wing house in Esri World Imagery. The name, address, and roof/footprint agree, so the former unresolved crosswalk is resolved for this asset.

## Geometry and modeling decisions

- The GIS ring was converted using the project origin and formula documented in `modular/AGENTS.md`: X = (longitude + 86.9137) × 111412.84 × cos(40.4274°); Y = (latitude − 40.4274) × 111040. Coordinates remain in campus space. The transformed footprint bounds are approximately X 875.56–912.59 m and Y −218.60 to −178.71 m. The 23.04 m southwest-facing long edge is used as the main photographed facade; its outward normal is approximately (−0.375, −0.927).
- The footprint is partitioned exactly into three adjoining GIS-ring regions: rear core (indices 0,1,2,3,4,5,12), front house (5,6,7,12), and terrace wing (7,8,9,10,11,12). Their union reproduces the source polygon without gaps or overlaps. The wall shell is emitted once, with one flat roof surface per region and a step wall only where adjoining roof elevations differ; there is no second full-height roof extrusion.
- Exterior photos show a three-storey principal facade and a lower attached rooftop terrace at its left end. The builder estimates the main facade at 10.1 m, the rear core at 9.5 m, and the terrace wing at 6.7 m (two storeys). The terrace wing receives the photographed open white balustrade; the higher roof edges receive short parapets. These elevations are visual estimates, not surveyed measurements. The source GIS's single-level/12-ft attributes conflict with the images and were not used for height.
- The principal facade has seven regular window bays on its three floor bands, with the centered ground-level entrance interrupting the lowest row. White painted masonry uses physical-meter facade UVs, a portable procedural brick/mortar pattern and bump detail; the material stays within the file and uses no external image texture. Dark sash glazing/shutters, white casings, belt courses, a centered classical entry pavilion, and steps follow the official photos. Exact opening dimensions and the precise history of the attached additions remain estimated.
- No reliable floor plan or registered interior drawings were found in the available evidence. A schematic interior is therefore omitted rather than invented.
- Street View was not used as a measurement source. The identity is established by the GIS name/address, chapter-owned address evidence, official exterior photography, and the aerial footprint overlay.
