## Higher-resolution duplicate and chronology support

[MSKTD June19,2026 case study](https://msktd.com/design-journal/entry/purdue-university-mechanical-engineering-building-renovation/) was publicly readable HTTP200 despite search snippets saying Protected. No login/access bypass was used. Saved `msktd_me_article.html` contains article text and image URLs. Publication date is June19,2026; photo capture date/photographer are unknown. The article says the final project remained within the existing building footprint, after earlier additions were considered; this does not certify every wall/opening.

`msktd_06.jpg` (2000px) and `msktd_03.jpg` are **the same exposure already saved as dossier `pepper_completed_exterior.jpg`**, cropped/resized differently (same people and poses). They add detail resolution, not independent viewpoints. Full inspection confirms two glazed leaves under a tall rectangular glazed transom/dark upper trim, a gridded semicircular fanlight, two-line engraved title, carved stone bands and spring blocks, and converging concrete approaches with simple metal handrails. Trees and pedestrians obscure much of the wall. `msktd_contact.jpg` retains the gallery screening context; interiors were excluded as exterior sources.

## New older supporting view

[School Designs / Scholer Gatewood submission](https://schooldesigns.com/Projects/purdue-university-roger-b-gatewood-wing-of-mechanical-engineering/), featured in the 2012 Architectural Portfolio, supplies `schooldesigns_01.jpg`. Actual file is281×225. It is a ground oblique of Gatewood's modern portal/front and adjacent short end, with lower tall narrow windows, taller middle groups and short upper groups. Useful for unobscured corner rhythm but too small for mullion detail. Capture date and first-image photographer are unknown; other gallery images credit Jon Denker/CAPS, which cannot automatically attribute the first blank-caption image.2019 upload path is not capture date. The three other gallery files were interiors and were excluded; their URLs remain in `schooldesigns_index.json` as screening history. This is additional old-condition evidence, not a post-renovation exterior survey.
