## Limits to preserve

Available sources support a detailed exterior concept, broad 3-wing/central-node organization, program areas, entry canopy, clerestory, classroom glazing, and selected interior visual language. No authoritative measured elevations, floor-by-floor plan, roof schedule, or current Street View source was verified in this research pass. Heights, precise facade stationing, interior room placement, roof edge profiles, and exact cardinal orientation of the photographed front elevation must be labelled estimates or left unverified until stronger sources are found. No security floor plan or device-placement map is being used.
