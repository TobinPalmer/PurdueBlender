# Elliott Hall of Music — round 8 sources and change notes

Work date: 2026-10-23. Scope: Elliott modular source and evidence in this folder only. The master blend, manifest, building index, and Hovde source were not edited.

## Sources used

- [SAH Archipedia: Edward C. Elliott Hall of Music](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/IN-01-157-0079) describes a red-brick exterior with Indiana-limestone banding, pilasters and ornament, a curving entry canopy, side-exit stair walls, and a second-floor Hovde walkway. These descriptions support feature types and materials, not the exact modeled locations or dimensions.
- [Purdue Convocations: Elliott Hall of Music seating chart](https://convocations.purdue.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Elliott-Seat-Map.pdf) shows a main-floor plan and first- and second-balcony charts with curved seating blocks. No scale or dimensions are supplied, so the interior model remains schematic.
- [Purdue Convocations: seating charts](https://convocations.purdue.edu/seating-charts/) says accessible seating is on the main floor and the venues do not have elevators.
- [Purdue Convocations accessibility guidance](https://convocations.purdue.edu/accessibility/) describes the north and south accessible entrances and step-free access guidance; it does not verify this model's door sizes or routes.
- [Purdue Convocations: Elliott 75th anniversary](https://convocations.purdue.edu/elliott-hall-of-music-75th-anniversary-1940-2015/) describes the building's sturdy brick walls and links construction photographs. The imagery did not yield a verified window schedule in this pass.
- [Purdue Convocations: Club Convos directions](https://convocations.purdue.edu/clubconvos/) describes a second-floor Hovde approach to Elliott's main lobby. The precise shared portal remains unresolved.
- The Elliott footprint follows Purdue BuildingShapes Feature 617: https://services1.arcgis.com/mLNdQKiKsj5Z5YMN/arcgis/rest/services/BuildingShapesZip4/FeatureServer/0.

## Round 8 changes

- Replaced the ambiguous connector model with an Elliott-side double-leaf glazed portal, limestone jambs/lintel, two canopy supports, and a short landing. SAH supports a curved canopy and the limestone vocabulary; exact portal location, door configuration/material, canopy support details, and all dimensions are estimates.
- Removed the connector shell, glazing, roof, terminal marker, and deferred label from round 5 because the render made it appear to penetrate the unverified Hovde wall. The Elliott landing now ends in open space with a review-only `LINK DEFERRED` marker. This is a deferred interface, not a completed walkway or an accessibility claim.
- Did not add facade windows. The sources located in this round do not provide a visually verified count/spacing schedule. The facade window pattern remains unresolved.
- Interior renders show only the auditorium components for clarity. Official chart evidence confirms the three seating levels and curved blocks, but seat count, row pitch, aisle width, floor elevations, stage dimensions, egress, and accessible clearances are not dimensioned by that chart.

## Saved asset and limitations

Blender 5.2.3 LTS saved and reopened `modular/buildings/elliott_hall_of_music.blend`; the Elliott asset collection contains 133 mesh objects after round 8. Review-only cameras and lights are temporary. No Hovde or master data was edited.

Still unverified: facade windows and exact ornament schedule; entrance/portal dimensions; canopy position/profile/support type; bridge endpoint and Hovde portal; continuous accessible route; and auditorium dimensions, seating layout, counts, circulation and egress.
