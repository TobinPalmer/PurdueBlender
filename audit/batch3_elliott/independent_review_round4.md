# Elliott Hall of Music — independent round 4 review

**Independent score: 6.2/10. Below the requested 9/10 threshold.** Reviewed 2026-10-23 by reopening `modular/buildings/elliott_hall_of_music.blend` in Blender 5.2.3, checking the 89-mesh `Asset_elliott_hall_of_music` collection, and inspecting the saved source-only, bridge detail, linked Hovde context, and auditorium renders. I did not edit the model or campus master.

## What improved

- The round-4 source reopens cleanly. The 496-object legacy reference collection remains separate from the 89-mesh export.
- The exterior keeps the rounded footprint, stepped massing, auditorium roof and raised flytower. The tile pattern reads clearly from above.
- The two Purdue-documented north/south accessible entries and black/gold awnings remain. The round-4 note correctly removes the unsupported interior access-route ribbons and clearance pads; retained blue wheelchair-seat markers are explicitly indicative.
- The linked-context images show a connector crossing the gap between Elliott and Hovde, consistent with the documented second-story connection. The builder did not alter Hovde or the master.
- The interior render has the broad three-level auditorium arrangement, separated blocks, aisles, balconies, stage opening, and curtains. It reads as a theater from the stage and rear views.

## Blocking defects

1. **Facade is still a blank mass.** The source-only overview shows a large uninterrupted red-brown wall around nearly the entire building, with no window apertures. Visible wall material has almost no readable brick bond or Indiana-limestone banding; only the roof tile texture and a few limestone trim elements read clearly. The result does not resemble the documented brick-and-limestone elevations. This is a major visual and source-match gap, acknowledged in the builder's own round-4 packet. Purdue/SAH references support the material family, and image search returned exterior photos with a curved brick facade, vertical pilasters and ground-level windows; exact counts and spacing still need direct image/elevation verification.
2. **Hovde end of bridge has no demonstrable doorway.** In both linked-context views the bridge arrives at and appears to meet/enter a broad Hovde wall panel. There is no clearly visible cut-through opening, door, or landing at that end. Purdue says visitors enter Hovde at the southeast entrance, take stairs/elevator to level two, and continue to Elliott's main lobby. That supports a route but does not establish this exact wall segment or prove that the current connector joins an opening. Coordinate with the Hovde asset owner and show the actual shared portal/opening before integrating this bridge into the master.
3. **Bridge shape and support remain speculative.** A long, narrow glazed tube with flat top/floor and repeated thin vertical frames is readable as a connector, but its about-18 m span, section, taper, material, supports and levels are estimates. The linked-context close-up suggests a flush/intersecting endpoint rather than a resolved doorway transition. No measured bridge plan or elevation was supplied.
4. **The two extra ground doors are not verified.** SAH supports additional ground entries between Elliott and Hovde, but the count, precise side/coordinates, width and door assemblies are guesses. The round-4 packet provides no ray or through-shell check for these three new openings (two ground doors and the upper portal); the earlier ray test covers only the north/south doors. The shown close view does not establish wall continuity around the new entries.
5. **Interior is still an illustrative seating blockout.** The theater volume and seat banks are useful, but seats are highly repetitive block forms, while row geometry, balcony levels, aisle widths, stage/backstage arrangement and all interior coordinates remain chart-informed or estimated. No current scaled architectural plan supports those dimensions. The shown interior views do not establish a connected lobby or a verified accessible path from the Hovde bridge to the main-floor wheelchair seats.

## Priority fixes before acceptance

1. Use a directly inspected Purdue/SAH/archive exterior image or a dimensioned elevation to lay out real facade openings, pilasters, limestone bands and entry composition; capture the selected reference image and map each modeled elevation to it. Do not infer a regular window schedule from the footprint.
2. Resolve Hovde's actual second-floor portal collaboratively against its current asset and the documented southeast entrance route. Cut/verify both wall openings, add a landing/door transition, and produce a close view that proves mesh continuity at each endpoint.
3. Add explicit shell ray/cross-section checks for all five apertures and verify bridge floor clearance, wall intersections, end coverage and alignment in the registered context.
4. Improve the exterior brick/stone material read and correct facade shading/seams. Keep estimated dimensions labeled as estimates.
5. Keep interior geometry marked conceptual until a dimensioned plan is available; refine circulation, lobby/stage/backstage transitions and seat/aisle proportions where sources support them.

## Source check

- Purdue Convocations' Club Convos directions establish the route from Hovde's southeast entrance to the second floor adjacent to Elliott's main lobby: https://convocations.purdue.edu/clubconvos/.
- SAH Archipedia describes Hovde and Elliott's second-story walkway, formal Hovde entry, additional ground-level entries between the buildings, red brick, Indiana limestone and red clay tile: https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/IN-01-157-0079.
- Purdue Convocations' public accessibility guidance supports the north/south accessible doors and awnings; the unscaled seating chart does not verify interior dimensions: https://convocations.purdue.edu/accessibility/ and https://convocations.purdue.edu/seating-charts/.

The sources support the building concept and route, but they do not verify the modeled dimensions, facade schedule, exact portal tie-in, or interior layout. The current asset is a meaningful improvement to the massing and auditorium blockout, but it is not close enough to 9/10 to accept or integrate as a finished building.
