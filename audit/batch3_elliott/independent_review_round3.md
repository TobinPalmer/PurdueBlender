# Elliott Hall of Music — independent review round 3

Reviewed 2026-10-23. I inspected the saved `modular/buildings/elliott_hall_of_music.blend`, the round 3 entrance and route renders, the exterior/interior views, the accessibility and route evidence, and current project placement metadata. I did not edit the building asset or either master blend.

## Rating

**Overall: 6.1/10. It does not meet 9/10.** This round fixes a significant omission by making two actual north/south wall openings and adding the Purdue-specified black-and-gold awnings. Purdue's current accessibility guidance supports those entrance labels and step-free access from each to main-floor seating. However, their positions and dimensions are estimates, and the newly drawn interior routes are not a credible or verified access layout. The principal Hovde connection and most of the architectural facade composition remain absent or unresolved.

## Evidence and findings

- The export inventory and `result_round3_accessibility.json` report 66 meshes, a 188-vertex GIS ring, two true openings, and zero added window holes. The wall ray check confirms an opening at 1.5 m and wall at 4 m at each selected location. That verifies a through-gap in the shell, not the real-world coordinate or door size.
- The current Purdue accessibility page says patrons may enter at the north or south access doors with black-and-gold awnings to reach main-floor seating without steps; it also says Elliott has no elevators and that wheelchair seating has nearby companion seats. This supports the pair and awning colors, not the modeled 3.8 × 3.0 m dimensions, exact map position, interior route, or blue clear-space dimensions. [Purdue accessibility guidance](https://convocations.purdue.edu/accessibility/)
- The SAH description says the formal campus entry is through a second-story walkway from Hovde Hall and that additional entries occur at ground level between the two buildings. It also describes the curved entry canopy, secondary entrance surrounds and stepped side-exit walls. Those elements are not represented convincingly in the current model/views. [SAH Archipedia](https://sah-archipedia.org/buildings/IN-01-157-0079)
- The north/south door renderings are close crops. The north view is dark and mostly shows a blank brick field with a broad black slab; the south view shows vertical pilaster-like strips but no visible curved canopy or contextual connection. The overall exterior render exposes large blank elevations after the unsupported window schedule was removed. Removing invented windows was the right evidence decision, but the resulting facade is not yet a finished representation.
- The top route render shows two long gold paths that do not follow an identified source plan. The route-coordinate list in `result_round3_accessibility.json` confirms extended perimeter traces plus a final diagonal segment to chart-derived targets. Since the seating map is unscaled, those targets cannot establish aisle position, travel distance, or clearances.
- Source metadata shows the original registration remains: the manifest origin is `[-111.0000, 49.9000]` m and the source local GIS shell is unchanged. The manifest still lists two export objects, so it must be refreshed after the accepted asset is integrated.

## Priority fixes

1. Use Purdue’s map and exterior imagery to place/shape the two accessible doors accurately; add and validate the Hovde second-floor bridge/formal entry and the ground-level Hovde-side connection. Do not describe the estimated door coordinates as verified.
2. Rework the route ribbons against an actual floor plan or remove them until a plan is available. As shown, they do not read as believable auditorium circulation. Keep only source-supported access facts in explanatory metadata.
3. Improve the facade from current orthogonal photos or archival images: model a plausible, source-backed opening rhythm; make the curved canopy and entrance surrounds legible; add the stepped side-exit walls and sculpture groups in their correct locations. Avoid replacing the zero-window facade with another unsupported uniform schedule.
4. Obtain a dimensioned section/elevation and scaled seating or floor plan before claiming accurate heights, roof/tower proportions, auditorium row coordinates, route widths or wheelchair clearances. Until then, clearly mark these as estimates.
5. After asset acceptance, refresh the modular manifest and building index, relink/reopen both masters, and verify the 66-mesh source path, placement marker and unchanged GIS footprint.
