# Elliott Hall of Music — independent review, round 6

**Score: 6.8/10. Does not meet the 9/10 target.** This score applies to the saved round-6 source only. Review was performed after opening `modular/buildings/elliott_hall_of_music.blend` in Blender 5.2.3 LTS and inspecting the supplied round-6 renders and evidence.

## Checks completed

- The saved file reopens and contains `Asset_elliott_hall_of_music` with 78 mesh objects.
- No objects in the export collection have render hiding or viewport hiding enabled in the reopened file. This supports the handoff's claim that exterior/roof hiding was limited to the interior renders.
- The exterior elevation shows the four continuous horizontal limestone bands. The broad fragmentary course issue described for round 5 appears corrected.
- Both new interior images clearly show auditorium seating. The stage-end view communicates the auditorium, and the plan view shows repeated seating blocks and aisles.
- The Hovde warning reads `HOVDE / LINK / PENDING`; the connector tip remains visibly separated from the neighboring building. The marker is a review aid, not an architectural feature.
- The evidence labels the masonry module, band dimensions and levels, window schedule, door positions, interior dimensions, seating spacing, and bridge endpoint as estimated or unverified. I found no round-6 statement claiming those estimates are measured facts.

## Remaining issues, prioritized

1. **Facade fidelity is still low.** In the exterior elevation, the brick reads mostly as a smooth, dark red surface; the staggered bond and mortar are not legible at this view scale. The pilasters and bands form a regular grid, but the evidence does not verify their spacing or positions. There are no visible facade windows, and the fly-tower mass is a plain block. SAH Archipedia supports the general use of red brick, Indiana limestone banding/pilasters, ornament, and a tile roof; it does not validate this modeled schedule or dimensions. The facade needs a stronger, inspectable brick treatment and source-backed openings/ornament before high fidelity can be claimed.
2. **Interior completeness and source fit remain uncertain.** The auditorium is recognizable and much better evidenced than in prior rounds, but both views show an open cutaway without a complete enclosing room/ceiling. The seat rows, stage opening, and aisles look schematic, and no dimensioned current auditorium plan or seating schedule is cited. The plan view is visually busy and its crop makes the perimeter and circulation difficult to assess. Treat all layout and seat-count implications as illustrative.
3. **Vertical form and roof remain estimates.** The exterior view has a flat-looking roof deck and an underspecified fly tower. The source notes explicitly label the tower and vertical envelope as estimated; no height/elevation source is supplied. Add verified elevation evidence or keep the shape clearly documented as an approximation.
4. **The connector status evidence is too tightly framed.** The close view proves that a gap remains, and its warning is legible, but it does not show both buildings or the full landing. Use a context view that identifies Elliott and Hovde and demonstrates the endpoint relationship. Keep the pending label out of presentation views, or make clear it is a temporary QA label.
5. **Accessibility evidence is partial.** Earlier five center-ray checks only show that rays pass through sampled mesh locations. They do not establish that doors are correctly placed, sized, operable, clear of seating, or connected by a continuous accessible route. The round-6 notes correctly retain these as unverified.

## Score rationale

The asset is now robust enough to reopen, has corrected continuous band geometry, and has clear auditorium views. Its general massing and source-described material palette are recognizable. It remains a concept-level model for the facade and interior: its openings, dimensions, roof/tower, seating, and Hovde connection are not verified, and the exterior texture/details do not yet read convincingly. **6.8/10; no 9/10 sign-off.**

## Most useful next work

1. Find directly inspectable, dated Purdue/archive exterior photographs or elevations and use them to establish the window, entry, ornament, and roof/tower forms; record any features that remain unknown.
2. Improve brick and limestone readability in a close facade render, then inspect a full elevation and both ends for continuity and clipping.
3. Obtain a dimensioned auditorium plan or explicitly retain a schematic interior; improve the cutaway and top-down views so shell, aisles, stage, and seat blocks can be checked together.
4. Resolve the Hovde landing jointly with the Hovde owner using a context render; keep the connection labeled pending until a continuous, source-supported route and portal are verified.
