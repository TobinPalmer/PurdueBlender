## Findings

### Round 10 portal repair

The pale limestone bands now stop at the portal and the two dark glass leaves are visible in the close view. That fixes the specific overlap called out in round 9. The new portal view is tightly framed; it confirms the local trim clearance but does not establish how the canopy, landing and entrance meet the whole facade. Brick, limestone, metal and glass are distinguishable, though the brick repeat is very regular and most dimensions remain estimated.

### Exterior fidelity and materials

The broader exterior evidence still shows large blank wall areas divided by a repetitive grid of light bands and vertical ribs. There is no verified window schedule, and no convincing evidence-backed facade openings, secondary entrances, side-exit stair treatment, or detailed fly tower/apse proportions. The roof and flytower remain simple masses. The round 10 change does not address these round 9 exterior gaps. The red brick and pale stone palette is appropriate at a broad level, but the current surfaces read as a stylized blockout rather than a detailed exterior model.

### Interior and texturing

The stage-to-audience render makes three seating levels, balcony edges, and curtains legible. Seats form dense, regular repeated blocks, with little material or color variation. The view does not show convincing stair and aisle geometry, backstage and orchestra relationships, or dimensioned circulation. Purdue’s current seating chart confirms the main floor plus first and second balconies and curved seating blocks, but it is not a dimensioned plan; those details should remain labeled schematic. The interior is materially populated but not yet sufficiently resolved or textured for a 9/10 assessment.

### Footprint and neighboring buildings

The round 9 comparison reports 0.167 m RMS and 0.259 m maximum vertex residual against Purdue BuildingShapes FID 617, with area difference -0.168%. The overlaid outlines visually track closely. This supports horizontal footprint placement at the stated approximate map conversion; it says nothing about height, elevations, interior, or surveyed accuracy. The adjacent FID 618 polygon touches Elliott’s outline, but that does not prove a doorway or route. The Elliott landing remains appropriately isolated pending a separate Hovde-side check.

## Work needed before 9/10

1. Build and document facade openings and elevation details from a pixel-verifiable current photograph or drawing; avoid guessing a window cadence.
2. Refine the fly tower, apsidal stage end, roof profile and secondary/side exits from source evidence.
3. Improve the auditorium’s seat materials and model legible aisles, stairs, stage and backstage relationships. Use dimensioned plans where available; otherwise preserve the schematic designation.
4. Show a wider, whole-building entry context and coordinate the Elliott/Hovde interface only after both portal elevations are verified.
