## Evidence and limits

The Purdue Daniels School [official self-guided tour PDF](https://business.purdue.edu/undergraduate/visit/self-guided-tour.pdf) identifies the Drawing Room on the first floor (page 6) and Parrish Library on the second floor (page 4). The pages support the room identities and broad furnishing/material cues: the Drawing Room photo shows blue and lime upholstery, warm wood tables, and acoustic screens; the Parrish photo shows low book shelving/books, warm wood dividers, dark counter surfaces, and booth seating. These photographs are not calibrated elevations or plans. All room XY locations, furniture dimensions/counts, ceiling grids, and circulation are therefore marked as estimates.

For exterior context, the coordinator previously inspected Google Maps Street View (March 2026) and reported limestone piers, narrow dark vertical windows, a reddish brick ground band, and a flat parapet. That imagery was not independently captured in this audit folder and does not validate the interior work.

## Round 6 — GIS vertical-field consistency check

A read-only check of Purdue BuildingShapesZip4 feature 54 records the raw vertical attributes and compares them with the builder's ground-relative datums. Under a conditional feet/common-datum reading, subtracting `G=12.7` from `F01` through `F07` yields 12.7, 28.0, 40.7, 53.5, 66.2, 78.9, and 91.6 ft, matching the modeled L1-L7 values. `R-G=104.3 ft` also matches the modeled roof-deck datum. This confirms that the preserved datums follow the GIS value sequence consistently; it is not an independent height check because those same GIS values informed the model.

The saved layer schema provides neither field descriptions nor units; aliases repeat the short field names. Feet are therefore inferred from the numeric sequence, not specified by metadata. `Elevation=612.4`, `Extrution=108`, and `R=117` have no documented relation to a vertical datum. The roof interpretation is ambiguous: `R-G` matches the deck, while raw `R` matches the modeled penthouse top if treated as an above-grade value. `Extrution` numerically matches the modeled parapet only under an above-grade-feet assumption. No roof, parapet, floor, or penthouse geometry was changed. Continue to treat height and roof levels as unverified pending documented field semantics or independent elevations. Details are in `evidence_round6.json`; the raw feature and layer metadata snapshots are retained as referenced there. The R4 saved-source validation remains the latest reopen check because this supplement is documentation-only.

## Round 7 — orthophoto footprint, LiDAR height and Purdue emergency maps

This source-only pass checked the footprint against an independent raster and added qualified vertical and interior-plan sources. The official Purdue FID 54 ring was drawn over an Indiana GIO 2023 orthophoto export using the service-returned WGS84 extent and no image-fit adjustment. The visible roof perimeter and re-entrant shape agree at screening scale with the GIS/model outline. That supports broad building identity, orientation and approximate position together with the coordinator's marker-to-manifest check; it does not certify wall-polygon residuals, since the model wire was derived from the same GIS ring and the project conversion is approximate. The raw crop, overlay and repeatable script are `indiana_ortho_krannert_2023_crop.jpg`, `krannert_2023_orthophoto_footprint_overlay_r7.png` and `overlay_krannert_ortho_r7.ps1`.

An independent height check uses Indiana's 2018 5 ft 3DEP DSM tile `in2018_30001885_12` at the GIS footprint and 22 point queries to Indiana's 2016–2020 bare-earth DEM. The 895 valid DSM cell centers have a median elevation of 721.71 ftUS; sampled bare earth has median 615.255 ftUS, for an indicative median surface difference of 106.46 ft. The p90 surface difference is about 106.69 ft and the footprint maximum about 107.65 ft. Most rooftop surface cells are therefore close to the model's 104.3 ft roof-deck and 108 ft parapet datums. The low p10 includes lower surfaces/voids and is not a roof height. The 5 ft DSM and interpolated terrain do not survey a named datum, but the 2018 capture does not independently support the model's 117 ft penthouse top. It also cannot prove the high penthouse is absent, so that volume remains explicitly unresolved; no roof geometry was changed. GIS `R` and `Extrution` still have undocumented units/semantics. Full values, calculation, sources and limits are in `roof_height_assessment_r7.json`.

The Purdue Daniels School Safety and Facilities site publishes `Krannert Emergency Maps.pdf`, with basement, ground and floor 1–7 room/corridor/stair/elevator diagrams. These show circulation and room adjacency beyond the two furnished interior areas, but the drawings are hand-sketched and have no stated scale, dimension strings, issue date or as-built status. They support topology only; R7 adds no partition walls based on the maps. The local PDF and page renders are retained in this audit folder, and their limits are detailed in `evidence_round7.json`.

No model geometry, material, placement, master or shared files changed in R7. The R4 saved-source validation remains the latest geometry/material validation; a fresh R7 source reopen/count check is in `validation_round7.json`. The shared manifest's deleted `KRAN_Surrounding_Plaza_Paving` inventory entry still needs coordinator-only correction.
The LiDAR p25 surface difference (~91.61 ft AGL) also coincides with the builder L7 datum (91.6 ft), while most cells sit around the main deck (104.3 ft) and the highest observed cell is near the 108 ft parapet. This pattern is consistent with a stepped roof profile; it does not identify the lower surface as an occupied story.
