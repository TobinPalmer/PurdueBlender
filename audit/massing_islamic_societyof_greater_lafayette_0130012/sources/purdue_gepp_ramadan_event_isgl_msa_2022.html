Global Celebration - Ramadan

Event Date:
April 26, 2022
Time:
8:20-9:30 pm
Location:
Islamic Society of Greater Lafayette, 1022 1st Street, Purdue University
Priority:
No
College Calendar:
Show

Global Engineering Programs and Partnerships is very excited to host an event with the Muslim Student Association (MSA).

Purdue students, faculty, and staff are invited to join the MSA as they break their daily fast during Ramadan, with a prayer, a meal, and a presentation about the customs and traditions of Ramadan.

Join us for this enriching event!
