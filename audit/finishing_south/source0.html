Mathematical Sciences Building

The Mathematical Sciences Building (MATH) houses the Departments of Mathematics and Statistics as well as College of Science Administration and Purdue IT.

Safety

  • Building Emergency Plan
  • MATH Evacuation and Shelter Procedures Quicksheet (PDF) gives you an overview of the procedures to follow in the case of an fire or emergency requiring you to seek shelter.

Building Deputy

Contact the building deputy with any building issue. Office hours are 7 am - 4 pm weekdays.

Bryan Dispennett
Phone: 494-1989
Email: bdispennett@purdue.edu
Office: MATH 199 (West Dock)

After hours, if there is a plumbing, electrical or lockout emergency contact Purdue Police Dispatch for assistance. Police Dispatch Phone: 494-8221
