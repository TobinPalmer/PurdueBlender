Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about life at 308 North Street. Your signed lease and addenda are always the final word if anything differs here. Leasing, meals, room types, tours, and what to expect as a resident.

Is food service really included in the lease?

  • Yes! About 18 meals a week are included in your lease (roughly a $5,000 annual value), not a separate dining plan.
  • That saves residents hours each week on shopping, cooking, and dishes so they can focus on class and campus life.
  • Chef-prepared meals with late plates when classes conflict, plus dietary options with advance notice. Details on Amenities.

Are utilities included?

  • Yes! Utilities are included in your lease.
  • See utilities included on the amenities page for details.

Is the facility furnished?

  • Yes! The building is fully furnished for student living.
  • What’s in your specific room depends on the floor plan.

Do you offer nine-month leases?

  • Yes! Nine-month leases align with the academic calendar (typically August 15 through May 15 with nine payments, not twelve).
  • Semester leases may be available in limited numbers. Ask when you apply.
  • Availability and exact dates change by year.

What are the lease rates?

  • Pricing is per resident (leased by the bed). Nine-month rates for the current academic year:
  • Double rooms (about 214–300 sq ft): $1,400–$1,650/month
  • Two-room suites with semi-private bath (195 sq ft/room): $1,500/month
  • Large two-room suites with semi-private bath (300 sq ft/room): $1,650/month
  • Select rooms with private attached bath: $1,650–$1,750/month
  • Security deposit is typically $500. Confirm on your lease.
  • Semester leases and prepaid discounts may be available. Ask when you apply.

How do tours work?

  • Schedule a tour by phone or email, or use our contact form.
  • We’ll confirm times, what to bring, and whether a video option is available if you’re out of town.

What room types do you offer?

  • Double rooms and two-room suites (two residents per room).
  • Semi-private and private bathroom options on select floor plans.

How does roommate assignment work?

  • Tell us your preference when you apply.
  • We’ll explain how matching works for your cohort.

Who is this a good fit for?

  • Incoming freshmen, transfer students, graduate students, international students, co-ops, and students with non-traditional timelines.
  • Ask how your dates line up with lease start and end for the year you’re interested in.

Do you have parking available?

  • Yes! Unassigned surface lot parking when available (about $125/month).
  • Many residents bike or walk to campus due to our proximity.
  • Spaces are leased on a first come, first served basis.

Is 308 North Street a Greek chapter?

  • No. We are independent student housing and are not affiliated with Purdue University.
  • The property is not associated with an active fraternity, sorority, or Purdue University club.

Still have questions?

  • Contact us by phone, email, or the contact form on this site.
  • We’re happy to help parents and students alike.
