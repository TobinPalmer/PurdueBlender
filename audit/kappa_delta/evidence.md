# Kappa Delta / Theta Nu model evidence

## Identification and site

- Official Purdue chapter site identifies the chapter as Kappa Delta, Theta Nu, and lists **358 Northwestern Avenue, West Lafayette, IN**. Captured copy: [`sources/chapter_home.html`](sources/chapter_home.html).
- Purdue Panhellenic's chapter listing corroborates the chapter identity and official chapter website.
- Wells & Wells' project portfolio names the project “Kappa Delta Purdue”; the linked project page is titled “Kappa Delta Purdue” and includes exterior and interior photographs labeled **358 Northwestern Avenue**. Captured pages: [`sources/builder_portfolio.html`](sources/builder_portfolio.html) and [`sources/architect_project.html`](sources/architect_project.html). The source photos used for visual reference are in [`sources/architect_images/`](sources/architect_images/) with an audit contact sheet at [`sources/architect_contact_sheet.jpg`](sources/architect_contact_sheet.jpg).
- The Tippecanoe County 2020 minor-subdivision/site plan identifies the sorority project at the southeast corner of Northwestern Avenue and Fowler Avenue. Captured plan and renders: [`sources/county_subdivision_plan.pdf`](sources/county_subdivision_plan.pdf), [`sources/site_plan-1.png`](sources/site_plan-1.png), and [`sources/site_plan-2.png`](sources/site_plan-2.png).
- Tippecanoe County GIS address point FID 61768 and parcel 79-07-19-287-001.000-026 correspond to 358 Northwestern Avenue; the point comments name the “UNIVERSITY- THETA NU KAPPA DELTA SORORITY.” Evidence snapshots: [`sources/address_point_358_northwestern.json`](sources/address_point_358_northwestern.json) and [`sources/parcel_358_northwestern.json`](sources/parcel_358_northwestern.json).
- OpenStreetMap way **860903232** supplies the four-corner building outline; the county address point is within about 1.8 m of its center. Source snapshots: [`sources/osm_kappa_delta_building_860903232_2026-10-27.osm`](sources/osm_kappa_delta_building_860903232_2026-10-27.osm) and [`sources/osm_map_358nw_2026-10-27.osm`](sources/osm_map_358nw_2026-10-27.osm). Nearby 2018 Indiana LiDAR predates this building, which appears in the 2020/2021 construction materials, so it cannot establish the current roof/facade heights; see [`sources/indiana_lidar_near_site.json`](sources/indiana_lidar_near_site.json).

## Geometry and placement

- The source outline is approximately **34.51 × 22.44 m**, with area **773.29 m²**. The asset preserves the four-point source outline as its building-footprint polygon and derives facade dimensions from the projected edge directions.
- The asset uses local geometry centered on the footprint. Place its origin at project campus XY **(379.06523556, -12.38651211) m**, using the project transform `x=(lon+86.9137)*111412.84*cos(radians(40.4274))`, `y=(lat-40.4274)*111040`. Approximate geodetic origin: **(-86.9092305, 40.4272884)**. This is about 4.8 m east and 2.8 m south of the county address point, consistent with placement near (but not necessarily at) the footprint centroid. The local axis follows the long road-parallel facade; source ring projection is preserved through an in-plane rotation.
- The 3-story, limewashed pale-brick residence is modeled with a narrower projecting central entry/gable bay, limestone string courses and surrounds, tall arched ground-floor glazing, upper sash windows with selective Juliet rails, central metal canopy/steps, dark hipped roof, and balanced paired dormers set symmetrically to either side of the central gable. The isolated ridge-end blocks from the earlier revision were removed. Facade rhythm is photo-informed; non-visible elevations are estimates.
- The interior collection is a **schematic**, not a verified floor plan: the first floor has a recognizable lounge group with sofas, armchairs and coffee table; a long communal dining table and chairs; kitchen cabinetry, quartz counters, sink, range, refrigerator, and island stools; and a photo-informed switchback stair/lobby with treads, balusters, and rails. Upper floors retain bedroom and partition cues. No measured floor plan was located. Do not present it as survey-quality interior geometry.
- Main dimensions and orientation come from mapping; facade composition and colors come primarily from the builder/architect photos and current Street View. Floor heights, wall thicknesses, hidden sides, room layout, roof framing, and exact roof pitch are estimated from images.
