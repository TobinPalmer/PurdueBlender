3rd and West is a new student apartment community in the Discovery Park District at Purdue. The dynamic form establishes a public plaza at the corner of 3rd and McCormick. The main entry leads into a sequence of landscaped courtyards; the north is more public and the south more private. A highly transparent ground floor of amenities visually connects the two courtyards, with an operable glass wall opening toward the south courtyard. Study and group-work spaces stack the full height of the building at the corners. Along the west facade, patterned Norman brick and glazing create texture; shifts in facade depth break up the mass.
