# Reference imagery review

Coordinating agent used Google Maps in the browser, not a blocked static web fetch. Builders' own source records supplement these observations. Pins identify buildings and context, not surveyed facade positions.

- Krannert: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Krannert+Building+Purdue resolved to KRAN/Graduate School of Management,403MitchDanielsBoulevard,40.4235193,-86.9109128. SGrant Street View March2026 showed tall pale limestone piers with narrow continuous dark vertical glazing, reddish ground-level base with broader windows, and a flat louvered parapet. Construction nearby is visible. Observations sent to builder.
- Hicks: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Hicks+Undergraduate+Library+Purdue resolved to JohnW.HicksUndergraduateLibrary, Ground504WStateStreet,40.4245248,-86.9126242. Builder separately inspected its Maps interior gallery; own report records open tables/chairs, carpet and suspended linear lighting.
- Rawls: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Rawls+Hall+Purdue resolved to100SGrantStreet,40.4237823,-86.9099567; a second nearby Rawls listing is also present. First gallery image attributed to Mu,November2024 showed pale atrium walls, cylindrical gray columns, glazed balustrades/wood handrails, crossing bridges, a broad straight stair on one side, dark carpet with study furniture, wood end panels and a shallow arched white ceiling with high glazing. Exact dimensions and the atrium's floor-by-floor outline are not established by this single view. Builder retained explicit uncertainty rather than guessing new upper-level voids.
- Lawson: builder independently viewed Maps photo attributed to FrankSadek,October2017; its evidence report records unresolved projecting-window/limestone-entry discrepancies. A place match does not certify those existing facade dimensions.
- Niswonger: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Niswonger+Aviation+Technology+Building resolved to1401AviationDrive,40.4162784,-86.9293968, at Purdue Airport. TommyTomTom June2022 first gallery photo showed a low red/brown brick wing with a black horizontal window/sign band, a long pale metal entrance canopy, dark teal glazed entry and a raised sloping pale roof over tall teal clerestory glazing. Interior gallery thumbnail showed cream hangar trusses, linear lights and light aircraft through a window. These observations refer to Aviation Technology, not the separate Holleman-Niswonger Simulator Center.

Images were viewed for reference, not baked into textures. Material identification is qualitative. Source dates and estimates remain in each builder report. Some interiors derive from historical plans and do not certify present-day configuration.
