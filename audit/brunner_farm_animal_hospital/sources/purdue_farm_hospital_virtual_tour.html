Farm Animal Hospital Tour

The David and Bonnie Brunner Farm Animal Hospital incorporates advanced design features and provides emergency care and treatment as well as advanced diagnostics and specialized veterinary medical care for livestock.

 

Start the Tour

The 23,900 square-foot facility is a separate building located to the east of the Equine Hospital. The physical separation of ruminants and swine from horses improves biosecurity.

Proudly AAHA-Accredited

We are an AAHA-accredited veterinary hospital. That means we hold ourselves to a higher standard. Pets are our passion. And keeping them healthy is our #1 priority. Here, we strive to deliver excellent care for pets. Because your pets deserve nothing less.
