  1. Location

Here we are:

750 S University Street, West Lafayette, IN (across the street from Lyles-Porter Hall)

Subject Parking: parking is available in the parking lot in front of the MRI Facility Building. There are two spots labeled "MRI". Visitors and Research Volunteers may park here free of charge.

Purdue A Permit: The parking lot in front of the facility building, Harrison Street parking garage and street parking on University Street.

Purdue C Permit:  Discovery Park parking lot.

Please be aware GPS maps may not be updated with this new location. As an alternative to the above directions you may enter 625 Harrison St. West Lafayette, IN into your GPS which will direct you to Purdue Veterinary Medicine - Lynn Hall. Life Sciences Imaging Facility is also located on Harrison Street, across the street and just west of Lynn Hall.
