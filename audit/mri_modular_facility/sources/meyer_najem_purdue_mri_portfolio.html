Purdue Research Foundation, Purdue University MRI Facility

Purdue needed a home for two research MRI magnets, one GE and one Siemens, supporting work on animal imaging and on concussions in athletes. We built the 3,800 SF facility in under three months.

  • Modular construction made delivery possible. Four units were framed, wired, plumbed, and finished at a plant in Elkhart, then trucked to West Lafayette and assembled into one building. The magnets came in through openings left in the roof, lowered by crane and coordinated with Purdue before the structure closed around them.
  • Intentional delivery through Design-Build. Each MRI room carries lead shielding designed to the specific magnet inside it, so the two rooms are purpose-built rather than identical. We finished the building with masonry veneer, roofing, aluminum windows and doors, and the site work around it.
  • Tight Site: The site was the hard part. An existing building on one side, an operating parking lot on the other that had to stay open, and four modular units plus two magnets to set by crane in between.
