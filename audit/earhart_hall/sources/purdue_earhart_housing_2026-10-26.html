Welcome to Earhart Hall

Earhart Hall offers traditional-style rooms with shared community bathrooms. Each room is air-conditioned with individual temperature controls for comfort year-round. The hall features inviting community spaces including a shared kitchen, private study rooms, and large social lounges—perfect for relaxing, collaborating, or connecting with friends. Best of all, Earhart Dining Court is located right downstairs, so you can grab a meal without stepping outside—especially convenient during Indiana’s chilly winters. For busy days, the “On-The-GO!” station offers quick, hot or cold meal options to keep you fueled and focused. 

Amelia Earhart & Our Faculty-in-Residence Program 

In 1936, aviation pioneer Amelia Earhart joined the Purdue faculty and lived in Windsor Hall (Duhme). Her legacy continues to inspire generations of students, especially women pursuing bold dreams. In honor of her trailblazing spirit, our Faculty-in-Residence program proudly bears her name—connecting students with faculty in meaningful, everyday ways that enrich the residential experience.

Itasca Club is the Residence Hall Club of Earhart Hall, named after the last ship to hear from Amelia Earhart. All Earhart Hall residents are members of this student organization. Itasca Club plans and implements events for residents, purchases materials for residents to rent from the front desk and works to create a sense of community throughout the entire hall. Our hall clubs across campus serve as a great gateway to your first leadership opportunity on campus! Interested? Connect with your Resident Assistant.
