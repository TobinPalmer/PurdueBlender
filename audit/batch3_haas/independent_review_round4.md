# Felix Haas Hall — independent review round 4

**Asset reviewed:** `modular/buildings/felix_haas_hall.blend`, opened independently in Blender 5.2.3. I inspected the round-four exterior, entrance, and first-floor renders against the preserved Purdue Events exterior photograph and Purdue CS first-floor diagram, then checked the saved continuity/reopen records and inspected the current material nodes and facade overlay bounds. The file reopened with `Asset_felix_haas_hall`, 972 direct export objects, and the original GIS envelope.

**Overall: 6.6/10. It does not meet 9/10.** The roof tile pattern is now legible at building scale, the two long-side treatments and west-end features were added, and unsupported cupola geometry was removed from export. The exterior still has a visibly blank wall plane in the overall view and the entrance remains a highly simplified attached facade. Color and material response differ from the reference image, and interior registration is based on an unscaled diagram.

## Evidence and limits

- Purdue Events exterior photo (`audit/batch3_haas/exterior_reference.jpg`) supports the general brick/limestone contrast, central entry, arched lower openings, and hipped roof. It is a perspective image, so it does not establish metric side-window placement or heights.
- Purdue CS [HAAS first-floor plan](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/resources/docs/HAAS_first.pdf) supports room labels and broad adjacency. It has no scale and does not validate the modeled room dimensions.
- Purdue Archives' [historical timeline](https://collections.lib.purdue.edu/timelines/puhistorical) supports the 17-step count, not the modeled tread dimensions.
- `continuity_check.json` records 17 treads, five near-side and five far-side lower glazing units, 12 long-elevation piers, six west-end piers, and zero cupola objects in export. `reopen_check.json` confirms the saved file reopened with 972 direct export objects and unchanged GIS bounds/transforms. These are structural checks, not proof of facade fidelity.
- `correction_evidence.md` correctly states that the opposite-side bays, west-end window schedule, roof color/course dimensions, and interior registration are estimates. The new work improves visual completeness but does not remove those uncertainties.

**Highest-impact remaining work:** inspect and render each of the four elevations separately; resolve the blank wall visible in the overview; reproduce the photo's characteristic brick-inlaid pale stone facade at the entrance and corners; retune the roof to the deeper red seen in the source; and obtain scaled/current interior or elevation documentation before claiming dimensional accuracy. The present evidence supports no 9/10 rating.
