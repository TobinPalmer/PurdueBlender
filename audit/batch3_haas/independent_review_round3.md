## Source and verification notes

- Purdue Archives’ historical timeline says seventeen steps lead to the Memorial Gymnasium entrance, supporting the step count but not the modeled metric dimensions: [Purdue University Historical Timeline](https://collections.lib.purdue.edu/timelines/puhistorical).
- Purdue Computer Science’s [first-floor plan](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/resources/docs/HAAS_first.pdf) supports room names and broad relationships only; it has no scale.
- Purdue Events’ [Felix Haas Hall page](https://events.purdue.edu/felix_haas_hall) and preserved local image `exterior_reference.jpg` support the qualitative brick/stone contrast, arched lower windows, main entry, and broad roof. They do not document the modeled side elevation or roof ornament dimensions.
- Google Maps was not used as dimensional evidence in this round. Its available gallery is user-contributed imagery and cannot establish building measurements.
- `continuity_check.json` and `reopen_check.json` support object counts, stair-to-threshold continuity, selected shell/roof bounds, transforms, and collection presence. They do not independently establish fidelity to undocumented elevations or interior dimensions.

**Highest-impact remaining work:** make the facade detail consistent across elevations, tune the rendered brick/limestone and roof tile pattern against the saved Purdue photo, and verify the roof ornament and interior registration from better references. The current evidence does not support a 9/10 rating.
