## As-built uncertainty

The official and reliable public evidence supports massing cues, four sloped masonry faces and historical interior furniture categories. It does not provide a private floorplan or dimensioned wall section. R2 therefore identifies the wall inclination, lower apron scale, furniture sizes and positions, and current floor topology as uncertain. Planned metal wall covers and master-plan room proposals are not represented as completed work.
