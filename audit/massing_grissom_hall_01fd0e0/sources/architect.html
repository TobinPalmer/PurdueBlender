Purdue University – Grissom Hall
West Lafayette, IN

The reinvented Grissom Hall is the beautiful, new home of the Industrial Engineering Department at Purdue University.  This fast-tracked project converted an early-20th Century structure into a naturally lit, modern work/learning environment for today’s students, faculty and staff.   The project was transformational!  With the Industrial Engineering Student Center at the heart of the 52,000 square feet of renovated space, Grissom Hall now features the following areas:

  • Open Workstations
  • A Work Café
  • Classrooms (Active Learning & Traditional)
  • Focus Booths
  • Cyber Labs
  • Collaboration and Teaming Rooms

MSKTD & Associates provided architectural, structural engineering and interior design services for this highly successful project at Purdue University.
