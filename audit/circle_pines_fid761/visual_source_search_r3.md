- Item page: https://earchives.lib.purdue.edu/digital/collection/debris/id/50711

- Retrieval date: 2026-10-28; archive item itself is a scanned 1954–55 yearbook page.

The yearbook page associates Circle Pines with a group photograph posed in front of a covered porch supported by columns. The page gives no street address and does not establish that the structure is the current FID 761 building. The 2013 Purdue Cooperative Housing history page also states that co-ops moved, rebuilt, and relocated, so historic visual continuity cannot be assumed: https://hmink12.wordpress.com/2013/04/17/purdue-cooperative-housing-history/.

## Modeling decision

The historical porch image is retained as dated context only and is not used to add a porch, columns, or facade details to the current candidate. With no current visual source, the saved exterior remains a conservative, neutral shell and a thin planar cap. Procedural material variation and soft exterior lighting improve legibility but do not identify an observed facade material.
