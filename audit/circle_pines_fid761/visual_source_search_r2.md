The current asset's continuity with any old image is unverified.
- General web/image queries for “Circle Pines cooperative Purdue house exterior photo”, “Circle Pines 1000 David Ross Road house photo”, and `site:circlepines.org` surfaced no attributable current facade image. Search results for other Purdue co-ops and unrelated homes were excluded.

## Modeling decision

Without an attributable current exterior reference, the candidate makes no claims about facade material, window positions/rhythm, entry, roof style, or interior plan. It uses a neutral outer wall shell and a thin planar cap only as a massing placeholder. The 3D room partitions, route, short steps, tables, chairs, and bed-like insert are explicitly schematic and are not presented as an as-built layout.
