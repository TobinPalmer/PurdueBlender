# Beering Hall material upgrade

Source: Purdue College of Education, https://education.purdue.edu/about/ ; official exterior image https://education.purdue.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Beering-4.jpeg (retrieved 2026-10-22, original image retained as reference.jpeg). This photograph supports warm red/brown face brick, pale limestone bands, and dark blue glazing. It does not measure brick dimensions or establish surveyed geometry.

Inspection: 463 original objects; 14 materials originally consisted only of Principled BSDF and output. Actual assigned meshes inspected: three masonry cores, 113 limestone objects, 101 glazed objects, 159 bronze objects, one brick plaza. All original geometry, object matrices, and collection membership preserved.

Changes: packed scanned masonry base color/roughness/normal/height plus physically scaled horizontal courses; subtle limestone grain, wood/metal/leather/carpet/terrazzo roughness and tactile variation; less perfectly smooth blue tinted glass with IOR 1.48. No subdivision, displacement geometry, geometry edits, positional changes, or master writes.

Limitations: materials substantially improve surface response but cannot establish photorealism by themselves. Original geometry has simple box wings, repeated windows and unsupported interior details. Existing green copper roof/finial differs from the official photo's pale circular roof cap; geometry and original material identity intentionally preserved for separate architectural review. Material microstructure is representative, not an on-site scan of Beering's actual brick. Exact current facade conditions and bond dimensions remain unverified.

Validation: saved asset reopened in Blender 5.2.3 LTS. verification.json compares all 463 original exported objects, mesh vertex/polygon counts and world matrices: identical. Four scanned red_brick images are packed and retain project-relative paths. Scene adds one separate MaterialReview_Beering collection with camera and sun outside Asset_beering_hall. No shared master edits. Final render staging camera uses the west side because inherited windows populate that facade.

Texture source: Poly Haven red_brick, https://polyhaven.com/a/red_brick, CC0; representative 1.4m tile with approximately 0.21m face lengths and 0.065m courses plus mortar. Shader contains packed base color, non-color roughness, tangent normal and 4mm bump height; metric UVs explicitly derived from object world coordinates and face orientation. Four meshes use metric UVs (three masonry cores and plaza slab). Final two masonry materials use this scanned set; ten secondary materials gained microtexture or improved optical response. AV display and gold medallion retain existing shaders.

Visual QA: first render exposed the windowless east face and excessively bright illumination. Revised hero to west side and reduced illumination/exposure; replaced heavily weathered industrial brick with cleaner modern red_brick. Final hero is 1200x1000. Remaining blank upper facades and simplified roof geometry are inherited architectural limitations, not verified facade fidelity.

## Final facade revision (supersedes material-only geometry statement above)
Coordinator expanded scope to address visibly blank facade blockers. Added 132 representative tower glazing assemblies across four elevations (six rows, with bronze jambs/rails) and 39 east classroom panes repeating the existing west facade rhythm. These are explicitly estimated exterior improvements informed by the official photo; their exact opening locations are NOT surveyed or architecturally verified. Original source meshes and all original object matrices remain unchanged; 699 new objects increase exported collection from 463 to 1162. Three original masonry cores received reversible 35mm two-segment bevel modifiers. Footprint/local frame/export collection preserved. Reflections replace inappropriate transmission through solid masonry backing. Material-only saved stage preserved separately as materials_only_backup.blend.

Final independent reopen verification passes original object subset, base mesh topology and matrices; all four referenced images packed. JSON field geometry_and_transforms_unchanged refers to ORIGINAL objects/base meshes and does not imply no new facade geometry or no bevel modifiers. No master writes. Surface improvement and blank facade repair achieved; exact facade opening fidelity and photorealistic architectural reconstruction remain unverified.
