### Executive Summary & Methodology
This audit verifies the exact physical cross sections, pavement material compositions, hardscape-to-greenspace ratios, and geometric dimensions of Purdue University's three primary pedestrian arteries:
1. **Centennial Mall & The Wilmeth Active Learning Center (WALC) / Bell Tower Precinct**
2. **The Engineering Mall (Purdue Mall) & The Engineering Fountain Plaza**
3. **Third Street Promenade (Student Success Corridor / Greenway)**

Data has been compiled from OpenStreetMap centimeter-accurate geodetic surveyed vectors, Purdue University *Giant Leaps Master Plan* documentation, project portfolios from landscape architecture lead **Rundell Ernstberger Associates (REA)** and civil engineer **Kimley-Horn**, high-resolution aerial orthophotography, and Google Street View visual audits.

## 1. Centennial Mall, WALC, and Purdue Bell Tower Precinct

### 1.1 Hardscape vs. Grass Ratio (Ground Truth Verification)
* **Historic Site Transition:** Prior to 2015, the site was occupied by the 1924 Purdue Power Plant (PUP) with its massive brick smokestack. Following demolition and WALC's completion in August 2017 (3.9-acre site master plan by REA and BSA LifeStructures), the precinct was re-engineered as an intense, urban multimodal concourse.
* **Pavement vs. Turf Ratio:** The immediate perimeter surrounding WALC (the west entry forecourt, north ME breezeway, and east Potter Quad) exhibits a **75% to 85% Hardscape / 15% to 25% Softscape ratio**.
* **Absence of Expansive Lawns:** There is **no continuous sweeping turf lawn** directly fronting the WALC. Unlike the Memorial Mall, the WALC forecourt is dominated by high-albedo architectural concrete paving bands and Purdue signature red clay brick paver fields engineered to support peak surges of 2,000+ students switching classes every 50 minutes, plus emergency/maintenance vehicular loads.
* **Bioretention & Rain Gardens:** The remaining 15–25% softscape consists of sunken, linear bioretention rain gardens, recessed stormwater filtration beds, and raised granite/limestone planters populated with native perennials, ornamental grasses, and specimen canopy trees (such as Sycamores and Honey Locusts).
* **Terrace Seatwalls:** Because the campus topography drops toward the east/southeast toward Potter, REA introduced extensive tiered rectilinear and curvilinear seatwalls:
  * **Coping Material:** Dressed Indiana Bedford Limestone (variegated), 16 to 20 inches (0.41–0.51 m) wide with chamfered edges.
  * **Wall Height:** 18 to 24 inches (0.46–0.61 m) above finished pavement grade.
  * **Wall Veneer:** Split-face limestone base or Flemish-bond dark brick matching WALC's lower masonry water-table.
  * **Function:** Acts as casual student seating, encloses grade-separated rain gardens, and prevents pedestrian compaction of root zones.

### 2.2 The Engineering Fountain (Class of 1939 Water Sculpture)
* **Center Coordinate:** `(X = 0.0, Y = 195.0)`.
* **Sculpture Massing:** 38 feet (11.58 m) tall; 228 tons of architectural precast white concrete; 4 sweeping parabolic vertical quarter-wings designed by Robert Youngman (1989).
* **Central Mirrored Safety Cylinder:** Added in 2001; 6.0 ft (1.83 m) diameter, 10.0 ft (3.05 m) height, fabricated from 316-grade mirror-polished stainless steel.
* **Dry-Deck Flush Drainage Basin:** No sunken standing pool. Features 4 concentric flush-to-grade stainless steel drainage grates (radii: 1.6 m, 3.1 m, 5.0 m, and 7.0 m).
* **Concentric Brick Ring:** Red clay brick paver ring extending from radius 7.5 m to 9.8 m, creating an overall circular fountain plaza with a **diameter of 64.3 feet (19.6 meters)**.

### 2.3 Promenade Network & Lawn Panel Geometry
* **Promenade Dimensions & Materials:**
  * **Central North-South Engineering Spine:** Broom-finished natural gray concrete, **20 to 24 feet (6.10 to 7.32 meters)** wide, expanding at node intersections up to **30 feet (9.14 meters)**. Edged with dark red brick soldier course pavers.
  * **Transverse East-West Promenades:** Connecting Hovde Hall east portico to MSEE west entrance: **16 to 20 feet (4.88 to 6.10 meters)** wide.
  * **South Promenade (to Centennial Mall / WALC):** **20 to 24 feet (6.10 to 7.32 meters)** wide, featuring expansive red paving stones.
  * **45-Degree Diagonal Spoke Walkways:** Width of **12 to 16 feet (3.66 to 4.88 meters)** radiating from the fountain plaza to building corners.
* **Geometric Shapes of the Lawn Panels:**
  1. **Immediate Fountain Quadrants (Forecourt):** Four symmetrical **curvilinear-truncated right trapezoids / quadrant wedges**. Each inner edge is concave (matching the 9.8 m radius brick circle), flanked laterally by diagonal and orthogonal concrete walks.
  2. **North Mall Lawn Parterres (Between Fountain and Armstrong Hall):** Two large, symmetrical **elongated rectangular parterres** measuring approximately **60 to 70 meters long by 22 to 28 meters wide (197–230 ft x 72–92 ft)**.
  3. **Edging & Enclosure:** Bordered by double allées of Honey Locust and Red Oak trees, low 18-inch (0.46 m) limestone seatwalls, and decorative sandstone piers.

## 3. Third Street Promenade (Student Success Corridor)

### 3.1 Total Building-to-Building Corridor Width
Third Street was converted in 2020–2021 from a 2-lane vehicular roadway with parking into a dedicated pedestrian and bicycle mall spanning from Martin Jischke Drive eastward to University Street.

Detailed laser-accurate corridor measurements across key segments:

  * Krach south facade (`Y = +6.0 m`) to Windsor Halls north facade (`Y = -24.6 m`): **30.6 meters (100.4 feet)**.
* **Segment B: Central Academic / Innovation Hub (Lawson / Bechtel vs. PGU / Hagle Hall):**

* **Segment C: Eastern Historic Core (Elliott Hall of Music vs. Psychological Sciences / Bell Tower / WALC):**

  * At WALC west facade junction: Opens into the **100+ foot wide** Centennial Mall concourse.

### 3.2 Paving Cross-Section & Multimodal Layout
* **1. Central Dedicated Cycle Track & Emergency Vehicle Way:**
  * **Width:** **20 to 24 feet (6.10 to 7.32 meters)**.
  * **Paving Material:** Smooth micro-surfaced asphalt treated with specialized high-durability acrylic coatings.
  * **Surface Branding & Graphics:** Embellished with prominent Purdue branding:
    * Gold-and-black **Motion "P"** emblems.
    * The **Boilermaker Special** locomotive silhouette.
    * The **Purdue Bell Tower** graphic.
    * The **"Every Giant Leap Starts with One Small Step"** inspirational typographic branding.
  * **Edge Delineation:** High-contrast white thermoplastic boundary striping and tactile raised rumble strips separate bike lanes from pedestrian paths.
* **2. Flanking Pedestrian Promenades:**
  * **North Walk:** **10 to 14 feet (3.05 to 4.27 meters)** wide.
  * **South Walk:** **10 to 14 feet (3.05 to 4.27 meters)** wide.
  * **Paving Material:** Broom-finished cast-in-place architectural concrete with sawcut 5x5 ft modular joints, edged with 2-foot decorative bands of Purdue red clay brick pavers (Flemish bond).
* **3. Concrete Curbs & Stormwater Bioswales:**
  * **Curbs:** Standard 6-inch (0.15 m) vertical concrete curbs are retained along outer bounds to separate pedestrian walks from adjacent rain garden bioswales.
  * **Linear Bioswales:** **6 to 10 feet (1.83 to 3.05 meters)** wide planted swales running parallel between walkways and building lawns to capture and filter stormwater runoff away from the critical campus utility tunnel network underneath.

### Key Modeling Takeaways for Blender Digital Twin Updates
1. **Centennial Mall / WALC:** Replaced flat green turf directly surrounding WALC with an expansive paved brick-and-concrete plaza (75–85% hardscape), inserted tiered 18–24" limestone-capped retaining seatwalls, and modeled discrete sunken bioretention beds rather than broad grass.
2. **Engineering Mall:** Set the central concrete spine width to **22 ft (6.7 m)**; surrounded the 19.6 m diameter flush fountain ring with 4 quadrant-wedge lawn panels, followed by symmetrical **200x80 ft rectangular lawn parterres** flanked by tree allées extending north to Armstrong Hall.
3. **Third Street:** Applied a total building-to-building corridor clearance of **100 to 117 feet (30.5 to 35.6 m)**, structured as:
   - 12 ft Concrete Pedestrian Sidewalk (North)
   - 6 ft Bioswale / Tree Buffer
   - 22 ft Central Asphalt Cycle Track with Purdue Branding & White Boundary Striping
   - 6 ft Bioswale / Amenity Buffer with Seatwalls
   - 12 ft Concrete Pedestrian Sidewalk (South).
