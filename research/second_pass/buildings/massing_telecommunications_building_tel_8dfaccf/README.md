# Telecommunications Building (TEL) — exterior second pass

**Research status:** exterior brief prepared with substantial gaps retained; provisional. **Scope:** research only. No Blender asset, builder, placement, or master scene was changed.

## Identity, date, and footprint

Purdue BuildingShapes feature **FID 199** identifies the target as the **Telecommunications Building (TEL)**, at **191 N University Street**, building code 1344. The October 30, 2026 feature response returns the same 29-vertex closed outline and attribute values as the October 26 audit snapshot. Its centroid is the queue's mapped location proxy, `(-280.8515, -153.3124) m`. The official Purdue campus map labels TEL at F7. OpenStreetMap way **159752232** independently names “Telecommunications Building (TEL)” and gives house number 191; the mapped outline is close to Purdue's ring and its `entrance=main` node 9975093914 is on the University Street side.

The two outlines agree well in plan: Purdue's GIS ring area is **606.28 m²**, OSM's is **584.35 m²**, centroid offset **0.96 m**, and boundary Hausdorff distance **1.45 m** under the documented local-campus transform. The official `Shape__Area` field, **1,048.03**, materially disagrees with both ring-derived areas. Retain the Purdue ring vertices for the plan and do not scale them to the attribute. These are map boundaries rather than a measured wall survey; the 29-vertex official ring includes short offsets that should not be regularized without a plan.

Purdue's GIS attributes report `Level_`, `NUM_LEVELS`, and `CountOfFLO` as **4**. The same record has `BUILT` in 1995 and `OCCUPIED` in 1996, and its `Extrution`, `R`, `Elevation`, and floor fields are present, but their units and derivation are undocumented. Treat the four-level count and dates as source-reported metadata, not a measured elevation. Purdue's October 2021 Space Report lists TEL / code 1344 at **19,044.26 sq ft** and 191 N University Street; this is a reported space total, not footprint area or height. An Archives and Special Collections vertical-file finding aid indexes “BUILDINGS – TELECOMMUNICATIONS (1996).” The Purdue University Architect records catalog an undated “Telecommunications Center Drawing” (UA 154, Series 1, Sub-Series 6, Item 69). The catalog result is accessible through search indexing, but the direct record endpoint returned HTTP 403 during this pass; no drawing content or date was inspected. The index is a retrieval lead, not an elevation source.

## Massing, roof, height, and materials

The official GIS outline is an elongated, slightly stepped footprint rather than a perfect rectangle. The 2018 north-up image shows a broad, low-slope/flat-looking roof and several small light-toned equipment forms. A cluster of roughly four larger rectangular units is visible on the east half of the roof, with smaller scattered rooftop objects; count and dimensions are approximate because of image softness and shadows. The orthophoto is the source for roof placement only. It does not establish parapet height, mechanical-screen walls, roof drainage or current equipment configuration.

An earlier audit segmented 2018 Indiana 3DEP COPC tile `in2018_29951885_12`: NAD83(HARN) Indiana West **ftUS**, EPSG:2968, vertical NAVD88 Geoid12B **ftUS**. It used ASPRS class 1 surface returns inside the footprint eroded by 3 m and class 2 ground returns in a 5 m exterior buffer. The source file records 1,450 interior class-1 and 1,640 ground returns, ground median 618.51 ftUS, and estimated surface-minus-ground height median **9.14 m**, p95 **10.56 m**, p99 **11.38 m**, maximum **11.44 m**. No class-6 building returns were available. Class-1 points are unclassified and can include residual trees/façade or other non-roof surfaces; this distribution is not a surveyed roof, eave, or ridge elevation. Keep it as a coarse 2018 overall-height constraint only. Do not convert four reported levels into equal storeys or treat the maximum as a roof edge.

The Street View reading supports red-to-brown brick on the North and East walls, pale horizontal masonry/concrete-looking courses at the north wall and upper wall transition, dark metal window frames, and a glazed stair/entry wall with a dark metal grid on the east. These are image-based material families, not specifications. Sun, shade, reflections and the August 2019 capture can shift their apparent color. The north wall's lower glazing is visually darker than the upper windows; do not infer tinted glass, exact mullion profile, or material finish from the thumbnail alone. No façade schedule, brick bond/specification, window schedule or roof specification was located.

The apparent stair inside the east glass volume is visible but its precise enclosure, connection and floor count need elevation or plan confirmation. The paved entrance approach, landscape planters, adjacent garage, curb and campus trees are site/context elements and should not be incorporated into the building mass without separate evidence.

## Evidence-led future-builder brief

Retain the official FID 199 ring as the provisional footprint, noting the Shape__Area conflict and the close OSM check. Make the north wall distinct from the east entry wall: the north view has a broad lower glazed band beneath a brick header and a small upper-window group; the east view has the glass stair/entry bay and paired ground-level doors against a brick wall with separate upper windows. Carry the stepped outline and roof equipment locations only as approximate plan observations from the 2018 orthophoto. A broad flat roof is supported; precise parapets, slopes, screens, wall heights and machine dimensions are not. Preserve the reported four-level count as a constraint with unknown datum. The 9.14 m LiDAR median is an uncertain historic overall-height estimate, not an eave or floor-height target. Use red/brown brick, light horizontal courses, dark frames and the glazed stair bay as photographed material zones, not measured color values. Keep the obscured north edge, complete south/west elevations, all unshown windows and hidden services explicitly unresolved; do not propagate the visible north schedule around the building.
