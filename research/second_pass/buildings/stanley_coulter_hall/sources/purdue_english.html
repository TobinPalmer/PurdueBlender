Our Mission

At Purdue English, you'll learn to think critically, communicate effectively, explore new ideas, and see the world from different perspectives. Whether you're passionate about literature, writing, language, or digital media, you'll develop skills that help you succeed in your career, connect with others, and navigate an ever-changing world.

An English degree is about more than reading and writing. It's about learning how to think, create, communicate, and solve problems. In our classes, you'll develop skills you can use in any field, including:

  • Reading and Writing: Learn to understand complex ideas, discover meaning in texts, and communicate your thoughts clearly and confidently.
  • Creative Thinking: Explore new perspectives, make connections across cultures and history, and use storytelling and imagination to approach challenges in new ways.
  • Research and Analysis: Ask thoughtful questions, evaluate information, recognize patterns, and bring ideas together to develop meaningful insights.
  • Digital Communication: Explore visual and digital media and learn how to share your ideas effectively with different audiences.

At Purdue English, you'll build the skills to adapt, think independently, and make a difference wherever your interests and career take you.
