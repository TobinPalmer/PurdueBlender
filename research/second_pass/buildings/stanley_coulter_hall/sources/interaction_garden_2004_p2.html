We can do no
great things -
only small things
with great love.

Mother Teresa

Spirit. In every language,
in every time and every
place, the word has a ring
of strength and determination.
It's the hero's strength,
the mother's resilience,
and the poor man's armor.
It cannot be broken,
and it cannot be taken away.

R.A. Salvatore

To northwest facing Stanley Coulter Hall.

Nothing pains
some people more
than having to think.

Martin Luther King

Happiness is inward
and not outward;
and so it does not
depend on what
we have,
but on what we are.

Henry van Dyke

Looking north towards the Purdue Bell Tower behind trees in the distance, slightly left of center, with Stanley Coulter Hall on the left.

Sometimes you just
have to take the leap,
and build your wings
on the way down.

Kobi Yamada

We think too small,
like the frog at the bottom
of the well. He thinks the
sky is only as big as
the top of the well.
If he surfaced, he would
have an entirely different view.

Mao Tse-Tung
