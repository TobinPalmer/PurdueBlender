## Scope and method

Complete a bounded second-pass exterior evidence review. Separate inspected as-built photographs from historic views, campus maps, GIS outlines, drawings, renderings and model-derived evidence. Record confidence and gaps. Historic sources can support the building’s architectural vocabulary but cannot by themselves establish the current exterior condition or every present-day opening.

## Initial source leads

- [Purdue Archives photo challenge, “From the Archives: Photo Challenge”](https://blogs.lib.purdue.edu/news/2017/03/27/from-the-archives-photo-challenge/): a 1924 archival exterior image is already preserved in the prior audit folder. The image appears to show the south tower/entry, flanking wings, steep slate roofs and gabled dormers shortly after construction; trees are not the main occlusion, but the date makes later additions/alterations a risk. Source-page context cites groundbreaking in 1922, laying the cornerstone November 25, 1922, and opening September 9, 1924.
- [Purdue Stories centenary feature](https://stories.purdue.edu/topic/the-purdue-memorial-union-turns-100/): the prior audit folder holds a 2024 frontal south-entry image. It supports the brick tower, pale carved stone arch, multi-light vertical glazing, flanking sculptural figures and ornate portal. The image is tightly framed; it does not show complete wings or side/rear faces. The article reports major ground-floor renovation in 2022; that interior work is not evidence of an exterior change.
- Purdue Archives GIS layer `Purdue/campus/MapServer/0`, queued feature 644; historic feature IDs in the prior audit are 278 (1935–36), 472 (1929), 482 (1938–40), and 292 (1953–55 PMU Club/hotel expansion). Existing evidence describes converted historical map outlines and an approximate match only; no surveyed dimensions should be inferred from them.
- Existing cited source notes are in `audit/purdue_memorial_union_refine/build_evidence.md` and `audit/purdue_memorial_union_independent_grade/source_evidence.md`. They are leads for this review, not a substitute for rechecking the source images and their limits.
