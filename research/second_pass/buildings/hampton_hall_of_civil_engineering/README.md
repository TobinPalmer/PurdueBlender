## Identity, phases and exterior condition

Purdue's Lyles School identifies the building as Delon and Elizabeth Hampton Hall of Civil Engineering at 550 Stadium Mall Drive. The 2012 naming article identifies the donor naming and states that the Civil Engineering Building was built in three phases, **1950, 1961 and 1986**. It does not map those years to present-day wings or give phase elevations. Treat the existing complex as a joined multi-phase mass; do not assign a visible wall to a construction phase from style alone.

The intended reference condition is the present exterior as shown in the Purdue Fall 2024 school article image (H1 below). The first renovation phase began in fall 2017 and was reported completed in December 2018. Purdue describes work on the basement and ground levels, including an accessible entryway and lab improvements. The 2017 drawing set is explicitly an interior renovation package, not an exterior elevation. A separate 2024 room 4247 phase-2 project manual is a later interior fit-out lead; its title page does not establish an exterior change. No inspected source indicates a replacement of the main facade after the Fall 2024 photograph.

The inherited first-pass record says the official FID 272 ring matches the saved shell to within 0.00001472 m in the project conversion, but that is a reproduction check against the same Purdue map source, not an independent survey. It also flags the central pale-stone/glazed bay as missing from the previous model and states that the source image cannot be registered to a particular GIS face. This pass supports that omission as a genuine architectural feature while leaving its compass face unresolved.

This is enough to reject a single uniform facade plane but not enough to assign the full outline's wings. The whole-building plan, court/lightwell condition, roof-step map, and adjacent-building boundaries remain unverified by a current aerial or plan overlay in this pass.

## Floors, vertical evidence and datasets

Purdue's Building Emergency Plan (adopted 2008, revised 2015; source URL and downloaded PDF retained in the first-pass audit) describes six floors: basement, ground, main/first, second, third and fourth. This is a level inventory, not proof of five wholly exposed above-grade stories or of equal heights. H1 shows a taller central glazed composition and step/roof lines, but its viewpoint is insufficient to count every level around the full building. The inherited audit's main wall height (17.25 m) and roof penthouse (18.25 m total) are explicitly schematic estimates, not retained source measurements; do not promote them to real-building constraints.

The first-pass source record reports the campus GIS field `Extrution=7` but excludes it as a height measurement because the service gives no field dictionary, units, or datum. Indiana's public 2016–2020 statewide USGS 3DEP collection is available as QL2 LAS/DEM products; the archived Indiana program documentation describes 2 pulses/m² point clouds, while Purdue iDiF provides a discovery/download interface. Those are regional availability facts, not proof of which tile covers FID 272 or its flight date. The statewide collection includes multiple years, so a tile-specific date must be queried from Indiana's detailed acquisition-date service. Published access paths differ: Indiana county deliveries are described in State Plane East/West, while the statewide web/EPT product documentation discusses Web Mercator and NAVD88/geoid products. Do not assign a CRS, vertical datum, acquisition date or resolution to a Hampton clip until the actual tile metadata is inspected. No Hampton-specific point cloud or elevation clip is retained in this pass. The next pass should select the FID 272 tile, retain its LAS metadata, inspect point classes and vertical units, and separate ground returns from roof/equipment/vegetation; point-return heights must not be described as surveyed wall heights.
