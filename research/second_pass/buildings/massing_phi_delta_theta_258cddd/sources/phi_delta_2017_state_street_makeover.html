State Street Makeover Underway

The latest and most intensive phase yet of the State Street Redevelopment Project began on May 15, and we’re already seeing some dramatic changes from our view at 503 W. State Street!

The project will transform State Street into an attractive and easy-to-navigate city point of pride–including tree-lined sidewalks, bicycle paths, public art, and more.

With many students home for the summer, a large portion of State Street (from Salisbury Street to McCormick Road) has been fully closed for construction, with several intersecting streets also having closings or restrictions. In these new photos taken out front of our house that show the entire road removed, you can already get a sense of the scope of the changes being made to the backbone of West Lafayette.

The summer phase of the project is scheduled to end on August 13 in time for students to return, and the next phase will continue through November. The entire project is expected to be completed by the end of 2018, in time for Purdue’s 150-year anniversary.

Buildings along State Street—including the front yard of the Phi Delta Theta house—are currently fenced off, but pedestrians are still allowed access to campus and to all businesses in the area. In fact, the Project’s FAQ page encourages everyone to still visit their favorite shops, restaurants and other businesses, since they will need business more than ever during the construction.

Though it’s bittersweet to see big changes to the street we all know and love, the Redevelopment Project is sure to bring great things to West Lafayette, Purdue, and even PDT.  You can check out the project details and community benefits pages to learn more and see renderings.

For the full timeline of construction and road closings, see the 2017 Vehicular Traffic Plan / Schedule.
