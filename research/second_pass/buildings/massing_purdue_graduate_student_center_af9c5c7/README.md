**Identity:** Purdue Graduate Student Center (PGSC), Purdue GIS FID 526, 504 Northwestern Avenue. It is a low office/student-center portion of the Northwestern Avenue Parking Garage, not a freestanding building.

## Identity, chronology, and boundary

Purdue’s current PGSC page identifies the facility at 504 Northwestern Avenue, gives its student organization and service use, and describes several rooms including a basement study area (P1). Purdue Archives says this portion of the Northwestern Avenue Parking Garage was originally the Visitor Information Center; that center moved to Purdue Memorial Union in January 2013, after which the portion became the PGSC. The retained Archives record lists the Visitor Information Center name for 1985–2013 and PGSC from 2013 onward (H1). Purdue for Life says the Visitor Information Center opened on the garage’s ground floor in 1985 (H2), while Purdue’s historical timeline says it was completed in 1986 (H3). A separate Archives finding-aid search result describes 1989 “new parking garage construction” photos and another record titled “Parking Garage Number 7, 1989”; those pages/PDFs were blocked from direct retrieval and their relation to the present garage is not established (H5–H6). These dates are retained as a chronology conflict, not collapsed into one asserted construction date.

The spatial boundary is critical. Purdue GIS FID 526 names PGSC and outlines a nine-vertex low center portion. A direct query for the Northwestern Avenue Parking Garage returns FID 550, with a much larger 26-vertex footprint, seven listed levels and the same street address (G1–G3). Under the project’s approximate local coordinate conversion, nearly all of the smaller FID 526 polygon falls inside the FID 550 polygon (about 99.999% by area; a roughly 0.005 m² numerical sliver lies outside). The north-up aerial overlay shows the small PGSC outline within the broad garage footprint/roof field (A1–A2). This supports a nested shared-volume interpretation: the PGSC is a lower occupied portion of the larger garage, not a separate object with a complete, detached roof. The 2025 Street View confirms open garage decks above the brick center frontage (SV60/SV90). The assigned PGSC asset should cover the student-center portion only; garage decks, ramps and garage structure belong to the host-garage scope. Exact structural ownership at the slab, column and wall junction is not visible in these sources.

The intended reference condition is the current use as PGSC, with its student-center name and entry frontage. The official header photo is stored in a 2025/09 upload directory, but the image capture date and photographer are not stated (V1). Street View is dated August 2025 in Google Maps imagery metadata (S1, SV*). No source found here documents a post-2013 exterior renovation or maps which 1985/86/89 components remain at each wall.

## GIS outline and dimensional constraints

The FID 526 query was requested as GeoJSON in WGS84; the layer metadata establishes native EPSG:3857 / WKID 102100, latest WKID 3857 (G1–G2). The ring has ten coordinates including closure, or nine distinct vertices. Applying the project’s local approximation—`x=(lon+86.9137)×111412.84×cos(40.4274°)`, `y=(lat−40.4274)×111040`—gives an approximate envelope **31.269 m east–west × 27.154 m north–south**, local polygon area **555.446 m²**, and perimeter **96.645 m** (A4). These are transformed map-outline measures, not surveyed exterior wall dimensions.

FID 526 raw `Shape__Area=960.184` and `Shape__Length=127.047` do not match local ground-scale values. Reprojecting the WGS84 ring back to the layer’s Web Mercator plane reproduces the larger source-plane figures; the local values are reduced by Web Mercator’s latitude scale factor. Therefore the raw area/length must not be treated as ground m²/metres. FID 526 also reports `NUM_LEVELS=2`, `CountOfFLO=2`, `Extrution=12`, `Elevation=622`, and `Floorplans=G:\GIS\web_pages\html_pages\VIC.html`; the schema supplies no vertical units or datum, and the campus-local floorplan path is unavailable. The PGSC page’s basement-study-area description could explain a two-level interior while only one low occupied frontage band is visible, but does not prove the GIS interpretation. Do not convert `Extrution=12` or `Elevation=622` to building metres.

FID 550’s transformed garage footprint is about **139.833 × 94.554 m**, with local plan area around **8,293.227 m²** (A4). Its raw level count is seven and `Extrution=84`; units/datum remain undocumented, so neither establishes a measured deck height. The outline comparison proves plan containment only, not which exposed garage columns, slabs or exterior walls should be attached to the PGSC model at any height.

## Materials, massing, roof, and dimensional interpretation

The visible center frontage is primarily dark red to brown brick with light mortar, a deep dark storefront zone, pale block lettering, a pale canopy/soffit and pale base/curb details (V1, SV60/SV90). V1’s direct sun makes the mortar and raised letters high-contrast; the Street View view is more diffuse and compressed. Brick bond is visibly running bond in the near zone, but mortar dimensions and brick course size are only approximate at this image resolution. Dark glazing is reflective; black appearance is partly shade and partly glass/graphic film, not proof that all panes are opaque. No finish schedule establishes brick product, mortar color, lettering material, frame alloy, canopy concrete/metal, soffit finish or exact color. Planters, banners/posters behind glass, curb, signage on garage, light poles, traffic and tree planting are not shell geometry.

The PGSC’s external shape cannot be treated as a normal freestanding box: the FID 526 polygon lies almost completely inside the footprint of a seven-level Northwestern Garage, and Street View shows garage decks continuing above its low brick front. The center’s roof and outer side walls are occluded or structurally shared. Aerial roof pixels inside FID526 belong to/are obscured by the host garage deck, not evidence for a separate PGSC rooftop. No inspected source supports eave, parapet, basement exposure, garage soffit, entry canopy or floor-to-floor dimensions. The PGSC official page mentions a basement study area; the visible exterior photo has one low storefront/covered frontage band, and GIS records two levels. These facts are consistent with a basement plus ground floor, but that relationship is an inference rather than a measured section.

## Existing asset discrepancy checklist and builder constraints

The existing model and audit are implementation evidence only, not independent proof of the building (see `audit/purdue_graduate_student_center/evidence.json` and `evidence.md`). The asset creates a low 3.65 m shell from FID526 and uses a feet-based interpretation of GIS `Extrution=12`; the audit says the remaining garage decks are handled by a separate garage asset/cutout interface. That height is explicitly an estimate, not a source dimension. The survey-like results from this pass modify/qualify the exterior brief:

- **Footprint nesting:** FID526 is essentially contained in larger garage FID550. The two footprints overlap in plan; their vertical separation, beam/slab breaks and facade ownership require architectural sections and cannot be inferred from the model’s Boolean interface.
- **Visible wall family:** retain the low red/brown brick sign band, pale raised name, deep shaded storefront/entry, brick piers and continuous low canopy as the only source-backed PGSC exterior family. V1 supports this wall; it does not support extending the same frontage treatment around all polygon sides.
- **Upper openings:** open deck bays in SV60/SV90 are garage openings and must not be copied onto the PGSC shell. Do not treat the contextual concrete deck facade as an unverified PGSC upper story.
- **Vertical dimensions:** the model’s 3.65 m wall height, master 4.5 m cutout level and undocumented FID526 `Extrution=12` are not measured PGSC heights. Existing integration data can help preserve the project’s component boundary but cannot resolve the real as-built section.
- **Interior:** room placement/furniture in the asset is schematic and is outside this exterior brief. The official page’s programs only constrain function; it does not provide a measured room plan.

**Builder constraints from this pass:** keep the PGSC identity limited to the low portion at the garage frontage; do not build an independent roof above it unless a section establishes one; preserve the asymmetrical signed header/storefront and covered pier-bay sequence only on the observed face; keep the open parking decks with the garage component; and leave the unseen side/rear/basement openings, full sign/canopy details, height and all structural junctions unknown. No model, builder, placement, owner assignment or master scene was changed during this research-only pass.
