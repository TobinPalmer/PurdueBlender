# 1. Wilmeth Active Learning Center (WALC): exterior research

Research prepared 2026-10-28. **Prepared with explicit coverage gaps; no model changed or accepted.** Stable asset `walc`, `modular/buildings/walc.blend`; future second-pass owner Rossen. The Thomas S. and Harvey D. Wilmeth Active Learning Center is at 340 Centennial Mall Drive, beside the Bell Tower. The tower is a separate asset, not a WALC roof feature. Purdue announced the building's public opening for August 7, 2017. Turner describes three above-ground stories and a basement. Use these identity references alongside the footprint rather than inferring floor count from large reading-room windows. ## Materials and color matching

Use V01, V02, V04, V05 and V09 together: red/brown variegated brick; pale warm cream stone; dark gray/brown eaves and brackets; muted olive-gray projecting metal frames; red/orange roof tiles; blue-gray reflective glass. These are visual descriptions, not measured reflectance or fixed RGB samples. West afternoon sun, north shade, foliage reflections and V06's blue-hour lighting produce materially different apparent colors.

Future matching should compare equivalent sun/shade patches from several daylight cameras, keep brick/stone/metal/roof materials separate, and match glass tint without painting reflected trees into the facade. Preserve the herringbone masonry and stone relief where they affect appearance. Neutral daylight, roughness and normal detail should be reviewed from near and far views before final color approval. No calibrated color target or physical material specification was found.

## Footprint, placement and measured elevation evidence

Purdue campus GIS identifies WALC as **FID906**. [Saved feature](sources/purdue_footprint_fid906.json) retains WGS84 rings, while [shared provenance](../../shared/location_source_provenance.json) identifies the repository-cached snapshot and hash. It is not a newly surveyed 2026 footprint. The manifest origin is approximately project XY `(28.930004, -1.219994)` metres. Preserve the stable asset/placement contract; no placement was changed during this research.

Indiana's [state LiDAR date index](https://gisdata.in.gov/server/rest/services/Indiana_Statewide_3DEP_Lidar_Dates/MapServer) returns tiles **in2018_29951885_12** and **in2018_30001885_12**, dated **April 20, 2018**—after WALC opened. [Query URLs](sources/geodata_query_urls.json), [tile results](sources/lidar_delivery_tiles.json), [crop/source metadata](sources/lidar_crop_summary.json) and original COPC metadata are preserved. Indiana's public elevation archive is listed as CC0 in the [AWS dataset registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/in-elevation/). Its newer statewide acquisition program does not prove that a newer WALC tile is available.

Both actual tiles were spatially queried through COPC at full available resolution, using the footprint envelope plus approximately 10 m. The portable regular-LAZ clips in [data/lidar](data/lidar/) retain 42,675 points total: 2,646 west/plaza points and 40,029 body-tile points. The west clip contains no points inside the building footprint; it is site/ground context. Raw tile URLs and clip SHA256 hashes are in the crop metadata. Full regional point clouds were not downloaded or committed.

- Actual header CRS: **NAD83(HARN) / Indiana West (ftUS) + NAVD88 height — Geoid12B (ftUS)**. The embedded horizontal source is EPSG2968 inside a bound CRS; the full WKT is retained. Vertical component is EPSG6360. Use the actual WKT rather than substituting NAD83(2011).
- Horizontal and vertical native units are **US survey feet**, converted by **0.30480060960121924 m/ftUS**. The building's scene Z origin is not NAVD88 elevation.
- Classes are 1 (unclassified), 2 (ground), and 7 (noise). **There are no class-6 building returns.** Roof candidates cannot be selected by assuming all class-1 points are a building.
- A 1 m inward footprint buffer contains 18,289 class-1 returns. Nearby outside-footprint class-2 median ground is 622.19 ft NAVD88 (189.644 m). Candidate height percentiles above that reference are **18.834 m (p50), 20.979 m (p90), 22.001 m (p99)**. These are return statistics, not certified eave/ridge heights; vegetation, roof objects, registration and ground choice require inspection. [Calculation notes](lidar_measurement_notes.json).
- The [point-observation plan](lidar_plan_observations.png) plots measured returns against the footprint; it is not a reconstructed model. Airborne density supports roof/massing investigation, not facade/window placement. A candidate USGS 2017 EPT catalog in the shared source folder was investigated but was **not** used for these measurements.

## Coverage gaps and next evidence to obtain

West has close, medium and far views from opposing obliques; north has useful oblique detail; south/east have a partially resolving corner view. Elevated and overhead sources resolve roof arrangement. **East/service recesses, complete north/south frontal opening schedules, current roof equipment, and close eave/corner details still need more photography.** None of the camera heights or viewing distances is survey-controlled.

Before a future model pass, acquire east-facing views from several positions and a low/upward corner series, a north/south frontal bay sequence, and recent elevated images where publicly available. Record absent access rather than inventing features. Segment the 2018 roof returns and compare them with dated aerials before deriving roof heights. Reconcile changes since 2017/2018 using recent views; keep old construction landscaping out of current geometry.
