    Bozho (Bodéwadmik), Hé (Lenape), Aloha (Hawaiian), Aanii (Anishinnabe), Yá’át’ééh (Diné), Greetings

    Since 2007, the Native American Educational and Cultural Center (NAECC) serves as a second home for the Purdue community. We are committed to nurturing student learning and academic success, we provide a welcome space for students, and we offer educational opportunities through engagement programs and events.

    We NAECC proudly represents over 70 tribal nations through our Native American, Alaska Native, Native Hawaiian, Pacific Islander students, alumni, faculty, and staff.

    The NAECC is open to all students and the entire campus throughout the year. Stop by for a visit, check out our upcoming programs and events, or attend office hours with our campus partners.
