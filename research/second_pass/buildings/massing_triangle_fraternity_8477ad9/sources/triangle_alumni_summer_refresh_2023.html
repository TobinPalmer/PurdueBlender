Alumni Organization Of Purdue Triangle is organizing this fundraiser.
Help the brothers of Triangle Fraternity at Purdue by contributing to the Summer Refresh of the Chapter house on Purdue’s campus.

This fund raiser is being run by the Alumni Organization of Purdue Chapter of Triangle. The Alumni Board will receive these funds and spend them on materials and contractors that will update the Triangle Fraternity House at 103 University Street in West Lafayette during the summer of 2023.

The updates to the house will include painting, flooring, light fixtures, drinking fountains, plumbing fixtures, new furniture, and other improvements. For more information please visit PurdueTriangle.com.
