Purdue University Hall Renovations – Indiana

Purdue University’s historic hall has been fully renovated, seamlessly integrating 19th-century architecture with modern academic functionality. Originally built in the late 1800s, the updated facility now serves today’s students with revitalized interiors while preserving its timeless exterior.

Waukegan Steel contributed custom structural steel for higher education to support the renovation—reinforcing the building’s framework without compromising its historical integrity. The completed project reflects a successful balance between preservation and progress.
