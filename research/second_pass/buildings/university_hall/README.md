Prepared 2026-10-28 local time; download timestamps also record UTC. **Prepared with explicit coverage gaps; no model changed or accepted.** Stable ID `university_hall`, source `modular/buildings/university_hall.blend`, second-pass owner Rossen. ## Identity and reference era

University Hall was dedicated in November 1877, according to Purdue's [History Department](https://www.cla.purdue.edu/academic/history/about/univ.html), and remains the surviving building from the original six-building campus. The [September 2025 CLA report](https://www.cla.purdue.edu/news/college/2025/university-hall-renovation-and-preservation.html) says the renovated building reopened for classes, with an October 25 dedication planned. This establishes a **post-2025 exterior target**, subject to verification of later changes. Earlier street views and 2018 LiDAR cannot certify the renovated condition.

[MSKTD's project page](https://msktd.com/project/university-hall/) identifies the design team and completed renovation, with an exterior photograph among predominantly interior photographs. [Purdue's June 30, 2025 story](https://stories.purdue.edu/restoring-purdues-university-hall-as-a-campus-crown-jewel/) discusses restoration and structural work through the roof and at the foundation. These sources explain why historical views must be reconciled; a preserved-looking shell is not proof that entrances, glazing, roof components and accessibility remained identical.

## Features that determine the exterior

| Part | Evidence-backed observations | Instruction for a future builder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall composition | U01/U02/U06–U08 show a long brick body with projecting vertical bays, differing end walls, a steep central mansard zone and an offset taller tower/spire. The rear view is visibly different from the east frontage. | Locate tower and projections from plan/overhead and multiple views. Do not center a generic spire or mirror east doors/stairs onto the west. |
| East middle bays | U08 clearly shows **four round-headed upper windows** in the recessed central field, with a corresponding narrower rectangular/segmental-headed row below. Larger paired-light openings occupy the flanking projections. | Count all stories and end bays separately; four verified central upper openings are not the complete building window schedule. Preserve differing head shapes rather than one repeated rectangular cutout. |
| Window heads and relief | U01 resolves cream stepped/segmented hood outlines around curved and shouldered openings, projecting sills with small supports, brick arches and white/light sash frames. Some lower openings have straight stone lintels; tower openings have pointed/gabled outlines. | Model reveal, sill and hood depth. Keep actual glazing divisions separate from a window's outer decorative arch. Do not turn the whole facade into Gothic pointed arches. |
| Entrances and grade | U08 shows **two separated east stair approaches**, raised door portals, black rails and a low railed planting/lightwell edge. U05 shows a west doorway closer to the rear grade and differing surroundings. | Verify current 2025–2026 doors, ramps and stair layout before using old frames. Do not invent a single central front porch or copy the east stairs across all faces. |
| Cornice | U01/U02 show a broad pale projecting eave/cornice with repeated ornate paired/bracket forms and corner clusters. Brick bands and courses emphasize floor levels and projections. | Preserve brackets and shadow depth at both close and campus-scale views; a flat white stripe omits important relief. |
| Mansard and dormers | Dark gray, small slate-like/scalloped pieces with repeated lighter diamond/floral-like motifs; rounded white dormer surrounds, larger roof-bay forms at projections and two smaller central east dormers visible in U08. | Trace roof breaks and dormer positions from all sources. Physical roof material/specification and motif counts remain unverified; avoid a smooth single hip or generic black roof. |
| Tower and spire | U02 shows a brick gabled tower, paired tall pointed/gabled lights, pale gable trim, open/louvered dormer-like vents around a steep slender spire and a decorative metal finial. | Separate tower brick, trim, vent blades, steep roof and finial. These small features strongly affect the silhouette; avoid an opaque block for each louvered opening. |
| Foundation and services | Rough pale stone basement, smaller low windows, downpipes, wall-mounted entrance lanterns and changes of grade are visible across U01/U06/U07. | Distinguish foundation texture/scale from upper brick. Record concealed service details as gaps rather than adding repeated arbitrary vents. |

## Materials and color matching

Compare U01/U02 with shaded U05 and the independent 2020 U04 baseline: warm red/brown variegated brick, light warm cream trim and sash, rough pale-gray basement stone, dark gray patterned roof and black/dark metal rails/downpipes. Low autumn sun warms the stone and brick considerably; overcast/shaded sources look cooler. No calibrated color target, reflectance measurement or roof-manufacturer finish schedule was obtained.

Use several lighting conditions to separate material color from illumination. Preserve the lighter roof motifs, window trim/head profiles, mortar scale and rough foundation; do not paint all pale elements one untextured white or match the roof solely from an aerial pixel. U03's temporary construction colors and U05's saturated panorama sky are not calibrated material references.

## GIS and actual LiDAR evidence

[Purdue GIS FID213](sources/purdue_footprint.json) retains the WGS84 ring and attributes; [shared source provenance](../../shared/location_source_provenance.json) identifies the repository-cached snapshot and project transformation. This is not a new 2026 survey or an as-built renovation outline. Preserve asset identity and placement; no manifest, builder or master was changed. The relationship to Oval Drive on the east and the west lawn/neighboring halls helps register views without merging nearby buildings into this asset.

Indiana's [state LiDAR date index](https://gisdata.in.gov/server/rest/services/Indiana_Statewide_3DEP_Lidar_Dates/MapServer) selects **in2018_29951885_12**, dated **April 20, 2018**. [Tile result](sources/lidar_delivery_tiles.json), COPC metadata, [crop statistics](sources/lidar_crop_summary.json), [exact requests](sources/site_download_index.json), and [portable measured-point crop](data/lidar/in2018_29951885_12.university_hall_clip.laz) are retained. The full-resolution spatial query returned **13,636 points**, including 4,185 inside the footprint. This is point data, not a reconstructed model.

- Actual compound CRS: **NAD83(HARN) / Indiana West (ftUS) + NAVD88 height, Geoid12B (ftUS)**; full WKT retained. Horizontal source EPSG:2968 inside a bound CRS; vertical EPSG:6360. Native XYZ are **US survey feet**, conversion **0.30480060960121924 m/ftUS**.
- Classes: 7,043 unclassified (1), 6,549 ground (2), 44 noise (7), **no class-6 building returns**. Unclassified returns are not automatically certified roof points.
- Outside-footprint ground median is **623.42 ft NAVD88**. A 1 m eroded footprint contains 3,367 class-1 returns; p50/p90/p99 heights above that ground are **19.971 / 21.866 / 25.804 m**. These are return-distribution statistics, not floor/eave/ridge dimensions. In particular, a high percentile is not the slender finial's total height.
- Segment roof, tower, spire and vegetation before using this cloud to constrain geometry; inspect registration and ground datum. The 2018 scan predates 2023–2025 works and cannot establish present roof/entrance modifications or facade mullions.

Indiana's public elevation archive is [listed as CC0](https://registry.opendata.aws/in-elevation/). A statewide newer acquisition program does not prove a newer University Hall tile is available. Reproduction is documented in `tools/collect_site_evidence.py` with actual source WKT and recorded hashes.

## Remaining evidence gaps

The dossier includes near/frontal, opposing corner, west-lawn, overhead and actual LiDAR evidence, plus contemporary project photos. **Current west/south/north close views after the 2025 renovation, a measured complete window schedule, elevated oblique roof photographs, exact finial/roof profiles and confirmed current accessibility/service details remain missing.** No surveyed camera heights or calibrated material samples were found.

A publicly indexed 2024 envelope-repair project-manual URL was investigated but returned 404; no uninspected plan/specification is represented as acquired evidence. Current repair/as-built drawings would improve roof/material and opening verification. Keep historic dates, temporary construction conditions and current project photos separate, and leave unseen details unverified.
