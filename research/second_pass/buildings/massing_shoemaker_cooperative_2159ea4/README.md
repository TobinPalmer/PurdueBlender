## Identity, building chronology, footprint, and reference phase

Purdue’s official campus GIS record FID 800 names `SOSH / Shoemaker`, address **351 Northwestern Av**, owner field `COOP`, and a ten-unique-corner polygon (G1). The university FSCL current cooperative-house roster links the Shoemaker site, and the operator’s current home page gives the same address and residence program; the operator’s home and history pages remain publicly active in the October 2026 retrieval (I2, I3). A 2024 FCSLA Life article caption independently names Shoemaker and describes the pictured participants on its front porch and balcony (V2). These sources establish the modeled target as the present 351 Northwestern house rather than a prior Shoemaker residence.

The cooperative organization chronology must not be mistaken for the building’s age: the operator says the association began as Twin Pines in fall 1938, occupied a second house on Andrew Place from 1966–1993, then moved to 351 Northwestern in 1994 (I1). The target structure’s construction date, architect and pre-1994 use are still unknown. Purdue Archives’ 1984 “Shoemaker Cooperative House” negatives predate that move and are organizational/history leads only, not photographs of the current asset (H1). West Lafayette’s 2020 downtown plan names the current address as an important structure encouraged to be saved, rehabilitated and reused; this is a planning recommendation, not proof of formal designation or a construction chronology (C1).

Fresh October 30, 2026 ArcGIS query FID 800 returns the same 10-vertex polygon as the October 28 raw query: all 11 closed-ring coordinates match exactly. Its source geometry has published area **498.85 m²**, perimeter **105.42 m** and project-local minimum rotated rectangle **23.07 × 17.02 m** at estimated axis 128.54° east-counterclockwise (G1, G2; see audit measurements). Those are source/map computations, not wall-survey dimensions or a facade azimuth. The nearest source record attributes contradict each other: `NUM_LEVELS=1`, `CountOfFLO=1`, and `PU_Occupie=N`, while `Level_=8`, `Extrution=17` (units undefined), the 2016 photo shows two above-grade window rows, the operator page identifies a “Floor Two” photo tour, and the live Purdue FSCL/operator listings describe current cooperative housing. Treat those GIS fields as unreliable occupancy/floor counts; do not claim they establish one floor, vacancy or exact height.

### Massing, roof, features, and materials

The photographed broad wall is a two-storey house-scale gable with a wide roof overhang and a shallow horizontal eave return. The one dominant gable roof is visible, with a second roof slope/ridge line behind it at the left shoulder and lower roof line over the porch corner. The ten-corner GIS footprint and the angled porch-side view indicate an irregular/returned plan rather than a simple rectangle, but no source proves the location or length of each roof wing, valley, rear extension or chimney. The model’s estimated ridge axis 128.54° is derived from the GIS minimum rotated rectangle, not from a geotagged photograph; true ridge bearing is unresolved. No dormers are visible in the documented broad gable, but the hidden roof planes have not been checked for dormers or equipment.

The upper porch/balcony is the distinctive depth feature: a projecting upper gallery at the building corner, with simple square posts, vertical balusters, a broad fascia/edge beam and shallow gable/eave cover; a stair with railings meets grade diagonally. The main roof has a modest overhang and visible soffit/fascia, with a gutter and downspout at the corner. The photograph also shows three round gable vents, paired dark shutters, exterior window AC boxes, a subtle horizontal line at the floor level, small foundation lights, and planting. The horizontal wall line may be a course, joint or finish change; source does not identify it. Treat sign, AC boxes, landscaping, utility wires/pole, neighboring buildings, freestanding walkways and stone entry marker as site/movable/service elements unless fixed architectural attachment is independently verified.

V1 is grayscale and V2 is small color reproduction with varied light; neither supports reliable wall hue, siding/plaster type, roof material/color or trim color. The wall reads smooth and light-toned with simple light window casings; the roof appears dark, and shutters dark. These are photographic appearance only. V1 shows distinct sash divisions and AC units, but not frame construction, exact window product, shutter operation, cladding joint/bond, mortar, base finish or porch material. No building specifications or finish schedule were located. Do not label the facade “white stucco” or the roof “asphalt shingle” as verified facts.

## Evidence-led future exterior brief and discrepancy check

For a separately authorized later exterior task, retain the official ten-corner footprint as a map constraint pending survey verification; separate the broad two-level gable volume from the corner upper porch/gallery and adjacent roof/wing; keep the strong three-bay-per-floor gable composition with its wider paired center sash, dark shutters and triangular 3-vent cluster; represent the projecting porch, open stair and lower foundation only to the extent supported by V1/V2. Preserve visible window ACs only if the target reference date/user requests them. Resolve compass orientation and unseen elevations from a mapped walkaround before setting face schedules. The sources do not support populating the complete house by copying its photographed facade.

Against the prior asset evidence, not treating the model as real-building truth:

* The fresh 2026 GIS query’s 10-corner ring matches the prior query exactly; the previous asset validation’s 0.0000139 m vertex difference is a coordinate conversion residual, not external survey accuracy.
* The independent grade report’s light-toned two-storey gable, six sash openings in two rows, dark shutters, three vents, corner balcony and low basement openings are consistent with direct inspection of V1. More precisely, “six bays” means three per floor; the central bay holds paired units.
* V2 provides a later 2024-published porch/balcony corroboration, not a new complete elevation. It provides no cardinal registration.
* GIS `NUM_LEVELS=1`, `CountOfFLO=1` and `PU_Occupie=N` conflict with both current operator/Purdue listings and photo evidence. Do not use those fields for physical floor count or vacancy without discovering their data definitions.
* The source cannot establish that building itself dates to 1938; the 1938 date belongs to the cooperative. The current structure’s own date and architectural phase remain unknown.
* Audit roof pitch, eave/ridge height, wall/roof colors, basement datum and exact porch geometry remain estimates. This research pass adds no survey or roof elevation.
