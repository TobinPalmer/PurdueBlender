**Research status:** exterior brief prepared with significant gaps retained; provisional. **Scope:** research only. No Blender asset, builder script, placement, or master scene was changed.

## Identification and dated design record

Purdue's chapter page identifies Kappa Delta, Theta Nu, at **358 Northwestern Avenue, West Lafayette**. Tippecanoe County's address point FID 61768 repeats that address and identifies the property as the Theta Nu Kappa Delta sorority; its location is `[-86.9092884034, 40.4273138872]` WGS84. The county parcel is 79-07-19-287-001.000-026. It is a parcel boundary, not the building line.

The finished building is identified directly by the Wells & Wells project page, which names “Kappa Delta Purdue,” gives Cline Design as architect, calls the project a **four-story** new sorority, lists 28 bedrooms, 64 beds and 28,411 square feet, and states completion in **October 2022**. The page's set includes five exterior images (project image numbers 18–22). Images 18–21 have no stated exposure date; the website metadata records their upload as October 31, 2024, which is not a photo date. Image 22 itself is captioned **“358 Northwestern Ave. / West Lafayette, IN / December 10th, 2022 / West View.”** This dated aerial is the strongest bearing registration for the front/entry elevation and visible roof plan.

Two county records describe earlier or separate proposals and must not be combined with the completed-house evidence:

- The February 26, 2020 BZA-2031 minutes describe razing four student-housing structures and replacing them with a **three-story** sorority proposal. The applicant described 60 beds and asked for a 28-foot height variance; the recorded vote approved variances 1, 2, 3, 4 and 7. This records a design-stage proposal, not an as-built elevation.
- The July 2022 4-UP Planned Development packet is titled for the **O'Malley / 4-UP apartments** and its renderings show a separate six-story apartment project. Its site sheet labels the Kappa Delta house at 358 Northwestern “proposed construction by others.” The six-story mass is not the Kappa Delta house and is excluded from this dossier's building form.

The distinct 2020 concept and completed four-story project remain a real chronology/design change. No later Kappa Delta elevation drawing, permit set, or as-built plan was located to reconcile it. Do not use the 2020 28-foot variance as a measured height for the completed building.

It says there is to be no vehicular access from either Northwestern or Fowler and identifies the existing alley off Fowler as the sole access. This is useful site/access context, not a current surveyed site plan. The county parcel geometry, OSM building outline, and 2022 aerial show different kinds of boundaries and must remain separate in later work. The earlier audit also retained an Indiana 2018 LiDAR coverage query; it predates the October 2022 completion and contains no current roof/elevation measurement, so it cannot constrain this house’s vertical shell.

## Massing, roof, and material reading

The completed project's four-story designation is the builder's written classification. In the ground photos, three principal facade levels are legible, with dormer/roof-window forms in the roof zone; the images do not establish a floor-to-floor datum or exact count of occupied roof levels. The silhouette is a multi-wing composition rather than a simple rectangular block: a main taller hipped-roof volume meets lower gabled projections and a narrower central entry/connector. The December 2022 aerial shows two arched/eyebrow roof dormers flanking a front gable, several intersecting dark-gray shingle planes, and a broad light soffit/eave with a repeated dentil-like trim line. Ground-level obliques show dark gutters/downspouts, arched dormer glazing and additional gable returns. Roof covering is visually consistent with shingles but no specification confirms the material. Hidden hips, valleys, attic floor, roof pitch, ridge/eave heights, parapets, rooftop equipment and drainage routes remain unmeasured.

Photo 18–21 and March 2026 Street View show pale warm-gray/cream brick across the visible exterior, with pale stone or cast-stone-looking belt courses, window surrounds and entry/base walls. Brick joints and tonal shifts are visible, but no product, mortar, or stone specification was found. Windows are dark-framed and divided-light; larger two-story or tall ground-floor glazing has multiple vertical and horizontal divisions, while upper sash groups are narrower and aligned vertically. Select upper windows have black metal Juliet guards. The right-side front entry has a dark curved metal canopy on pale supports, while the left front door is sheltered by a white-column canopy/pergola. Dark traditional wall lanterns repeat at doors and along the facade. A low brick retaining wall shapes the elevated front walk and separate entry approaches; a concrete ramp and black metal handrails are visible from Street View and the aerial. Keep site walls, walkways, parking and landscaping distinct from the building shell.

The photographs are high-value appearance sources but are not calibrated. Their bright daylight and postproduction may affect brick/stone color; choose the facade/material distinctions from observed source regions, not model-audit renders. The 2020 county file is parcel/site evidence. The 4-UP renderings are for another project. Neither supplies Kappa Delta wall elevations or roof measurements.
