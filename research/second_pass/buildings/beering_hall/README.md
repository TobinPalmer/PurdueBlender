## Material and color comparison

Compare B01/B02 direct daylight with the flatter elevated B03/B05 and B08 close detail. Brick is varied red/brown/orange with fine pale mortar; pale stone/cladding/bands differ in smoothness; tall glazing and frames appear dark gray with sky reflections; roof tile is muted red/orange; the circular cap/posts are pale cream/white. Keep rough-faced band stone, smoother surrounds, cladding, brick, metal, glass, tile and roof membrane separate.

Blue reflections are not blue opaque glazing, and dark shaded sash/louvers are not one uniform facade paint. Upload/compression/lighting differences affect apparent colors. No calibrated color target, reflectance/roughness measurement or manufacturer finish sample was acquired. Near and elevated sources should guide geometric shadow depth rather than permanent painted shadow strips.
