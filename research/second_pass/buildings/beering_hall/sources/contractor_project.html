Beering Hall-Purdue

Beering Hall-Purdue

Location: Indiana
Service: Building Maintenance, Facade & Building Envelope Services
Expertise: Masonry

BCR was the general contractor for an extensive maintenance project of Purdue University’s Beering Hall in West Lafayette, IN. This project restored the facade of the 30 year old Liberal Arts College and College of Education. The scope included replacement of all exterior sealants and general masonry repairs. This seven-story building was a logistical challenge because it required extensive use of lifts, scaffolding, and swing stages. This project, completed on time, with great results, will allow the landmark college building to serve students for many generations! BCR is proud to have helped such a prestigious school to keep it’s campus beautiful and in great condition.
