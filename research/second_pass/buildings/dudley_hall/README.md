## Initial evidence and boundaries

The queue asset is Dudley Hall, the Purdue campus map feature 432 / DUDL. Existing local source records include an official Purdue GIS footprint snapshot, Purdue Engineering's June 18, 2019 Dudley naming announcement, and an Ennead Architects photo of the combined Dudley and Lambertus project. The combined-complex photo clearly labels Lambertus on the left and Dudley/Lambertus together at the central entrance; individual visible facade zones cannot be assigned to Dudley's independent footprint without more evidence.

The 2019 announcement describes Dudley as four stories with a basement and about 145,000 square feet. Ennead project material describes the 2022 Gateway Complex as Dudley and Lambertus together, with red brick, pale projecting wall/cladding, broad multi-story glazing and a terracotta-colored screen visible in the retained photograph. These are starting observations only; this pass must separate the two halls, establish current exterior faces and connections, inspect the actual source and locate facade/roof/dimension evidence.
