# Purdue Historic Facilities and Buildings database capture: Lilly Hall

- Retrieved: 2026-10-30 from <https://collections.lib.purdue.edu/campus/buildings/122>.
- Building record: Lilly Hall of Life Sciences (LILY), database record 122.
- Archive: Purdue University Archives and Special Collections, Purdue Campus Facilities and Buildings Historic Database.

## Transcribed record facts

The page dates the building record to 1959–present and reports 389,782 gross square feet, with a dedication on December 14, 1960. Its narrative says construction began with the west wing in 1951; Agronomy moved into it in 1955; construction on the main entrance began in 1957; Biology and Animal Sciences moved into the east wing in 1959; it was renamed Lilly Hall of Life Sciences in 1960; and a basement was constructed and the building remodeled in 1967. The record also lists the 1958 South Wing and Small Animal Building as completed that year.

Construction fields list 1951–1959, Scholer Corporation as architect, Kettelhut Construction, Tonn and Black, Hosler and Pearson, and Square Deal Electric as contractors, and “Limestone” under materials. This material field does not identify a facade or location. In the black-and-white exterior photographs, the long wall surfaces read visually as coursed masonry; the exact wall and trim materials remain to be confirmed.

The page has four undated exterior photographs: archive records PPBUC00712 (item 785), PPBUC00714 (787), PPBUC00718 (791), and PPBUC00719 (792). Full images and item metadata JSON are saved beside this capture. The photographs are visual evidence, not dated construction-stage proof.

The renovation selector lists both 1958 and 1967. The 1958 entry names Kettelhut Construction, Tibetts Plumbing and Heating, Robbins Electric, and Murphy Elevator, and states the South Wing and Small Animal Building were completed. The 1967 entry names Scholer Corporation, Mid-Republic Construction, Shambaugh and Sons, and Hatfield Electric; its note says the basement was constructed and the building remodeled.

The citations list Purdue Board of Trustees minutes dated 1948-10-20, 1950-08-03, 1953-01-21, 1953-09-16, 1957-04-17, 1958-04-15, 1960-12-14, 1967-06-02, and 1969-03-04, plus a College of Agriculture history PDF and the Purdue inventory. The linked PDF at <https://ag.purdue.edu/agry/Documents/Lilly_Hall_history.pdf> returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-30. The meeting-minute links are research leads only; their drawings/content were not inspected in this pass.

## Boundary cross-check

The related database record <https://collections.lib.purdue.edu/campus/buildings/66> describes the separately named Life Science Animal Building (LSA), formerly the Small Animal Building, as a 1956–1958 Scholer/Kettelhut building. It lists 49,800 gross square feet and brick as a material. A fresh Purdue GIS search on 2026-10-30 finds it separately as FID231 at 190 S Russell Street, four floors, adjacent to FID581. The two transformed GIS polygons overlap by approximately 0.43 m², consistent with boundary digitization tolerance; that overlap does not show a physical connection.

The sign “3D Animal Phenotyping Facility” is visible in the southward rotation of a single March 2026 street panorama. Given the separately mapped LSA and the direction of view, the sign may belong to LSA or another neighboring facility; it is not assigned to Lilly Hall from this image.
