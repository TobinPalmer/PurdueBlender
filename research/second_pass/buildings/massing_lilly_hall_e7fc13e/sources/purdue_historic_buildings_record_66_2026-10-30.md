# Purdue Historic Facilities and Buildings database capture: Life Science Animal Building

- Retrieved: 2026-10-30 from <https://collections.lib.purdue.edu/campus/buildings/66>.
- Record: Life Science Animal Building (LSA), record 66.

The database dates the building 1958–present, says it was originally called the Small Animal Building, and describes it as built with the Life Science Building (now Lilly Hall of Life Sciences), Life Science Ranges and Life Science Plant and Soils Laboratory. Construction fields say 1956–1958, name Scholer Corporation as architect and Kettelhut Construction, Inc. as contractor, list “Brick” under materials, and report 49,800 gross square feet.

The page supports a distinct companion-building identity. It does not itself establish exact wall-to-wall connections, current facade details or whether the “3D Animal Phenotyping Facility” sign visible in a Street View rotation belongs to this building.
