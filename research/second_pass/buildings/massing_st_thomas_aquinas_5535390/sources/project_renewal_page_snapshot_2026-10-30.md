# St. Thomas Aquinas Project Renewal page snapshot

Source: https://www.boilercatholics.org/project-renewal
Retrieved: 2026-10-30 via public page view (raw HTML request returned 403; this is a text transcription of the inspected page, not publisher HTML).

- Page identifies three connected buildings: the original church, new church addition, and office/classroom addition.
- Office/classroom window replacement began in 2019 and completed summer 2025.
- A center-church ramp and two new confessionals completed spring 2026; parish says wheelchair users can access the back of church.
- Bell tower interior LED lighting and cross lighting completed summer 2026.
- Church stained-glass/clear glass replacement is still being assessed; architect discussions began summer 2026, with no timeline/cost estimate. Mentioned groups: north/south altar-side windows, three large west-wall windows, and glass around the upper east section.
- A large masonry project covering the brick wall behind altar and other masonry is in design; studies were conducted 2019 and 2023, design funded in part through 2025 grant, preliminary documents being reviewed, bids hoped for before end of 2026. The page does not state that masonry repairs have been completed.
- Gather Space flashing/masonry repair completed spring 2025; parish says the roof itself remains in good condition.
- HVAC controls were upgraded fall 2025 and design began summer 2026; this is not exterior finish evidence.
